# Schedule Configurations app

Schedule Configurations is the register of the platform's recurring background jobs. Each row is one job — its **Name**, the **Technical Name** the platform runs it by, the **App List** it belongs to, and the cadence it runs on.

## What is this app for?

The register answers a maintenance question: what runs automatically, and how often. The shipped rows cover jobs such as the auto-sync behind [SendGrid Monitoring](/apps/transactional/sendgrid-monitoring), the auto-refresh behind [User Management](/apps/transactional/ias-user-management), the daily settlement run for payment instructions, and the job that expires abandoned deal drafts.

The platform reads **Interval (Minutes)** when it decides when a job runs next. Jobs that schedule themselves re-read that value on every cycle, so an interval you change here is picked up on the next run without restarting anything.

## When should I use this app?

Come here to change how often a scheduled job runs, to see which jobs exist and at what cadence, or to start looking into a job that no longer seems to run on schedule.

This app does not run jobs. Running a job on demand happens in the app that owns it — for example **Sync All** in the [SendGrid Monitoring app](/apps/transactional/sendgrid-monitoring).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, and the schedule rows carry no additional role restriction of their own.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter or browse to the job you want — the list shows **Name**, **App List**, **Technical Name**, **Interval (Minutes)**, **Interval Label** and **Is Active**.
2. Open the row. **General Information** shows the same facts plus the job's **Description**.
3. Change **Interval (Minutes)** to the new cadence and, where you want the readable text to match, update **Interval Label**.
4. Save. The platform uses the new interval the next time it plans that job's run.

That is the whole workflow: the interval and its label are the two values you maintain, and the run itself happens in the background.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Only two values are editable.** The job name, technical name, app, description and **Is Active** flag are shown for reference; they cannot be changed here.
- **Rows are not created or deleted here.** A new scheduled job arrives with the platform's configuration, not through this screen.
- **An interval has to be a positive number of minutes.** A job with no usable interval is not scheduled for a further run, so the cadence you type here is what the platform depends on.
- **Interval Label is descriptive text.** The scheduling uses **Interval (Minutes)**; the label is what readers see next to it.

## Where can I review the results?

Reopen the row and confirm **Interval (Minutes)** and **Interval Label** show what you saved. The real proof is in the job's own output: the [SendGrid Monitoring app](/apps/transactional/sendgrid-monitoring) records each run in **Data Sync History**, and [User Management](/apps/transactional/ias-user-management) shows when its data was last synced.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **A job has stopped running on schedule:** check its **Interval (Minutes)**. A row left without a positive interval is not scheduled again until the platform next starts — ask your platform administrator to correct the value and restart the service.
- **A field you expected to change is locked:** only the interval and its label are maintainable in this app by design.
- **An edit control is missing:** check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; checking a cadence and changing an interval are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
