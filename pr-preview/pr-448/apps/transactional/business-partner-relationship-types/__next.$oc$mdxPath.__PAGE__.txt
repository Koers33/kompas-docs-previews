# Business Partner Relationship Types app

Business Partner Relationship Types is the catalogue of the roles one business partner can hold in relation to another — **Contact**, **Trader**, **Client Admin**, **Partner Manager** and the rest. Each type has a code, a name, a description and an **Area** such as Trading, System Functions, Legal, Clients, Risk, Treasury Compliance, or Documents & Notes.

## What is this app for?

The catalogue is what makes a relationship between two business partners meaningful. When a relationship is recorded on a business partner, it picks one of these types, and the type's **Area** is carried onto that relationship. Some types also carry practical weight: **Client Admin** and **Partner Manager**, for example, are the types that decide whether a related user can maintain or read that business partner's record. Maintaining the catalogue here is therefore how a new relationship role becomes available, how a name or description is corrected, and how a type's area is changed.

## When should I use this app?

Use this app when a relationship role needs to be added, renamed, re-described, deactivated, or moved to a different **Area**. Record the relationships themselves on a partner record in the Business Partners app — this app maintains the types those relationships choose from; see [Business Partners for Treasury](/user-guides/treasury/business-partners-for-treasury).

## Who can access this app?

This app applies no role restriction of its own beyond portal access: whether it appears in your launchpad is a question for your platform administrator (see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access)). Changes here reach existing records once you sync them, so treat the catalogue as shared configuration: an area you change is the area other users will see on relationships of that type after the next sync.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Open a type and edit its details — the name, description, **Active** flag or **Area** — then save.
2. If you changed the **Area**, the type's **Synced** value turns off. That is the signal that existing relationships still carry the old area.
3. Run **Sync Area** on that type. The type's area is written to every existing relationship that uses it, and **Synced** turns back on.

If you skip step 3, the type shows as not synced and relationships keep the area they had; nothing else about them changes.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Area changed or not:** **Sync Area** only does work when the type is out of sync. On an already-synced type it changes nothing, so the value to watch is **Synced**.
- **Area on existing relationships:** the area shown on a recorded relationship follows its type. It is not maintained row by row on the partner — it arrives through **Sync Area**.
- **Access:** your ability to open and save a type still depends on your role and permissions.

## Where can I review the results?

Check the **Synced** column first: after a sync it should be back on for the type you changed. Then open a business partner and look at its **Children** and **Parents** sections: relationships of the type you changed should show the new **Area** alongside the type's name. If the type's area changed but relationships still show the old area, the type is still waiting for **Sync Area**.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

If relationships do not pick up your area change, re-open the type and check **Synced**; run **Sync Area** while it is off. If you cannot save the type at all, check your access first and use the message shown by the app.

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet. Editing a type and syncing its area are covered on this page.
