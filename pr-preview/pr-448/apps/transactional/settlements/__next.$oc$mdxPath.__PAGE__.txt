# Settlements app

Settlements is the platform's record of money that has actually moved. Each row is one settlement — a **Settlement Reference**, the **Settlement Status**, the deal and payment behind it, the amount and currency, and the **Bank Confirmation Reference** the bank returned.

## What is this app for?

The app tracks an obligation from "expected" to "done". It is the evidence that a deal's cash leg was carried out, and it links three things that are otherwise easy to lose track of: the deal, the payment instruction, and the cash the bank confirmed.

A settlement is not typed in. It is written by the platform when a bank confirmation is processed against a submitted payment instruction, and from that moment it is a read-only record.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to check whether a deal's obligation has settled, to find the bank confirmation reference for a payment, to trace which nostro account was used, or to investigate a settlement that has not gone through.

Day-to-day payment work — authorising a payment, processing a confirmation — happens in the [Payment Instructions app](/apps/transactional/payment-instructions). This app is where you verify the outcome. The end-to-end flow is described in the [Settlement and Cashflow Guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, and the settlement records themselves carry no additional role grant — but they are read-only for everyone: there is no create, edit or delete here.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter by **Settlement Reference**, **Settlement Status**, **Settlement Date**, **Settlement Currency** or **Deal**. The list opens newest settlement date first.
2. Open a settlement. **General Information** shows the reference, status, date, amount, currency, bank confirmation reference and **Settled At**; **Deal** shows the deal, payment instruction, actual cashflow and nostro account; **Actual Cashflow** shows what the bank confirmed; **Audit Trail** shows who touched the record and when.
3. Read the status. A settlement is recorded as **Settled** when the confirmed amount matches the deal's projected cashflow closely enough; when it does not, it stays **Unsettled** for someone to investigate.

The consequential work happens before and after this screen: the payment is authorised and confirmed in [Payment Instructions](/apps/transactional/payment-instructions), and the settled result is visible in the deal and in the cash balances.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Status drives the colour:** **Settled** is green, **Unsettled** is orange, **Partially Settled** is blue and **Failed** is red.
- **Status is set by the platform, not chosen.** It reflects what the bank confirmation matched, so there is no control to move a settlement to another status here.
- **Nothing is editable.** Every field is read-only and the record cannot be created, changed or deleted in this app — a correction belongs in the payment and confirmation flow that produced it.
- **Filters only offer what exists.** A deal that has produced no settlement will not return a row, no matter how the filters are set.

## Where can I review the results?

Start with the settlement itself: **Bank Confirmation Reference** and **Settled At** in **General Information** are the two facts that show money moved, and **Audit Trail** carries the record's integrity hashes alongside the who-and-when.

Then check the knock-on records. The [Payment Instructions app](/apps/transactional/payment-instructions) shows the payment's own status, and the [Cash Account Balances app](/apps/transactional/cash-account-balances) shows the confirmed settled balance for the nostro account. The deal itself is followed in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals).

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **You expect a settlement and see none:** settlements appear only once a bank confirmation has been processed against a submitted payment. Check the payment in [Payment Instructions](/apps/transactional/payment-instructions) first.
- **A settlement is stuck on Unsettled:** the confirmed amount did not match the deal's projected cashflow closely enough. Compare the amounts in the **Actual Cashflow** section and work the difference through the payment flow.
- **You cannot edit anything:** settlements are read-only by design, not a permissions problem.
- The registered messages for this flow are listed in the [block 9xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-9xx-csh).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet. The full walkthrough — payment, bank confirmation, settlement, matching and balances — is in the [Settlement and Cashflow Guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow); the checks above are summarised on this page.
