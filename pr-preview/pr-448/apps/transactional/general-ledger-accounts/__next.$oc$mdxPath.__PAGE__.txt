# General Ledger Accounts app

General Ledger Accounts is a company's chart of accounts on the platform. Each record is one account for one company, recognisable by its **GL Code** and **Account Name**, and it carries both the everyday accounting facts (**Account Type**, **Account Group**, **Normal Balance**, **Currency**) and the regulatory classifications that reporting and the accounting engine rely on.

## What is this app for?

The record is organised into three sections. **Account Definition** holds who the account belongs to and how it behaves — including the core banking mapping in **CBS GL Code**, whether it is a **Management Ledger Only** account, and whether it is **Active**. **Regulatory Classification** states how the account is treated for IAS 7, its **HQLA Level**, its **SARB BA700 Line**, FRTB book type, tax treatment and OCI recycling rule, and whether cross-valuation adjustment, expected credit loss provisioning or effective interest rate handling apply to it. **Approval** records **Approved By** and the **Approval Reference** the account relates to.

The accounting engine posts to these accounts, which is why they are set up before any deals are captured.

## When should I use this app?

Use it while a company's accounting set-up is being built out, when a new account is needed, when an existing account's classification has to be corrected, or when an account should be retired. It is also the place to confirm which accounts a company has and how each one is classified before you link them to anything else.

Day-to-day postings are not entered here — this app keeps the accounts those postings resolve to. The step-by-step build, in the order the platform expects it, is in the [company and accounting setup guide](/user-guides/admin/company-and-accounting-setup).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The app's own metadata records no additional role restriction and no narrower launchpad audience, so everyone who can open it sees the same accounts.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter the list by GL code, account type, cash classification, HQLA level, active state or management-only flag, then open the account you need.
2. Select **Create** to add an account in draft, or open an existing one to correct it.
3. Complete **Account Definition** first — **Company**, **GL Code**, **Account Name** and the classification basics — then set the values in **Regulatory Classification**.
4. Record the approval detail in **Approval** if your procedure requires it. **Approved By** and **Approval Reference** are chosen from existing records rather than typed, and no approve or reject control is offered on this page.
5. Save. Correcting an account later follows the same steps; the record keeps its creation and change stamps.
6. To take an account out of use, switch **Active** off rather than deleting it.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Active:** the list carries a green indicator for an active account and a red one when it is off. Inactive accounts stay in the register.
- **Management Ledger Only:** flagged separately in the list so management-ledger accounts are easy to spot among the rest.
- **HQLA Level:** shown with its own indicator — Level 1 green, Level 2A blue, Level 2B orange, and anything else neutral.
- **Fixed value lists:** **Account Type**, **Account Group**, **Normal Balance**, **Cash Classification**, **HQLA Level**, book type, tax treatment and OCI recycling rule are chosen from the platform's lists, so only the values your configuration offers are selectable. **Company** is likewise limited to configured companies.
- **Approval detail:** **Approved By** and **Approval Reference** point at existing approval and business partner records; if the right entry is not there, it has to be created before it can be selected.
- **Deleting:** accounts cannot be deleted from this app. Switch **Active** off instead.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows **Company**, **GL Code**, **Account Name**, **Account Type**, **Normal Balance**, **IAS 7 Class**, **HQLA Level**, **BA700 Line**, **MGMT Only** and **Active** for every account, so you can review a whole chart of accounts at a glance. On the record, **IAS 7 Class** and **HQLA Level** also appear as header indicators, and the three sections hold the full detail.

Downstream, the records that resolve an account show which one they used: financial node account mappings, client ledger overlays, treasury accounts and nostro accounts all point at a general ledger account. Do not assume every account is referenced — that depends on how far each company's set-up has progressed.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- A delete command is refused: accounts are retained by design. Switch **Active** off to retire one.
- A value help offers no choice: only configured companies and platform list values are available, so the entry you expect may not exist yet.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating an account, classifying it, retiring it and checking the outcome are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
