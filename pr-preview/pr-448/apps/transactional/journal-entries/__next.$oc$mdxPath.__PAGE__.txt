# Journal Entries app

Journal Entries is the read-only register of the accounting the platform produced for deals. Each entry records one deal and one occasion — **Deal Confirmed**, **Periodic Revaluation**, **Maturity Settlement**, **Deal Rolled**, **Deal Cancelled** or **Deal Drawdown** — the ledger it posted to, when it was processed, who created it, and the postings behind it.

## What is this app for?

The app lets you see what was posted and why, without having to trust a summary. An entry carries its **Entry Hash** and **Previous Hash**, so it sits in a chain, and can point at the entry it reverses. Its **Postings** section breaks the entry down line by line: sequence, general ledger account, debit or credit, amount, currency and accounting event category.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to verify the accounting behind a deal, to trace an entry to its general ledger accounts, to follow a reversal back to the original, or during a review of what a period contains.

It is not where entries are created or corrected. Corrections are requested through the [Accounting Reversal Requests app](/apps/transactional/accounting-reversal-requests), and accounts are maintained in the [General Ledger Accounts app](/apps/transactional/general-ledger-accounts).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). Its service applies no role restriction of its own, so everyone who can open it sees the same rows, and no role can turn it into an editing screen: there is no create, edit or delete for anyone.

## How does work progress in this app?

There is nothing to progress. An entry appears once the platform has processed the occasion for a deal, you read it, and any follow-up — reversing a posting, correcting a mapping — happens in the app that owns that work.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Read-only everywhere:** an empty action toolbar is normal here, not a permissions problem.
- **Occasion and ledger decide what you are looking at:** the ledger ranges over **International Financial Reporting Standards**, **South African Generally Accepted Accounting Practice**, **Regulatory Reporting** and **Management Accounting**, so filtering by ledger narrows the register to one reporting view.
- **Reversals link back:** a reversing entry names the original through **Reversal Of**, which shows the original entry's hash.
- **An empty list:** entries are created by the platform, so no rows for a deal means no accounting has been processed for it yet — they cannot be added by hand here.

## Where can I review the results?

Open an entry and read **General Information** for the deal, occasion, ledger, processing time, creator and hash pair, then **Postings** for the debits and credits that make it up. The list carries the processing time, occasion, deal, ledger, entry hash, creation time and creator, so a single row is often enough to confirm an entry exists.

Balanced, chained entries are what the period close checks look for, and a posting you need to undo is raised as a request in the [Accounting Reversal Requests app](/apps/transactional/accounting-reversal-requests).

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **A deal you expected to see has no entry:** the posting may have been blocked — a closed or locked accounting period, an unbalanced entry or a missing account mapping all stop postings, and the message names the reason. The registered messages are listed in the [block 6xx](/reference/error-codes/block-6xx-acc) and [block 14xx](/reference/error-codes/block-14xx-acc) error references.
- **You want to change an entry:** entries cannot be edited here. Raise the correction through the [Accounting Reversal Requests app](/apps/transactional/accounting-reversal-requests).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; reading an entry, following its postings and tracing a reversal are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
