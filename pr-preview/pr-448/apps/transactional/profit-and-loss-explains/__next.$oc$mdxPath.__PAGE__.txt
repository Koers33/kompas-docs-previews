# Profit & Loss Explains app

Profit & Loss Explains stores a dated explanation of profit and loss for one deal. You choose the **Deal** and the **Explain Date**, and the platform fills in the rest: the effects behind the movement, the unexplained remainder, and whether the **Threshold Breached** flag applies. The list shows the **Deal**, **Explain Date**, **Total Profit & Loss** and **Threshold Breached** for every record.

## What is this app for?

One record answers one question: for this deal, on this date, where did the profit or loss come from? The object page splits that answer into **P&L Components** — the effects attributed to the new deal itself, market movement, currency translation, accruals, model change, parameter change, rounding residuals and EIR amortisation — and a **Summary** with the explain date, the total, the unexplained amount and the threshold flag.

Every component amount is read-only. The platform works them out when you create the record, so this app is for producing and reading an explain, not for typing figures in.

## When should I use this app?

Use it when someone asks why a deal's profit or loss looks the way it does on a particular date: create an explain for that deal and read the breakdown. The **Threshold Breached** flag marks the records where the unexplained portion was outside the platform's threshold when the record was created, so those are the ones worth reading first.

The deals themselves are maintained in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals), and the accounting those deals produce is read in the [Journal Entries app](/apps/transactional/journal-entries).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, so it is treated as available to anyone with portal access.

The service behind the screen is narrower: the **Treasury** role is granted reading and creating these records, and no other role is granted access to them.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Create a record and choose the **Deal** and the **Explain Date**.
2. As the record is created, the platform fills in the component effects for that deal, along with the total, the unexplained amount and the threshold flag.
3. Read **P&L Components** and **Summary**, then save.
4. The record is finished once saved: the deal choice and the component amounts are fixed in the app, and there is no delete command, so pick the deal and date deliberately.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **The deal decides the numbers.** Two records for the same deal with different explain dates are separate records with their own figures; nothing here rolls them together.
- **Components are read-only for everyone.** If a figure looks wrong, the record is evidence of what was derived at creation time rather than a value you can correct in place.
- **The threshold flag is set at creation.** It reflects the record as created and is not recalculated afterwards.
- **Deal selection is fixed once the record exists**, so a record against the wrong deal cannot be repointed.

## Where can I review the results?

**Summary** on the record carries the outcome — total, unexplained amount and the threshold flag — and the list gives the same information across deals and dates without opening anything. From there, the deal itself is in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals) and the posted results are in the [Journal Entries app](/apps/transactional/journal-entries).

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **The deal cannot be found:** the message names the deal you chose — see the [block 16xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-16xx-pnl) for the registered messages in this app. Pick a deal that exists and create the record again.
- **A component field will not accept input:** components are read-only by design; only the deal and explain date are yours to choose.
- **A permission message:** creating these records is granted to the **Treasury** role — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating an explain and reading its breakdown are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
