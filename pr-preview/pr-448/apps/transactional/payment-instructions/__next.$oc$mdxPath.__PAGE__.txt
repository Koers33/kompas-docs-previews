# Payment Instructions app

Payment Instructions is where a deal's settlement payment is driven from authorisation to bank confirmation. Each instruction names its **Payment Reference**, the **Deal** and **Company** it belongs to, the **Amount** and **Currency**, the **Settlement Date**, the **Channel** it travels on, the **Beneficiary Name** and account, and the three states that decide what happens next: **Payment Status**, **Screening Status** and **Blocking Reason**.

## What is this app for?

Instructions are created by the platform when a deal reaches settlement, so an instruction already carries the deal's amount, currency, beneficiary and settlement date by the time you see it. The app exists to review those instructions, authorise them, release the ones screening held, and record the bank's confirmation.

The statuses you will see describe where a payment is on that path: **Awaiting Authorisation** when it is first created, **Submitted** once authorised, and **Confirmed** once the bank acknowledgement has been processed. **Queued** means the payment is awaiting submission, **Held** means screening stopped it, **Failed** means it did not go through, and **Queued SAMOS** means it was authorised outside the SAMOS window and is waiting for the next one.

Instructions cannot be created or deleted here and every business field is read-only — the app is for driving and reviewing payments, not entering them. The **Documents** section is where supporting paperwork, including the bank confirmation, is attached.

## When should I use this app?

Use it during settlement: to clear payments waiting for treasury authorisation, to release a payment that screening held, or to record that the bank confirmed a payment. The full flow is described in the [settlement and cashflow guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow).

Deal capture, confirmation and balances are not done here — those live in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals) and the [Cash Account Balances app](/apps/transactional/cash-account-balances).

## Who can access this app?

Payment instruction records carry an additional grant to the Treasury role, so a user without that grant cannot work with them even when portal access succeeds. The app itself is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## How does work progress in this app?

1. The platform creates the instruction when a deal settles. It starts **Awaiting Authorisation** with **Pending Screening**, and the screening result is recorded on the record.
2. Review the instruction and select **Authorise Payment**. It moves to **Submitted**, and **Authorised By** and **Authorised At** are filled in. A **SAMOS** payment authorised outside the operating window instead becomes **Queued SAMOS**, and the message tells you the time of the next window.
3. When screening reports a potential match the payment is **Held**, with a **Blocking Reason** such as **Sanctions HIT — AML Hold** or **SRR Compliance Block**. Once the case is resolved, **Recycle Held Payment** returns it to **Awaiting Authorisation** with screening pending again and records the recycling decision.
4. When the bank confirms, attach the confirmation under **Documents** and select **Process Bank Confirmation**. The instruction becomes **Confirmed**, and in the same step the platform records the settlement, the actual cashflow and the match against the deal's projected cashflow.

Treat this as orientation; follow your bank and settlement procedures before authorising.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Each action fits one state:** **Authorise Payment** is offered only while the payment is **Awaiting Authorisation** and screening has not reported a **Hit**; **Recycle Held Payment** only while **Held**; **Process Bank Confirmation** only while **Submitted**.
- **Bank confirmation has conditions:** at least one document on the record and a projected cashflow for the same deal and currency. The message names the condition that failed, and registered messages are listed in the [9xx payment and cash error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-9xx-csh).
- **Channel decides the route:** **SAMOS** covers ZAR domestic payments and is subject to its operating window, **SWIFT GPI** covers foreign-currency international payments, **EFT** is the batch channel, and **Internal** is a transfer between a company's own nostro accounts.
- **Colour is the quick read:** green for **Confirmed** and **Screening Cleared**, blue for **Submitted** and **Queued**, orange for **Awaiting Authorisation**, **Queued SAMOS** and **Pending Screening**, red for **Held**, **Failed** and **Hit**.
- **Read-only for everyone:** apart from the three actions and the **Documents** section, nothing on the record is editable, whatever your role.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows **Payment Status**, **Screening Status** and **Blocking Reason** with their indicators, so one scan shows what needs attention; filter it by company, deal, channel, currency or settlement date. Open a record for **General Information** (the deal and the amounts), **Settlement Details** (channel, beneficiary, status and the submission, authorisation and confirmation times), **Documents**, and **Audit Trail**.

The outcome is verified outside this app: the [settlement and cashflow guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow) shows where the settlement and the match against the projected cashflow are recorded, and the [Cash Account Balances app](/apps/transactional/cash-account-balances) shows the balance afterwards. Those records appear once a bank confirmation has been processed — not every instruction has them yet.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- **Authorise Payment is not offered:** the payment is not **Awaiting Authorisation**, or screening has reported a **Hit**. Clear the hold first with **Recycle Held Payment**.
- **Process Bank Confirmation is refused:** the payment must be **Submitted**, must have at least one document, and must find a matching projected cashflow — the message says which condition failed. See the [9xx reference](/reference/error-codes/block-9xx-csh).
- **You are told the payment is queued:** the SAMOS window is closed; note the next window time from the message and check the status afterwards.
- **An edit control is missing:** the payment fields are read-only by design. Check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; authorising, releasing and confirming a payment are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
