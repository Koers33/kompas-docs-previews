# App Lists app

App Lists is the platform's application register. Every app the platform ships has one row with its identity — **Code**, **Name**, **Description** and **Data Classification** — plus a curated **Context Card** that explains what the app is for and how it is governed.

## What is this app for?

The register answers practical questions about an app without opening it: what it does, how its data is classified (for example master data, transactional, configuration, reference, audit, mixed, read-only derived, or global master data), which roles it primarily serves, where it sits in the workflow, which entities it governs, which other apps relate to it, and which rollout phase it belongs to. Other platform records point at apps through this register — attachments, scheduled runs and button definitions all carry an app reference — so keeping the register accurate keeps those references meaningful.

## When should I use this app?

Use App Lists when you need to check or correct the descriptive metadata for an app: a purpose statement that has gone stale, a data classification that is wrong, or a related-app link that no longer resolves. Use the apps themselves for day-to-day business work — this register describes them rather than doing what they do.

## Who can access this app?

The register applies no role restriction of its own beyond portal access: whether the app appears in your launchpad is a question for your platform administrator (see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access)). Note that the register is not a place to add or remove apps: rows cannot be created or deleted here, and the descriptive content you can maintain is the purpose and **Context Card** of an existing row.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Open a row. **General Information** holds the app's identity — its code, name, active flag and the technical, service and launchpad details the platform uses.
2. Edit the **Context Card** content and save. Two rules are checked on save: the purpose must not begin with "This app" (start with what the app does), and every code listed under related apps must be an app that exists in this register. A purpose you save becomes the app's English description, so the text you see afterwards is what readers get.
3. If you want to retire a curated card without removing the app, select **Clear Card**. It empties the purpose and every card field and leaves the register row — including its code, name and service identity — in place.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **The card already has content:** **Clear Card** is only offered when a purpose or card field is filled, so it disappears once the card is empty.
- **Related apps:** the picker only offers apps from this register, and a hand-typed code that does not exist is refused with a message naming the unknown code.
- **The row itself:** new apps are not created here and rows are not deleted here; your edits update what is already registered.

## Where can I review the results?

After saving, reopen the row and check the **Context Card** section: the purpose and the fields you filled should be shown, and the list should show your **Data Classification** and **Is Active** value. After **Clear Card**, the same section should be empty while **Code** and **Name** remain unchanged. When a related app is referenced, check that its row still exists in the register.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

If a save is refused, read the message: a purpose beginning with "This app" and an unknown related-app code are both explained in the [app and configuration error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-33xx-apc). Rewrite the purpose or pick a registered app and save again. If you cannot edit at all, check your access first.

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet. Editing a purpose, correcting a related-app link and clearing a card are covered on this page.
