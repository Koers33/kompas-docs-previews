# Evidence app

Evidence is the audit trail of sealed evidence packs. When the platform confirms a deal — or a linked FEC transaction, term loan or treasury deposit — it seals a pack: the pack's hash is chained to the pack before it, and the record is stamped with who sealed it and when.

## What is this app for?

Each pack carries a **Pack Code**, a **Seal Status**, a **Defensibility Level**, **Sealed At**, the person who sealed it, and the **Previous Hash** and **Entry Hash** that prove the record has not been altered, plus an **Items** list of what the pack holds.

The **Seal Status** values read as follows: **Pending Seal** (the pack is being assembled and is not yet sealed), **Sealed** (verified, hashed and sealed) and **Invalidated** (the pack failed hash verification). The **Defensibility Level** reads as **Legally Defensible** (sealed with a hash chain, trusted timestamp and intact custody chain), **Review Required** (sealed but missing one or more defensibility criteria) or **Non Final** (pending seal or draft state).

## When should I use this app?

Use it to confirm that a sealed pack exists for a piece of work, to read its defensibility level, or to show the hash values that demonstrate the record has not changed. The deals and confirmations themselves are worked in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals).

## Who can access this app?

Reading a pack requires portal access, and changing one is granted at the service level to the **Treasury Dealer** and **Compliance Officer** roles. These are service permissions; what you can actually do still depends on the roles assigned to you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). In normal use nobody edits packs by hand: the platform does the sealing.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. The platform seals a pack when the underlying confirmation happens. It sets **Seal Status** to **Sealed**, stamps **Sealed At** and the sealer, links the pack to the one before it through the hashes, and records a defensibility level.
2. The only status changes accepted are **Pending Seal** → **Sealed** and **Sealed** → **Invalidated**. Any other change is refused with a message.
3. A scheduled integrity check re-verifies sealed packs. If a hash no longer matches, the pack is moved to **Invalidated** and a security incident is raised for someone to resolve.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Filters:** **Pack Code**, **Seal Status**, **Defensibility Level** and **Sealed At** decide which rows appear.
- **Status and level:** a **Non Final** pack is not yet sealed, while **Review Required** means the pack is sealed but does not meet every defensibility criterion.
- **Read-only detail:** **Entry Hash**, **Previous Hash**, **Sealed At** and the sealer are not editable, and packs cannot be deleted.
- **Your role:** changing a pack remains limited to the **Treasury Dealer** and **Compliance Officer** roles.

## Where can I review the results?

Open a pack and read the **Details** section for its status, defensibility level, sealing time and sealer, and its previous and entry hashes. The **Items** section below it lists what the pack contains, with each item's type, reference and capture date. Reading the **Entry Hash** against the **Previous Hash** is the quickest way to show that the chain is unbroken.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- A status change is refused: only the two transitions listed above are allowed. The registered messages for this app are listed in the [block 6xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-6xx-acc).
- A pack shows **Invalidated**: the integrity check found a hash mismatch. That is a matter for your compliance process, not a value you can correct here.
- No pack exists for work you expected: packs are created on confirmation, so work that has not reached confirmation has none.

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; finding a pack, reading its seal details and interpreting a status are covered on this page.
