# Client GL Overlays app

Client GL Overlays lets you give one client its own general ledger code on one of the company's financial nodes. An overlay is the client-specific exception to the company's normal accounting mapping: when a posting is made for that client on that node, the client's code is used instead of the company's default.

## What is this app for?

Each overlay records a company, a **Client**, a **Company Financial Node** and a **Client GL Code**, with an optional **Client GL Name**, a linked general ledger account and an effective period (**Effective From** / **Effective To**). It exists so that postings made on behalf of a client are coded to that client's account rather than to the company's own account on the same node.

An overlay only takes effect when it is approved, still **Is Active**, and the posting date falls inside its effective period. When those conditions hold, the platform uses the **Client GL Code** for that posting; otherwise it falls back to the company's own treasury account for the same financial node. If neither exists, the platform records a missing general ledger mapping for someone to fix.

## When should I use this app?

Use it when a client's postings must land on a client-specific account on a particular financial node — for example when onboarding a client for on-behalf trading, or when a client's coding needs to change. Company-wide account mapping is not done here: that belongs to the **Financial Node Account Mappings** and **Treasury Account Master** records, and the client's own details belong in **Business Partners**.

## Who can access this app?

Reading an overlay requires portal access. Creating, updating and running **Submit For Approval**, **Approve** and **Reject** are granted to the **Treasury** role at the service level — these are service permissions, and what you can actually do still depends on the roles assigned to you (see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access)). A user without that role is refused when submitting, with a message telling them to contact their administrator.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Create the overlay in draft. **Client**, **Company Financial Node** and **Client GL Code** are mandatory; choose the company, enter the codes and set the effective period, then save.
2. Select **Submit For Approval**. The action is offered once the record is saved, and it creates the approval request for the overlay.
3. A Treasury user opens the saved overlay and selects **Approve** or **Reject**.
4. **Approve** records who approved it and when, then switches the overlay on. **Reject** switches it off. An overlay that has not been approved is not used for postings, whatever its **Is Active** flag says.

Treat this as orientation and follow your approval procedure before approving an overlay.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Is Active:** the list can be filtered on it, and it is the visible outcome of the decision — on after approval, off after rejection.
- **Client category:** the client must be an organization business partner; anything else is refused with a message.
- **One per node:** there can only be one active overlay for the same company, client and financial node. A second attempt is rejected as a conflict — open the existing record instead.
- **Effective period:** **Effective From** must be on or before **Effective To**. Outside the period, the overlay is simply not applied to postings.
- **Deleting:** records cannot be deleted from this app. Correct an overlay or switch it off instead.
- **Access:** writing and all three approval actions remain limited to the **Treasury** role.

## Where can I review the results?

The overlay record itself is where you verify the outcome: check **Is Active** in the list or on the record, together with **Client GL Code** and the effective period. Where an overlay applies, the client's postings use that code — confirm the account on the accounting records for those postings. An overlay that is off, or outside its effective period, means the company's default account for the financial node was used instead.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- **Submit For Approval** is refused: you need the **Treasury** role. See [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
- You are told an overlay already exists for that client and node: open the existing record rather than creating another.
- The client will not be accepted: only organization business partners can be used as the client.
- The registered messages for this app are listed in the [block 13xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-13xx-bpv).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating an overlay, submitting it, deciding on it and checking the outcome are covered on this page.
