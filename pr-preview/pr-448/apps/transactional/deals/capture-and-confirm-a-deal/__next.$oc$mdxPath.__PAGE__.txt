# Capture and confirm a deal

Use the Deals app to record a treasury transaction and move it through capture and independent confirmation. During capture, enter the company, counterparty, instrument, dates, and applicable terms; saving places the deal in **Captured** status. A different eligible Treasury user—not the person who captured it—then confirms the deal. After confirmation, verify its status and review any relevant cashflow or accounting outputs; settlement is a separate back-office process.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users with access to the Deals app can create and update deal records. A user cannot confirm a deal they captured; confirmation must be performed by a different eligible user.

## What do I need before I start?

- Access to the Deals app and the company for which you are entering the deal.
- The counterparty, instrument, dates, currency, and terms needed for the selected deal type.
- A second eligible Treasury user who can confirm the deal after you capture it.

Deal details and sections vary by instrument and acting capacity. Complete the fields the app marks as required for the selected deal.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the Deals app and select **Create**.
2. Enter the deal details, including the company, counterparty, instrument, dates, and applicable deal terms.
3. Review the deal details and select **Save** to activate the draft. Confirm that the deal reaches **Captured** status.
4. Ask a different eligible Treasury user to open the captured deal.
5. The second user selects **Confirm Deal**.
6. Reopen or refresh the deal and verify that its status is **Confirmed**.

The **Confirm Deal** action is available for captured deals, subject to the user's permissions and the separation-of-duties rule.

## How do I know it worked?

The deal shows **Confirmed** status. Review its related sections, such as cashflows or journal entries, when those outputs are relevant to the deal.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Confirm Deal** is unavailable, check that the deal is captured and that the current user did not capture it.
- If the action remains unavailable, check the user's access and resolve any validation messages shown by the app.
- If the deal cannot be saved or confirmed after those checks, contact your Kompas administrator or deal-process owner.
