# Forex Symbols app

Forex Symbols is the register of currency pairs. Each record sets a **Base Currency** and a **Quote Currency**, and the **Currency Pair** shown on the record is filled in from those two currencies for you.

## What is this app for?

A symbol is the reusable definition of a pair: state the two currencies once and every other app can refer to the same record. Markets link to symbols in the [Forex Markets app](/apps/transactional/forex-markets), and price observations are recorded against those links in the [Forex Price Data app](/apps/transactional/forex-price-data).

## When should I use this app?

Come here when a pair you need does not exist yet, or when a pair's details need correcting. Defining what a market trades is done in [Forex Markets](/apps/transactional/forex-markets) and quoting prices in [Forex Price Data](/apps/transactional/forex-price-data) — this app is where the pair itself lives.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The symbol records carry an additional service-level grant to the **Treasury** role, so working with them needs that grant as well; the app's own metadata records no narrower launchpad audience.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Select **Create** and choose the **Base Currency** and the **Quote Currency** — both are required.
2. The **Currency Pair** field fills in automatically from the two currencies; it is read-only, so there is nothing to type there.
3. Save. A combination that already exists is refused, so each pair is stored once.
4. A saved symbol is retained — it cannot be deleted. To stop a market trading it, switch off the market's link in [Forex Markets](/apps/transactional/forex-markets) instead.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Derived pair text:** **Currency Pair** is calculated from your two currency choices, so it only appears once both are selected.
- **One record per combination:** the save checks whether a symbol for those currencies already exists and refuses the duplicate with a message, so a pair is not registered twice.
- **Read-only downstream:** once a symbol exists, its currencies are shown read-only where the symbol is linked, so a correction has to happen here.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows each symbol's pair, base and quote currency, and you can filter it by **Currency Pair**, **Base Currency** or **Quote Currency** to confirm a record exists. Open a record and use **General** for the full definition. The places that consume it are worth checking too: the **Traded Symbols** table in the [Forex Markets app](/apps/transactional/forex-markets) shows which markets link the symbol.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- A message about a symbol for those currencies already existing: the pair is already registered — open the existing record instead of adding another. The registered messages are listed in the [block 11xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-11xx-forex).
- The **Currency Pair** stays empty: both **Base Currency** and **Quote Currency** have to be chosen first.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating a pair, reading the derived pair text and handling a duplicate are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.

## What should I do next?
