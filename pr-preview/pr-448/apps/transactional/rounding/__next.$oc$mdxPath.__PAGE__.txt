# Rounding app

Rounding is where the platform's global rounding policies are maintained. Each policy combines a **Policy Set** — the calculation it governs, such as monetary posting, FX rate display or effective interest rate calculation — with a **Scope** that says how broadly it applies, and fixes **Decimal Places**, the **Rounding Mode**, an optional **Increment Value**, an **Effective From** date and whether it is **Active**.

## What is this app for?

The list is the register of those policies: policy set, scope, decimal places, rounding mode and effective date per row, filterable by policy set, scope, instrument type and active state. Opening a policy gives you the same values plus the currency, increment and end date that complete it.

The policies are the platform's rules for a calculation; the app also lets you hand those rules to a specific company. The derivation action on a policy takes every active global policy and writes a company-level copy for the company you choose, reporting how many rows it created and how many it updated in the process.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to add or correct a rounding rule, to put a rule into or out of use with its effective dates and active flag, and to give a company the current global rules after you have changed them.

Individual rounding decisions the platform takes while working are not shown here; this app is for the rules, not for the figures they produce.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, so it is treated as available to anyone with portal access.

Reading the policies goes with portal access; the rule on this service grants changing them to the **Treasury** role, so users without that role can review the policies but not save changes.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Find the policy you want by policy set, scope, instrument type or active state, and open it.
2. Maintain decimal places, rounding mode, increment value and the effective dates, then save the draft.
3. When the global rules have changed, run the derivation action from the policy header, choose the company and confirm. It copies each active global policy to a company row, replacing the company's previous derived row, and returns the number created and updated.

The counts in the result are your confirmation that the derivation ran; the company rows it writes are company-level records and are not listed on this screen.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Only active policies are handed to a company.** A policy you have switched off is left out of the next derivation.
- **Effective From and Active are required.** A policy cannot be saved without them, and the date range decides whether the rule is in force.
- **An increment must be zero or a positive number.** A negative value is refused with a message naming it — see the [3xx rounding error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-3xx-rnd).
- **Scope decides how widely the rule travels:** the global scopes describe a platform-wide default or an instrument-level override, and the derivation adapts them to the company and company-plus-instrument scopes when it writes the company copy.
- **Your role decides whether you can save.** A missing edit control points at [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Where can I review the results?

Read the policy row itself: the list shows the policy set, scope, decimal places, rounding mode and effective date, and the object page adds currency, increment, end date and active state. After a derivation, the result dialog's created and updated counts are the immediate check.

Where a policy is missing, the platform says so at the point of use — a message such as "No rounding policy configured for policy set … and company …" names the gap, and the [3xx rounding error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-3xx-rnd) lists every message in this area with its recovery text.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **The increment value is refused:** it must be zero or a positive number.
- **A required value is missing on save:** **Effective From** and **Active** have to be set before the policy will save.
- **Work elsewhere reports that no company policy exists:** the company has not been given the global rules yet — run the derivation for that company, or ask your administrator.
- **You can open the list but not save:** changing policies needs the **Treasury** role — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; maintaining a policy and deriving a company's policies are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.

## What should I do next?
