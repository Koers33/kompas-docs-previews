# Roles and Access

Kompas decides what each person can see and do by role. This page explains how access works, what the role categories are, why some actions need a colleague, and who to contact about access.

## How do I get into the platform?

Every user signs in through the company portal. Two things are needed: a portal-access entitlement that lets you open the Kompas launchpad at all, and one or more functional roles that decide which apps and actions you see. Without portal access you cannot reach any app; without a functional role you reach the launchpad but see no working screens.

## How do I know which role I have?

Your role is assigned to your account by an administrator, usually on request from you or your line manager. If you are unsure what you hold, ask the platform administrator — they can see your assignments and the company each assignment applies to. Roles are held per company, so the same person can have different roles for different companies.

## What role categories exist?

The platform defines thirty individual roles grouped into ten categories. The detail of every role lives in the authorization section of this site; the categories are:

| Category | In one line |
|---|---|
| Treasury | Deal-facing roles — dealers capture and manage trades, managers and the head of treasury approve and oversee them. |
| Back Office | Confirmation, settlement, and back-office management — verifying deal details and getting money where it needs to go. |
| Risk | Risk analysis and management up to the Chief Risk Officer — limits, exposures, and portfolio monitoring. |
| Compliance | Compliance and AML roles — screening, conduct oversight, and the only roles that can change sensitive counterparty statuses. |
| Finance | Accounting roles up to the CFO — journals, general ledger, and period control. |
| Operations | Operational oversight and data quality — keeping the platform's reference data trustworthy. |
| Administration | Platform configuration and user administration — the people who switch features on and assign access. |
| Client | Client-facing roles — viewing statements and acting as the treasury contact for a client relationship. |
| Audit | Internal and external auditor roles — broad read access for assurance work. |
| Governance | Policy and committee roles — setting and overseeing the rules the platform enforces. |

## Why can't I do an action I expect to be able to do?

Usually for one of two reasons: your role does not include that action, or the action is protected by a two-person rule.

Kompas enforces segregation of duties on sensitive steps — for example, the person who captures a deal may not be the person who confirms it, a rollover needs approval by someone else, and period-close and reversal steps require a second, different person. If an action is blocked with a permission or "different person required" message, it needs a colleague with the approving role, not an access change.

Some actions are additionally blocked by compliance policy: deals with a blacklisted or KYC-expired counterparty are prevented outright, and legal holds block transitions on affected deals.

## Who do I contact about access?

The platform administrator assigns and changes roles and portal access. Access requests are normal through your line manager; changes to what a role can do are a governance decision.
