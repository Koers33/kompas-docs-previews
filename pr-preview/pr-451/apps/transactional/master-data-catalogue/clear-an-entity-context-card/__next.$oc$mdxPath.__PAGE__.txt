# Clear an entity context card

Remove the saved governance context from an entity's card while keeping the entity and its platform registry details intact.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Clearing removes every context-card value, so confirm the reset with the data steward before proceeding.

## What do I need before I start?

- The catalogue entity whose card should be reset.
- Confirmation that its current context-card details are no longer needed or have been recorded elsewhere.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Master Data Catalogue**, find the entity by **Entity Name** and open its record.
2. Review the existing **Context Card** and confirm the reset is intended.
3. Select **Clear Card** and complete the action.
4. Reopen or refresh the record. Confirm the context-card fields are empty and the entity is still listed.

## How do I know it worked?

The card's purpose, entity type, steward, governance, related entities, seed/lifecycle fields, key fields, tenant flag, phase, specification reference and status glossary are cleared. The entity name and registry row remain unchanged.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Clear Card** is unavailable, confirm you are on an existing catalogue entity and check your access.
- If the registry row or entity name appears missing, do not try to recreate it here; the catalogue entries are platform-maintained.
- Clearing affects the card fields on the selected record; review the target entity carefully before running the action.
