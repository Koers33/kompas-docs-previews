# Fill in an entity context card

Record the purpose and governance context for an existing platform entity without changing the catalogue's registry data.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Registry entries are maintained by the platform; this task updates only their context cards.

## What do I need before I start?

- The entity's purpose and data steward role.
- Its governance rules, related entity names, seed-data status, lifecycle policy and key fields.
- Its tenant-specific status, creation phase, specification reference and status-field meanings, where applicable.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Master Data Catalogue**, filter by **Domain Group** and find the entity by **Entity Name**.
2. Open the entity and complete its **Context Card**: **Purpose Summary**, **Entity Type**, **Data Steward Role**, **Governance Rules**, **Related Entities**, **Seed Data Status**, **Lifecycle Policy**, **Key Fields**, **Is Tenant Specific**, **Phase Created**, **Spec Reference** and **Status Field Glossary**.
3. Choose entity type, steward, seed-data status and lifecycle policy from their fixed lists.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal save flow.
5. Reopen the entity and confirm the context card displays the saved information.

## How do I know it worked?

The entity retains its registry identity and shows the saved context card. Its **Entity Name**, **Master Data**, **Domain Group** and registry type remain as maintained by the platform.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If a list value is unavailable, it must be added to the relevant platform list by its owner; it cannot be created here.
- **Related Entities** must be comma-separated names of entities already present in the catalogue. Correct any name rejected on save.
- If the row itself is missing, contact the platform administrator; this app cannot create registry entries.

## What should I do next?

Review the saved card with its data steward.
