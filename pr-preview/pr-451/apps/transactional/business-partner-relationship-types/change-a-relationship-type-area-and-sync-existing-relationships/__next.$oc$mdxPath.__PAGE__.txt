# Change a relationship type’s area and sync existing relationships

Change the **Area** assigned to a relationship type and propagate it to active partner relationships that use that type.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and has no additional entity-level role restriction in its service. Your administrator controls whether the app is assigned to your launchpad and whether you have the required access.

## What do I need before I start?

- The relationship type to change and the new **Area** it should use.
- An understanding of where the type is used. Some types, such as **Client Admin** and **Partner Manager**, affect access to a business partner’s record.
- Time to review the affected relationships after the synchronization.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Business Partner Relationship Types** and select the relationship type to change. You can filter by **Synced** to find types that already have an unsynchronized area change.
2. Change the type’s **Area** and select **Save**.
3. Confirm **Synced** is off for that type. The area on existing relationships has not changed yet.
4. Select **Sync Area** for the type.
5. Refresh the type and confirm **Synced** is on.

## How do I know it worked?

The type shows the new **Area** and **Synced** is on. In Business Partners, review the **Children** or **Parents** relationships that use the type; active relationships now show the new area. Inactive historical relationships are not part of this app’s synchronization projection.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Sync Area** runs while **Synced** is already on, it makes no changes. Change and save the **Area** first, then run the action when **Synced** is off.
- If **Synced** remains off after the action, refresh the type and check its current **Area**. If it is still out of sync, contact your platform administrator or the owner of the relationship catalogue.
- If you cannot open or save the type, ask your administrator to check your portal access and app assignment.
