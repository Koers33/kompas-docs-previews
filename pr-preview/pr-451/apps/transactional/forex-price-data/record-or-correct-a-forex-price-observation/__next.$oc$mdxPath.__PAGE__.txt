# Record or correct a forex price observation

Record a bid/offer observation for a market symbol and value period, or correct a saved observation.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the **Treasury** role to maintain price observations.

## What do I need before I start?

- A market and a **Market Symbols** link that already exists.
- The observation’s date, time, **Value Period**, **Bid Value** and **Offer Value**.
- If correcting a row, the existing observation to update.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Forex Price Data** and select **Create**, or open the observation to correct it.
2. Choose **Market** and **Market Symbols**, then enter **Date**, **Time** and **Value Period**.
3. Enter both **Bid Value** and **Offer Value**.
4. Save the observation. The **Difference Amount** and **Difference Percentage** are calculated by the platform.

## How do I know it worked?

The observation appears for the selected market, symbol, date and period. When both bid and offer are present, the difference amount and percentage are populated.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the market or symbol is missing from the value help, create it in Forex Markets or Forex Symbols first.
- If the difference values are blank, verify that both **Bid Value** and **Offer Value** were saved.
- Price observations are retained rather than deleted; correct the saved row if its values were entered incorrectly.
