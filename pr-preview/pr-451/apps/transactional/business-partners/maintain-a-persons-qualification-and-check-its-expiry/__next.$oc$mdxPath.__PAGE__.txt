# Maintain a person’s qualification and check its expiry

Record or update a natural person’s qualification and use its calculated expiry status to see whether it is current, nearing expiry or expired.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users can maintain qualification records. Client users can read qualifications only where their own record or an authorized relationship grants access; they cannot add or update a qualification.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved **Natural Person** business partner. The **Qualifications** section appears on person records after the business partner has been saved.
- The qualification type, name, date obtained and issuing institution, plus an expiry date if the qualification expires.
- Supporting evidence for any verification or renewal you record.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Business Partners** and open the saved person’s record.
2. Go to **Qualifications**. Select **Create** to add a qualification, or open the existing qualification you need to update.
3. Select the **Qualification Type** and enter the **Qualification Name**, **Issuing Institution** and **Date Obtained**. Enter **Expiry Date** when the qualification has one.
4. If you are recording verification, enter **Verified At**; when saved, the app fills **Verified By** from your account if it is not already set.
5. Save the qualification, then refresh or reopen the record to review **Expiry Status**.

## How do I know it worked?

The qualification appears in the person’s **Qualifications** table. **Expiry Status** is calculated from the expiry date: **Expired** when the date has passed, **Expiring Soon** when it is within 30 days, **Valid** when it is more than 30 days away, or **No Expiry** when no expiry date is set. You do not enter this status yourself.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Qualifications** is not shown, confirm that the record is a saved **Natural Person**; the section is not available on every partner category.
- If you can view qualifications but cannot edit them, you are likely signed in with the **Client** role. Ask a Treasury user to maintain the record.
- If **Expiry Status** does not match the evidence, check the saved **Expiry Date** and refresh the record; the status is calculated when the qualification is read.

## What should I do next?

Keep the qualification details and expiry date aligned with the current supporting evidence.
