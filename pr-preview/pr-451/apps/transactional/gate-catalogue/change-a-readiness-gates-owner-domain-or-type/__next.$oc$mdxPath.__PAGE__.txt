# Change a readiness gate owner, domain or type

Update a gate's governance mapping and record why the accountable owner, business domain or gate type changed.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow the platform's approval process for changes to shared gate governance; no separate role restriction is declared in the app service.

## What do I need before I start?

- The gate to update and the approved new **Readiness Gate Type**, **Business Domain** or **Accountable Owner Role**.
- A change reason of at least 10 characters.
- If assigning a new owner, confirmation that the role is active.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Gate Catalogue**, find the gate and review its current ownership, domain and type.
2. Select **Edit** and update the relevant **Readiness Gate Type**, **Business Domain** and/or **Accountable Owner Role**.
3. Enter the **Change Reason** beside the mapping fields. The reason must have at least 10 characters.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal draft-save flow.
5. Reopen **Mapping History** and confirm each changed mapping has an entry with its previous value, new value and reason.

## How do I know it worked?

The gate shows the saved mapping values and the related **Mapping History** entries. Confirming a new business domain or owner also clears its proposed indicator.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the save reports that a reason is required or too short, enter a reason of at least 10 characters and retry.
- If the new owner role is inactive, select an active role.
- If the field is rejected as fixed, only **Readiness Gate Type**, **Business Domain**, **Accountable Owner Role** and **Is Active** are editable mapping values. Gate identity, series, sequence, rule and behavior fields cannot be changed here.
- If history cannot be written, the gate change is rolled back; check the record before retrying.

## What should I do next?
