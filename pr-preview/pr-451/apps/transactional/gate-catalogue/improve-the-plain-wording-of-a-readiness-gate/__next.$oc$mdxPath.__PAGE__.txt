# Improve the plain wording of a readiness gate

Make a readiness gate's explanation clearer to users, update check wording if needed, and record whether the explanation has been reviewed.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow your team's review process before marking wording confirmed.

## What do I need before I start?

- The gate and the check whose wording needs improvement.
- Clear, user-facing text for what the gate checks, why it matters, what to do, and examples of ready and not-ready outcomes.
- The applicable field limits: gate explanation fields range from 300 to 600 characters; check wording is limited to 300 characters.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Gate Catalogue**, select the gate and read its **In Plain Words** and **Checks** sections.
2. Select **Edit** and update the relevant **Plain What It Checks**, **Plain Why It Matters**, **Plain What To Do**, **Plain Example Ready** or **Plain Example Not Ready** fields.
3. To revise an individual check, update its **Plain Wording** in the **Checks** section. The check code and description are read-only.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal draft-save flow. Wording-only edits do not require a change reason.
5. After the wording has been reviewed, set **Wording Confirmed** accordingly and save. Reopen the gate to verify the text and confirmation state.

## How do I know it worked?

The updated explanation appears in **In Plain Words**, the revised check text appears in **Checks**, and **Wording Confirmed** reflects whether the wording review is complete. Wording edits do not create **Mapping History** entries.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the text exceeds its field limit, shorten it and save again.
- If the app refuses a change to check code, description, rule or another fixed field, only the plain-wording fields are editable here.
- If the gate update is refused because a change reason is required, check whether a mapping field also changed; wording-only updates need no reason, but mapping changes do.

## What should I do next?

Review the changed explanation with the gate's accountable owner.
