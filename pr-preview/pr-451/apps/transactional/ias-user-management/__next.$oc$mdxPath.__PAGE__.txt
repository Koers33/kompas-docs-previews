# User Management app

User Management shows the people who can sign in to the platform, kept in step with the platform's identity service. Each row is one person — **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Email** — with the two facts the workflow turns on: **Groups Count** and **Has Partner**.

## What is this app for?

The app answers three questions about a person: do they have any access groups, does the platform already know them as a business partner, and when did they last sign in. It is also where you act on those answers — refresh the list, claim a person into the platform, onboard someone as a client, or remove their group access.

The record page adds the detail: **User Identity**, **Groups**, **Contact Preferences**, **User Status**, **Security Information** and **System Information**.

## When should I use this app?

Use it when a new person has to be brought into the platform, when someone should be given client access, when you need to confirm why a person sees the tiles they do, or when access has to be removed.

User records themselves are not created or deleted here — they arrive from the identity service. This app maintains the platform-side links and group memberships.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The app's own metadata records no additional role restriction and no narrower launchpad audience. Because these controls change what another person can reach, treat **Revoke Access** as a consequential action and follow your own procedure before running it.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Open the list. Three views are offered: **All**, **Without Access** (people in no groups) and **Pending Onboarding** (people with neither groups nor a business partner).
2. Select **Refresh Data** whenever you need the latest state — it pulls the current users and their group memberships and updates the list. The list is also refreshed automatically after each change you make.
3. Select **Claim User** for a person who is not yet known to the platform. This creates their business partner record, including their email, if one does not already exist, and **Has Partner** turns green.
4. From the **Pending Onboarding** view, select **Onboard Client** to claim the person and add them to the client group in one step. **Groups Count** rises once the assignment is made.
5. To correct details or group membership, open the record, edit it and save — the change is written back to the identity service and the list refreshes behind it.
6. To remove someone's access, open their record and select **Revoke Access**. This clears every group membership the person has.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **The list is a cached copy.** **System Information** shows when it was last synced, and **Refresh Data** brings it back in line with the identity service if it looks stale.
- **Groups Count** is red at zero and green above zero; **Has Partner** is red when there is no linked business partner and green when there is; **Active** is green when the person is active and red when they are not.
- **Status** is green for an active person, orange for someone new who has not yet been activated, and neutral for anything else.
- **Revoke Access** is only offered on a saved record — not while a draft is open — and only removes group memberships. The person and their business partner record both remain.
- **Create and delete are not available.** Records come from the identity service, so there is no button to add or remove one.
- Access does not vanish mid-session: a person who is already signed in keeps their existing access until their session is refreshed.

## Where can I review the results?

**Groups** lists the memberships a person currently has, and **Groups Count** in the list gives the same answer at a glance — zero after a revocation, one or more after an onboarding. **Has Partner** confirms the link to the business partner, and **Security Information** and **System Information** show sign-in and sync detail.

For a claim, open the [Business Partners app](/apps/transactional/business-partners) and search the person's name: the record created by **Claim User** appears there with their email. For an onboarding, the surest check is what the person can see themselves — a client should now reach only the client-facing tiles.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **Refresh Data** does not update anything, or fails: the connection to the identity service is not available — ask your administrator to check the integration before retrying.
- **Claim User** reports that a business partner already exists: the person has been claimed before; check **Has Partner** rather than claiming again.
- **Onboard Client** is refused: the client group it assigns has to exist in the identity tenant first.
- **Revoke Access** seems to have done nothing: the count drops to zero in this app, but the person keeps their current session until it refreshes.
- An action is missing or you are told it is not allowed: your own roles do not include it — check [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet. The full walkthrough — refresh, claim, onboard, revoke and how to verify each one — is in the [user management testing guide](/user-guides/admin/testing-user-management), and the flows covered on this page are summarised above. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
