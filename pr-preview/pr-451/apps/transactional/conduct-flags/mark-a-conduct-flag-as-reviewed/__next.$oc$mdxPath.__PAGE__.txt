# Review an open conduct flag

Review a conduct concern raised against a deal or exit request and record that you have examined it.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to read flags and use **Mark Reviewed**. The flag is read-only; the platform creates it when deal or exit-request processing detects a concern.

## What do I need before I start?

- An **OPEN** conduct flag.
- The flag’s **Flag Reason**, description and linked deal or exit request.
- Your team’s conduct-review procedure for the type of concern shown.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Conduct Flags** and filter **Status** to **OPEN**.
2. Open a flag. Read the reason and description, then follow its link to the related deal or exit request for context.
3. Select **Mark Reviewed** after examining the concern.
4. Refresh the flag and confirm its status is **REVIEWED**.

## How do I know it worked?

The flag changes from **OPEN** to **REVIEWED**. **Reviewed By** and **Reviewed At** show who recorded the review and when.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Mark Reviewed** is not offered, confirm the flag is still **OPEN**; reviewed or escalated flags cannot be moved back by this action.
- If the linked record does not open, refresh the list and check your access to the underlying deal or exit request.
- If the action is refused, check your Treasury access and the current flag status; see the [deal error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-4xx-dls) for registered messages.

## What should I do next?
