# Add a company nostro account

Register a company's bank account so settlement processing can use it for new deals in the account's currency.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow your company's account-setup and approval procedure.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Company**, **Nostro Code**, **Nostro Name** and **Currency**.
- The matching **GL Account**, **Settlement System**, **Nostro Status** and **Effective From** date.
- The correspondent bank details, including **SWIFT BIC** and **Account Number**, where applicable.
- The **HQLA Level** and whether **MT940 API Available** applies.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Nostro Accounts** and select **Create**.
2. In **Nostro Details**, complete company, code, name, currency, **GL Account**, **Settlement System**, **Nostro Status** and **Effective From**.
3. In **Correspondent Details**, select the **Correspondent Bank** and enter the SWIFT BIC, account number and MT940 API setting as applicable.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal save flow.
5. Reopen the record and verify its company, currency, GL account and status.

## How do I know it worked?

The account appears in the list with its company, code, name, currency, GL account and status. An **Active** status makes it available to settlement processing for new deals.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If a required value is missing, complete the company, code, name, currency, GL account, settlement system and status.
- If the company, currency, bank or account is missing from a value help, configure it in its owning app first.
- If no active nostro exists for the deal currency, complete this setup before retrying deal confirmation.
- Nostro accounts cannot be deleted; change their status when they should no longer be used.

## What should I do next?

Review the account in **Nostro Accounts** and confirm it is **Active** for the required currency.
