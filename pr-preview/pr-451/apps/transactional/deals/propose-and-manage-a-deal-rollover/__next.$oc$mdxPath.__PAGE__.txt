# Propose and manage a deal rollover

Propose replacement deal terms for a confirmed deal, then have a different eligible Treasury user approve the rollover.

## Who can do this?

Eligible Treasury users with access to the confirmed deal can propose a rollover. A different eligible Treasury user must approve it; the proposer cannot approve their own proposal.

## What do I need before I start?

- A **Confirmed** deal that is not already locked by another pending rollover.
- The rollover type, closing deal leg(s), and proposed opening-leg terms, including positive notional and valid value/maturity dates.
- The approval procedure for the proposed replacement terms.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the confirmed deal and select **Propose Rollover**.
2. Select the applicable **Rollover Type** and closing leg(s). For a partial rollover, enter the **Closed Notional** to be rolled; it cannot exceed the deal’s notional.
3. Enter the opening-leg terms: notional, currency, value date, and any applicable maturity date or interest rate.
4. Submit the proposal. It enters **Pending** and locks the closing deal against edits to its contractual terms while the rollover is awaiting a decision.
5. A different eligible Treasury user reviews the pending proposal and selects **Approve Rollover**. If the proposal needs correction first, use **Amend Rollover Proposal**; if it should not proceed, use **Abandon Rollover** with a reason.
6. Reopen the deal and review the rollover status and linked deal lineage.

## How do I know it worked?

An approved rollover records the closing and opening legs and links the resulting deal lineage. The rollover request shows its decision; the related deal status and terms reflect whether the rollover was fully or partially approved.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Propose Rollover** is unavailable, confirm the deal is **Confirmed** and has no pending rollover.
- If a closing leg is unavailable, confirm it belongs to a confirmed deal and is not already in another rollover.
- Opening-leg notional must be positive, and its value date must be before its maturity date when a maturity date is supplied.
- If **Approve Rollover** is blocked because you proposed it, ask a different Treasury user to decide; the two-person rule cannot be bypassed.
- If the proposal is already pending, amend or abandon that proposal rather than creating another one.

## What should I do next?
