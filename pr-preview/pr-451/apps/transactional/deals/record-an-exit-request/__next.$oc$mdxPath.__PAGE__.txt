# Record an exit request

Record a client or counterparty request to exit a confirmed deal without treating the request itself as a completed settlement or deal closure.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users with access to the confirmed deal can record an exit request, subject to record-level permissions.

## What do I need before I start?

- A deal in **Confirmed** status.
- The request type and the time the request was received.
- If a fee is charged, the fee amount, currency, and whether it was disclosed when the deal was agreed.
- A delay justification if processing took more than eight hours.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the confirmed deal and select **Record Exit Request**.
2. Choose the **Request Type** and enter **Request Received At**.
3. Record whether a fee was charged. If **Fee Charged** is on, enter **Fee Amount**, **Fee Currency**, and **Fee Disclosed At Inception**.
4. Add **Delay Justification** if needed, and include **Notes** that explain the request.
5. Submit the action and open the deal’s **Exit Requests** section to review the saved request.

## How do I know it worked?

An exit request is attached to the deal with its request type, received/processed times and fee details. Recording it does not itself change the deal to settled or closed; those are separate lifecycle steps.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- The action is available only for a **Confirmed** deal.
- If the fee is marked as charged, provide both the fee amount and currency.
- Choose a valid request type: **Cancellation**, **Rollover**, or **Other**.
- If a fee was charged but not disclosed at inception, or processing exceeded eight hours without justification, the platform may raise a conduct flag for review.

## What should I do next?
