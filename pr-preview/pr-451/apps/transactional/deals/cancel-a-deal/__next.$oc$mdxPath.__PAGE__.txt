# Cancel a deal

Cancel a deal that should not continue and record why it is being stopped.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users with access to the deal can use **Cancel Deal**, subject to the deal’s record-level permissions and current status.

## What do I need before I start?

- The deal to cancel and a clear cancellation reason.
- Confirmation that the deal has no pending rollover. A deal locked by a pending rollover cannot be cancelled until that proposal is resolved.
- Confirmation that the deal is not already settled, cancelled, defaulted or terminated.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Deals** and select the deal you intend to stop.
2. Review its lifecycle status and related history to confirm cancellation is appropriate.
3. Select **Cancel Deal** and enter the required **Reason**.
4. Submit the action and refresh the record.

## How do I know it worked?

The deal’s lifecycle status changes to **Cancelled**, and the cancellation reason is recorded in its lifecycle history.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Cancel Deal** is unavailable, check that the deal is not in a terminal status.
- If a rollover is pending, resolve or abandon that rollover before attempting cancellation.
- If the app requires a reason, provide one before submitting. Registered deal messages are listed in the [deal error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-4xx-dls).

## What should I do next?
