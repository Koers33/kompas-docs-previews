# Register a currency pair

Add a reusable base/quote currency pair so markets can link to it and price observations can be recorded against it.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the **Treasury** role to maintain currency symbols.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Base Currency** and **Quote Currency** for the pair.
- Confirmation that the pair is not already registered.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Forex Symbols** and select **Create**.
2. Choose the **Base Currency** and **Quote Currency** from their value helps.
3. Confirm that the derived **Currency Pair** is the pair you intend to register; it is calculated by the app and is not entered manually.
4. Save the record.

## How do I know it worked?

The pair appears in the symbol list with its base and quote currencies. The calculated pair text is available for selection in Forex Markets and Forex Price Data.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the pair text is blank, select both currencies.
- If the save reports that the combination already exists, open the existing pair instead of creating a duplicate.
- Saved symbols cannot be deleted. To stop a market trading a pair, switch off its link in [Forex Markets](/apps/transactional/forex-markets).
