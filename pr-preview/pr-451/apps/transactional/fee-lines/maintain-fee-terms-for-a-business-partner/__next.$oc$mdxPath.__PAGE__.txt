# Maintain fee terms for a business partner

Create or update a partner’s fee line so the platform uses the intended classification, value and effective dates for future cost provisions.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and has no additional service-level role restriction. Follow your organization’s approval process for changes to fee policy.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Business Partner** and action/fee arrangement the line should cover.
- The **Fee Main Category**, **Fee Group** and **Fee Type** from the fixed lists.
- The agreed amounts, percentages and **From Date** / **To Date** window.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Fee Lines** and select **Create**, or open the existing partner fee line to update it.
2. Choose **Business Partner**, **Fee Main Category**, **Fee Group** and **Fee Type**.
3. Enter the applicable **Fixed Amount**, **Additional Percentage**, **Minimum Amount**, **Maximum Amount**, **Markup Amount** and **Markup Percentage**. Set the validity dates and add a **Comment** if needed.
4. Save the line. To stop using an existing fee line, switch **Is Active** off and save it; fee lines are not deleted.

## How do I know it worked?

The line shows the selected partner and fee classification, the saved terms, validity window and **Is Active** state. Additional and markup percentages must be between 0 and 100.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the line will not save, confirm **Business Partner**, **Fee Main Category**, **Fee Group** and **Fee Type** are set.
- If a percentage is rejected, enter a value from 0 to 100.
- If you want to remove a fee, turn **Is Active** off; deletion is not available in this app.
