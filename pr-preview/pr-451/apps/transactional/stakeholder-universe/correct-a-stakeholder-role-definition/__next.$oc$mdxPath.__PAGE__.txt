# Correct a stakeholder role definition

Update the description, screen scope or action permissions recorded for a role without changing its role code.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Because role definitions describe expected access, follow your organization's approval and access-governance process before saving changes.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Role Code** and **Role Name** of the role to update.
- The approved **Description**, **Screen Access** and **Action Permissions** values.
- The applicable **Auth Category** from the configured category list.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Stakeholder Universe** and filter by **Auth Category** or find the role by **Role Code**.
2. Open the role and review **General Information**.
3. Update its **Description**, **Screen Access** and/or **Action Permissions**. Keep **Role Code** unchanged.
4. Save and activate the draft.
5. Reopen the role and confirm its definition matches the approved scope.

## How do I know it worked?

The role row shows the intended name and category, and **General Information** shows the saved description, screen access and action permissions. The published [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access) reference reflects the role definitions maintained here.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the role code is wrong, it is the identity used to match and assign the role; it cannot be changed. Coordinate any new role code through the role-governance process.
- If an **Auth Category** is missing, it must be configured in its owning platform list.
- Role definitions do not assign roles to people. Use [User Management](/apps/transactional/ias-user-management) and the identity-administration process for assignments.

## What should I do next?

Review the role description in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access) and check actual assignments in [User Management](/apps/transactional/ias-user-management).
