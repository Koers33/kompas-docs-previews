# Import stakeholder definitions from a spreadsheet

Stage multiple role definitions from an Excel workbook and push the approved rows into Stakeholder Universe.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can import and apply stakeholder-role definitions. Follow your role-governance approval process before pushing changes.

## What do I need before I start?

- The approved role category, code, name, description, screen access, action permissions and active state.
- A non-empty **.xlsx**, **.xlsm** or **.xls** workbook using the Stakeholder Universe template.
- Confirmation that existing role codes are correct; **Role Code** is the natural key and cannot be changed by import.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Stakeholder Universe** and select **Import Spreadsheet**.
2. Select **Download Template** and fill the columns for **Auth Category**, **Role Code**, **Role Name**, **Description**, **Screen Access**, **Action Permissions** and **Is Active**.
3. Choose the completed workbook and select **Upload**.
4. Review the **Staged** and **Skipped** counts. Upload stages rows; it does not yet apply them to the role register.
5. After reviewing the workbook and summary, select **Push Now** or use **Update Entries** to apply the staged rows.
6. Review the created/updated counts and reopen the roles to verify their definitions.

## How do I know it worked?

The push reports created and updated row counts and any errors. Existing roles are matched by **Role Code**; new codes create role rows. Reopen the role list to verify the values that were applied.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the file is rejected, choose a non-empty Excel workbook with a supported extension.
- If rows are skipped, review the import counts and workbook values, including the role code and required name.
- If an existing role needs a new code, do not repurpose the import; coordinate the change through the role-governance process.
- If the master-data catalogue entry is missing, contact the platform administrator; the app cannot create its own registry entry.
