# Retire a general ledger account

Switch off an account that should no longer be selected for new work while retaining its record and accounting history.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow your accounting change-control procedure before retiring an account used by mappings or postings.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company and **GL Code** of the account to retire.
- Confirmation that the account is no longer required for new mappings and that its existing references have been reviewed.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **General Ledger Accounts** and find the account by company and **GL Code**.
2. Open it and review its account classification and the downstream mappings that refer to it.
3. Edit the account and switch **Active** off.
4. Save and activate the draft using the app's normal save flow.
5. Return to the list and confirm the account's **Active** indicator is off.

## How do I know it worked?

The account remains in the register with **Active** switched off. It is retained for records and history, but should not be treated as an active account for new configuration.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the account is still used by a financial node, treasury account, nostro account or client overlay, coordinate a replacement mapping before retirement.
- If delete is refused, that is expected: accounts are not deleted from this app. Switch **Active** off instead.
- If the edit control is unavailable, check your assigned roles and portal access.
