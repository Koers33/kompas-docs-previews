# Review a cost provision entry and its source rate

Find a recorded cost provision, see which action raised it, and compare its saved value with the action’s current rate.

## Who can do this?

Users who can open the Cost Provisions app can review its records. The app is read-only for everyone.

## What do I need before I start?

- The action, approximate time or user associated with the provision, if known.
- Access to **Cost Provision Rates** if you also need to inspect the action’s current settings.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Cost Provisions** and filter or sort the entries to find the relevant record.
2. Open the entry and read its **Cost Provision Classification**, **Cost Provision Value**, **Provision Date/Time**, **Username** and **Button Code**.
3. Use **Button Code** to identify the action, then open **Cost Provision Rates** and find the corresponding action row.
4. Compare the entry with that row, keeping in mind that the entry stores a snapshot of the rate at the time it was created.

## How do I know it worked?

You can identify which action raised the entry and which classification and value were recorded at that time. A difference from the current rate may simply mean the rate changed later.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If no entry appears, clear filters. This app cannot create entries; the platform records them when the relevant action runs.
- If the current rate differs from the entry, compare the entry’s timestamp with the current row; existing entries are not rewritten when a rate changes.
- If you need to change the rate for future entries, use [Cost Provision Rates](/apps/transactional/cost-provision-rates).
