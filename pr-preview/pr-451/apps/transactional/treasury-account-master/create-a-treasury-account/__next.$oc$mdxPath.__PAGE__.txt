# Create a treasury account

Register the company treasury account that connects a company financial node to the general ledger account it posts through.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to create or change accounts. Follow your accounting setup and approval procedure before submitting the new mapping.

## What do I need before I start?

- A **Company** and one of that company's **Company Financial Nodes**.
- The compatible **GL Account** and **Accounting Path**.
- The **Treasury Account Code** and **Treasury Account Name**.
- The applicable **Account Purpose**, **Leg Type** and **Effective From** date.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Treasury Account Master** and select **Create**.
2. Choose the **Company**, **Company Financial Node**, **GL Account** and **Accounting Path**.
3. Enter **Treasury Account Code** and **Treasury Account Name**. Add the **Account Purpose**, **Leg Type** and **Effective From** date where they apply.
4. Save and activate the draft.
5. Review the saved account's **General** section and confirm the node, company, GL account and path are correct.

## How do I know it worked?

The account appears in the list with its code, name, company, company node, GL account and active state. The selected node and GL account pass the service's same-company and category-compatibility checks.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the node does not belong to the selected company, choose a company financial node owned by that company.
- If the node category is incompatible with the GL account type, choose an approved compatible GL account.
- The **Treasury Account Code** is immutable after saving; check it carefully before creating the record.
- If a value help is missing the company, node, GL account or path, configure it in its owning app first.

## What should I do next?
