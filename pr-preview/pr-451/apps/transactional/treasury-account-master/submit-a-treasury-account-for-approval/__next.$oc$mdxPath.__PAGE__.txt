# Submit a treasury account for approval

Create a pending approval record for a saved treasury account so an authorised Treasury user can review the mapping.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to use **Submit For Approval**. Follow the company's approval procedure for treasury-account mappings.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved treasury account with its company, company node, GL account and accounting path checked.
- The account is ready for review and the approver has the context needed to assess the mapping.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Treasury Account Master** and select the saved account.
2. Review the **General** section and confirm the financial node and GL account pairing is correct.
3. Select **Submit For Approval**.
4. Refresh the record and confirm an **Approval** record is linked and pending.

## How do I know it worked?

The account's **Approval** section links to an approval record with a pending decision. The account remains in the register while it awaits review.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the account is not saved, save it first; the approval action is for an existing account.
- If submission is refused, confirm your Treasury role and review the same-company and GL-category checks on the account.
- If an approval record is already linked, review its state before submitting again.
