# Review an evidence pack

Confirm that a sealed evidence pack exists for a piece of work and inspect its integrity and defensibility details.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read evidence packs, subject to their record-level access. Treasury Dealer and Compliance Officer roles have the service permission to change pack records; routine review does not require editing a pack.

## What do I need before I start?

- The deal, FEC transaction, term loan or treasury deposit the evidence should relate to.
- A pack code, seal status or approximate sealing time if you are narrowing the list.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Evidence** and filter by **Pack Code**, **Seal Status**, **Defensibility Level** or **Sealed At**.
2. Open the pack for the relevant underlying work.
3. In **Details**, review the status, defensibility level, sealing time and sealer.
4. In **Items**, review the captured references and dates.
5. Compare **Previous Hash** and **Entry Hash** as part of the chain-integrity review.

## How do I know it worked?

You have confirmed whether the pack is **Sealed**, **Pending Seal** or **Invalidated**, and whether its defensibility is **Legally Defensible**, **Review Required** or **Non Final**. A sealed pack shows its seal time, sealer and hash-chain details.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If no pack exists, the underlying work may not have reached confirmation; packs are created by the platform during confirmation.
- If a pack is **Invalidated**, an integrity check found a hash mismatch. Follow the compliance/security process; do not edit hash fields to make the pack appear valid.
- If a status change is refused, only the documented transitions **Pending Seal → Sealed** and **Sealed → Invalidated** are accepted.
