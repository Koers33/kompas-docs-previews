# Check whether an email was delivered

Find a sent message and review its provider status, engagement and event timestamps.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read the monitoring views. The app is read-only; **Sync All** refreshes provider data but does not edit an email.

## What do I need before I start?

- A recipient or sender address, subject or approximate send date.
- The correct time period for the message.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **SendGrid Monitoring** and select **Sent Emails**.
2. Filter by recipient, sender, subject or status to find the message.
3. Open it and review **General Information** for addresses, subject, status and timestamps.
4. Review **Engagement** for open and click counts, and **References** for message/template identifiers.
5. If the row may be stale, select **Sync All**, then reopen or refresh the message.

## How do I know it worked?

The message shows the latest synced provider status and event time. **Delivered**, **Opened** or **Clicked** indicates delivery; a bounce, block or spam status explains why delivery failed.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If no message appears, check the search filters and **Synced At** before concluding that no email was sent.
- If the status is bounced, blocked or spam, inspect the related **Bounces**, **Blocks** or **Spam Complaints** view for the provider's record.
- If **Sync All** fails, review **Data Sync History** and ask your administrator to check the email integration.

## What should I do next?

If the provider reports a delivery failure, share the message reference and recipient with the notification owner.
