# Add a non-trading day to the trading calendar

Record a recurring holiday or a one-off market closure so business-day calculations can skip that date.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Confirm the holiday's market scope and date with the calendar owner before adding it.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Holiday Name** and whether it repeats on the same month and day every year.
- The **Country** and **Currency** if the closure applies only to a specific market.
- For a recurring entry, the **Month** and **Day**; for a one-off entry, the full **Holiday Date**.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Non Trading Dates** and select **Create**.
2. Enter **Holiday Name** and select **Country** and **Currency** when the holiday is market-specific.
3. Decide the entry type before saving. Turn **Fixed** on and enter **Month** and **Day** for a recurring holiday, or leave **Fixed** off and enter **Holiday Date** for a one-off closure.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal save flow.
5. Reopen the entry in **Fixed Dates** or **Non Fixed Dates** and verify its scope and date values.

## How do I know it worked?

The holiday appears in the matching view. Fixed entries show **Month** and **Day**; one-off entries show **Holiday Date**. Business-day calculations skip weekends and the holidays recorded here.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the date fields do not match the kind of entry, confirm **Fixed** was set correctly before creation; it cannot be changed afterward.
- If the required country or currency is not offered, that value must be configured before it can be selected.
- Entries cannot be deleted. Correct the existing record rather than creating a replacement when the holiday details were entered incorrectly.

## What should I do next?
