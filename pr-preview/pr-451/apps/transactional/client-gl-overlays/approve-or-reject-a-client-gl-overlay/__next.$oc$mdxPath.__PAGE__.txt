# Approve or reject a client GL overlay

Review a submitted client GL overlay and approve it for use or reject it so it remains inactive.

## Who can do this?

You need the **Treasury** role to use **Approve** or **Reject**.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved client GL overlay with an approval request linked to it.
- The company, client, financial node, client GL code/account and effective period needed to judge the mapping.
- The accounting approval needed to make this client-specific mapping active.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Client GL Overlays** and select the submitted overlay.
2. Review the **Company**, **Client**, **Company Financial Node**, **Client GL Code**, any **Client General Ledger Account**, and the effective dates.
3. If you approve the mapping, select **Approve** and provide an **Approval Narrative** if one is needed for the decision.
4. If you reject it, select **Reject** and enter the required rejection reason.
5. Reopen or refresh the overlay and check **Is Active**.

## How do I know it worked?

After approval, **Is Active** is on and **Approved By** is recorded. The overlay applies only when the posting date is within its effective period. After rejection, **Is Active** is off and the overlay is not applied.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the actions are refused, confirm you have the Treasury role.
- If the overlay does not contain enough information to decide, contact the requester to resolve the client, node, account or effective-period details before approving it.
- If another active overlay exists for the same company, client and financial node, resolve that conflict before relying on the mapping.

## What should I do next?

After approval, verify the mapping on a relevant posting when one occurs.
