# Activate an instrument for a company

Complete the company's eligibility evidence and independent confirmation, then activate the instrument so it can be selected for that company.

## Who can do this?

Users with **Portal-Access** can create an eligibility record. **Treasury** is required to upload evidence, validate the evidence chain, confirm eligibility and activate the instrument. The person who confirms eligibility must be different from the evidence uploader and follow the Compliance Officer review procedure.

## What do I need before I start?

- The instrument and company. Eligibility is resolved from the instrument family, company regulatory category and jurisdiction.
- An eligibility rule that allows the instrument for that company. A prohibited combination cannot be activated.
- Access to the required supporting documents and an independent authorized reviewer.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Instrument Catalogue**, select the instrument and choose **Create Eligibility Record**. Select the company. The platform creates a pending record and lists the evidence required by its rule.
2. For each required pending item, select **Upload Evidence**, choose the company and **Evidence Type**, and attach the corresponding document.
3. Select **Validate Evidence Chain** and review the completed and pending counts. Resolve every missing required item before continuing.
4. A different authorized user selects **Confirm Eligibility**, chooses the company and enters a compliance statement of at least 50 characters. The uploader cannot confirm their own evidence.
5. Once the record is **Confirmed**, select **Activate Instrument** for the company.
6. Reopen **Company Activation** and **Eligibility Record** to review the active status and the recorded evidence/confirmation history.

## How do I know it worked?

The company's activation is **Active**, and the eligibility history contains the confirmation and activation entries. The platform also raises a follow-up task to complete the instrument's GL mapping.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If a record already exists for this company and instrument, use the existing eligibility record rather than creating a second one.
- If the instrument is prohibited or no eligibility rule matches the company, stop and contact the platform administrator; do not bypass the rule.
- If evidence remains pending, upload the missing required documents and validate again.
- If the uploader tries to confirm, ask a different authorized reviewer to confirm; the separation-of-duties rule cannot be bypassed.
- If activation is refused, confirm eligibility is **Confirmed** and that the instrument is not already active for the company.

## What should I do next?
