# Reinstate a suspended instrument

Restore a company's instrument activation after the reason for a temporary suspension has been resolved.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to use **Reinstate Instrument**. Follow the company's approval and compliance procedures before restoring access to the instrument.

## What do I need before I start?

- An instrument whose latest eligibility status is **SUSPENDED** for the company.
- Confirmation that the suspension reason has been resolved and the instrument may be used again.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Instrument Catalogue**, select the instrument and review the company's latest **Eligibility Record** and **Company Activation**.
2. Confirm the latest status is **SUSPENDED** and the restriction is cleared.
3. Select **Reinstate Instrument** and choose the company.
4. Reopen the activation and eligibility history to verify the new active status.

## How do I know it worked?

The company activation returns to **ACTIVE**. A new sealed eligibility-history entry records the reinstatement; the prior suspension entry remains in the chain.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the latest status is not **SUSPENDED**, this action cannot reinstate it; review the current eligibility history first.
- If the instrument was **REVOKED**, direct reinstatement is refused. Revocation requires a completely new evidence chain; contact your platform administrator to arrange the approved new eligibility path.
- If the action is unavailable, check your Treasury role, the selected company and the activation's current status.
