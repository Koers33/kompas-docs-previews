# Activate a company financial node

Propose a company’s use of a standard financial node, then approve the saved record so it becomes active for that company.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles your administrator assigned. The service adds no separate role restriction, but activating a node is an authorization decision; follow your company’s approval procedure.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company and the **Global Financial Node** it should use.
- The local **Company Node Code**, permitted accounting paths and **Effective From** date.
- Any **Override Mapping** details required for this node.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Company Standard Financial Nodes** and select **Create**.
2. Choose the **Company** and **Global Financial Node**. Enter the **Company Node Code**, set **Permitted Paths** and **Effective From**, and complete **Override Mapping** if needed.
3. Save the record. It remains inactive until approved.
4. Review the saved configuration and, when authorized, select **Approve**.
5. Refresh the record and confirm **Active** is on and the **Approval Record** section identifies the approver.

## How do I know it worked?

The node is **Active**, and **Approved By** and the approval record are populated. The company can now use that node in account mappings and related accounting configuration.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Approve** is unavailable, check that the record is saved and inactive; the action is not offered for an already-active node.
- If the company or global node is not selectable, confirm those records exist and are available in their respective apps.
- If the node is already active, review it rather than approving it again.
- Company financial nodes cannot be deleted. To stop using one after activation, follow [Take a company financial node out of use](./take-a-company-financial-node-out-of-use).
