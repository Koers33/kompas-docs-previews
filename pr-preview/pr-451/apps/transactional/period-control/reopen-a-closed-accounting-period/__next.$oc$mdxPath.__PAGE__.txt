# Reopen a closed accounting period

Return a closed, unlocked accounting period to Open when approved work still needs to be posted.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required. Follow the accounting period-change approval procedure before reopening a period.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Company**, **Ledger Code** and **Period Name** of the period.
- A period currently in **Closed** status; a **Locked** period is final and cannot be reopened.
- Confirmation of what postings remain and the planned close-and-lock steps after they are completed.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Period Control** and find the period by company, ledger and period name.
2. Confirm its **Status** is **Closed**, then review the close checklist and timestamps.
3. Run **reopenPeriod**.
4. Refresh the period and confirm **Status** is **Open**. The prior **Closed By** and **Closed At** values are cleared.

## How do I know it worked?

The period returns to **Open**, allowing postings under the normal accounting controls. It remains the same period record; it is not recreated.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the period is **Locked**, it cannot be reopened; do not attempt to bypass the final lock.
- If the period is already **Open**, there is nothing to reopen.
- If the action is refused, confirm the period's current status and your Treasury access before retrying.
