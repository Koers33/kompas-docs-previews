# Payment Requests app

Payment Requests is where a payment to a supplier is formalised and tracked: one request names the **Client** and **Supplier Business Partner**, the invoice and **Payment Due Date**, the **Pay Currency** and amounts, the **Payment Condition**, **Payment Option**, **Payment Urgency** and **Payment Remainder**, the **Project** it belongs to, the **Supplier Bank Account**, and a **Payment Request Status** that says where the request stands.

## What is this app for?

A request turns an invoice or advance into something treasury can act on, with the paperwork attached to it. The record is numbered for you when it is created, the tax code is filled in from configuration, and the sections on the record hold the supporting detail: **General**, **Notes** and **Documents**.

The request carries a **Payment Condition** — **Advance Payment** or **Payment After Delivery** — which decides which documents have to be attached, and a **Finalised** flag that locks the details before the request is sent.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to raise a payment for a client's supplier, to finish the documentation on a request that is still open, or to see what has already been submitted to treasury. Creating and sending a request are steps 2.4 and 2.5 of the [client workflows guide](/user-guides/client/client-workflows), which also covers the client request and internal contract that lead up to it.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). Payment request records themselves are granted to the Treasury role, and client users reach them through their business partner relationship: a **Request Author** relationship gives full access to that client's requests, while a **Request Viewer** relationship is read-only. Whichever applies, a client user sees only the requests of the client they are linked to.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Create a request. It opens as a draft with the status **Pre Open**, a generated **Payment Request Number**, and the tax code filled in from configuration.
2. Complete the mandatory details — client, supplier, project, invoice, dates, currency, amounts, payment condition and supplier bank account — and save. **Tax Amount To Pay** and **Total Amount To Pay** are read-only on the record.
3. Check the paperwork against **Documents**, using **Documents Submitted** to confirm what the chosen payment condition requires.
4. Switch **Finalised** on. The details lock, and **Send Request** appears on the saved record.
5. Select **Send Request**. The status moves to **Waiting Treasury** and the request becomes read-only — from here it is treasury's work, and the **Notes** section is where the conversation continues.

Treat this as orientation; follow your organisation's payment procedure before sending.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Editing has one window:** a request is editable only while its status is **Pre Open** and **Finalised** is still off. Once it is finalised or sent, the fields are locked for everyone.
- **Send Request appears only on a saved record** that is still **Pre Open** and already finalised — an unsaved draft or an unfinalised record offers no button.
- **The document check depends on the Payment Condition:** the required documents differ between **Advance Payment** and **Payment After Delivery**, and the action reports the missing ones by name. Once the request has reached **Documents Submitted**, **Payment Completed**, **Remainder Completed** or **Transaction Completed**, the check is no longer offered.
- **Fields follow your choices:** **Supplier Bank Account** is shown once a supplier is selected, and **Project Item** once a project is selected; clearing the supplier or project clears the dependent field.
- **Amounts must agree:** the save is refused if **Total Amount To Pay** does not equal the amount excluding tax plus the tax amount, or if the supplier bank account currency does not match **Pay Currency**. The message names the field.
- **Notes are shared selectively:** treasury readers see notes flagged **Show To Treasury**, and a client user sees the notes they created themselves.
- **Requests are not deleted here** — the delete action is hidden.

## Where can I review the results?

The list carries the status, the client and supplier, the invoice and due dates, the amounts and **Finalised**, so a single row answers "where is this request?". Filter it by supplier, pay currency, invoice date or payment due date. On the record, **General** holds the request itself, **Notes** holds the discussion, and **Documents** holds the supporting files with who uploaded them and when.

Progress beyond **Waiting Treasury** is not something you type in: the status field is maintained by the platform's actions. Confirm the wider outcome in the process that consumes the request rather than expecting this app to record it.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- **The request will not save:** complete the mandatory fields, then check the message about the total amount or the bank account currency.
- **Send Request is not offered:** check that the request is saved, still **Pre Open**, and that **Finalised** is switched on.
- **Documents Submitted reports missing documents:** upload the named documents under **Documents** and run the check again — the required set follows the **Payment Condition**.
- **You cannot see another client's request:** client access follows the business partner relationship described above. See [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
