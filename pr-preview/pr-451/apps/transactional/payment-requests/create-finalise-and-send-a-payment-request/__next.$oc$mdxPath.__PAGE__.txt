# Create, finalise and send a payment request

Create a supplier payment request, attach the documents required by its payment condition, finalise it and send it to Treasury.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users can work with payment requests. A Client user linked as a **Request Author** can create and send requests for that client; a **Request Viewer** can read them only. Both client roles are limited to their linked client.

## What do I need before I start?

- The client, supplier, project and invoice details.
- The invoice date, **Payment Due Date**, pay currency, amount excluding tax, tax amount and total amount.
- The supplier bank account in the same currency as **Pay Currency**.
- The **Payment Condition** and its required documents. Payment options, remainder and urgency are available where they apply.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Payment Requests** and select **Create**. The new request starts in **Pre Open**, receives a **Payment Request Number**, and its tax code is populated from configuration.
2. Complete **Client**, **Supplier Business Partner**, **Project**, **Invoice Number**, **Invoice Date**, **Payment Due Date**, **Pay Currency**, **Amount To Pay Excluding Tax**, **Tax Amount To Pay**, **Total Amount To Pay**, **Supplier Bank Account** and **Payment Condition**. Add optional payment details as needed.
3. Save the request and attach the documents required for the selected payment condition in **Documents**. Confirm the total equals amount excluding tax plus tax, and the bank account currency matches **Pay Currency**.
4. Review the request. Switch **Finalised** on and save; this locks the request details.
5. Select **Send Request** while the saved request is still **Pre Open** and finalised. The request moves to **Waiting Treasury** and becomes read-only for the client.
6. When offered, select **Documents Submitted** to validate the attachments for the selected payment condition. If required documents are missing, the app names them; attach them and run the check again.

## How do I know it worked?

The request has a generated number and shows **Waiting Treasury** after **Send Request**. If the document check completes, the status becomes **Documents Submitted**. The request and its attachments remain available for Treasury follow-up.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If save reports an amount mismatch, ensure **Total Amount To Pay** equals **Amount To Pay Excluding Tax** plus **Tax Amount To Pay**.
- If the supplier bank is refused, select an account whose currency matches **Pay Currency**.
- If the document check lists missing files, attach the named document types before repeating it.
- **Send Request** is offered only for a saved, finalised request still in **Pre Open**. If it is already finalised or sent, its details cannot be edited here.
- If the supplier, project or bank is missing from a value help, configure the record in its owning app first.

## What should I do next?

Track the request in **Payment Requests** and continue the wider client-to-payment sequence in the [Client Workflows Guide](/user-guides/client/client-workflows).
