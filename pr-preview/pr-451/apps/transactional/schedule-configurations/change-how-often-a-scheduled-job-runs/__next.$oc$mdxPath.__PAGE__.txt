# Change how often a scheduled job runs

Update the interval used by an existing recurring job and its human-readable interval label.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read and update schedule rows; the app declares no additional role restriction. Confirm the intended cadence with the owner of the job before saving.

## What do I need before I start?

- The job's **Name**, **Technical Name** or **App List** so you can identify the correct row.
- The approved **Interval (Minutes)** and matching **Interval Label**.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Schedule Configurations** and find the job by name, app or technical name.
2. Open the row and check its **Is Active** state; this field is read-only here.
3. Update **Interval (Minutes)** to a positive whole number. Update **Interval Label** if its readable description should match.
4. Save and activate the draft using the app's normal save flow.
5. Reopen the row and verify the interval and label.

## How do I know it worked?

The row shows the new **Interval (Minutes)** and **Interval Label**. The platform uses the new interval the next time it plans that job's run; the label is descriptive only.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the interval is missing or not positive, enter a positive number of minutes.
- If **Name**, **Technical Name**, **App List** or **Is Active** is read-only, that is expected; only interval and interval label are maintained here.
- If the job is missing, jobs are platform-configured and cannot be created in this app. Contact the platform administrator.
- This app changes cadence; it does not run a job immediately. Use the owning app's on-demand action where one exists.
