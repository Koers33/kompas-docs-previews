# Propose and decide a position lifecycle change

Propose an agreed partial close, full close, rollover, early termination or other position change, then have an independent Treasury user authorise or reject it.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to propose, authorise and reject lifecycle actions. The proposer cannot authorise their own proposal; a different Treasury user must make the authorisation decision. Client users can read positions but cannot change them.

## What do I need before I start?

- A position in **Open** status.
- The approved **Action Type**, closing amount and **Remarks** describing the change.
- A second Treasury user available to decide the proposal.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Positions**, select the open position and review its contractual amount, currency and accumulator fields.
2. Select **Propose Lifecycle Action**. Choose **Action Type** from the list, enter the agreed **Closing Amount** and **Remarks**, then submit.
3. Confirm the position moves to **Proposed** and review the resulting **Lifecycle Action** record.
4. A different Treasury user opens the proposed position and selects the matching lifecycle action record.
5. Select **Authorise Lifecycle Action** to approve the proposal, or **Reject Lifecycle Action** and enter the rejection reason.
6. Refresh the position and review its status and any linked continuation position.

## How do I know it worked?

The lifecycle action shows **Authorised** or **Rejected**. An authorised partial close creates a new open continuation position for the remaining amount; a full close leaves no remainder. The action record retains its proposer, decision-maker, timestamps and remarks/rejection reason.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the position is not **Open**, it cannot take a new proposal; check its current status first.
- The closing amount cannot exceed the position's contractual amount. Confirm the approved amount before submitting.
- If authorisation is refused because you proposed the action, ask a different Treasury user to decide it. Do not bypass the separation-of-duties rule.
- If you need to change contractual amount or currency, those fields are immutable; use a lifecycle proposal rather than editing them directly.
