# Review a journal entry for a deal

Find the accounting entry produced for a deal occasion and trace its postings, hash chain and any reversal link.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read entries; the app is read-only and has no create, edit or delete controls.

## What do I need before I start?

- The deal number or deal record.
- The occasion you want to review, such as **Deal Confirmed**, **Periodic Revaluation**, **Maturity Settlement**, **Deal Rolled**, **Deal Cancelled** or **Deal Drawdown**.
- The ledger if you need a specific reporting view.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Journal Entries** and filter by **Deal**, **Occasion Type**, **Processed At** or **Ledger Code**.
2. Open the matching entry and review **General Information** for the deal, occasion, ledger, processing time, creator, **Entry Hash** and **Previous Hash**.
3. In **Postings**, review each sequence, general ledger account, debit/credit indicator, amount, currency and category.
4. If **Reversal Of** is populated, follow it to identify the entry being reversed and compare the original and reversing postings.

## How do I know it worked?

You can identify the entry's deal and occasion and see the complete debit/credit lines behind it. A reversal entry links to the original entry through **Reversal Of**; the hash fields show its position in the entry chain.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If an expected entry is missing, confirm the deal occasion was processed and clear or broaden the filters. Entries are created by platform processing, not in this app.
- If processing was blocked, check the user-readable message.
- If you need to correct an entry, do not edit it here. Request a correction through [Accounting Reversal Requests](/user-guides/accounting-reversal-requests).

## What should I do next?
