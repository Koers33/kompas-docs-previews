# Suspend or revoke an instrument for a company

Stop a company from using an instrument by suspending it temporarily or revoking its eligibility, and record the reason for the decision.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to use **Suspend Instrument** or **Revoke Instrument**. Follow the company's approval and compliance procedures before changing an instrument's availability.

## What do I need before I start?

- The instrument and company activation to change.
- A clear suspension or revocation reason.
- A decision on whether the restriction is temporary (**Suspend**) or a revocation requiring a new eligibility evidence chain before future use (**Revoke**).

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Instrument Catalogue**, select the instrument and review **Company Activation** and its **Eligibility Record** history.
2. Select **Suspend Instrument** for a temporary stop, or **Revoke Instrument** for a revocation.
3. Select the company and enter the required **Suspension Reason** or **Revocation Reason**.
4. Submit the action. Reopen the company activation and eligibility history to confirm the new status and reason.

## How do I know it worked?

The company activation is no longer active. The history records a **SUSPENDED** or **REVOKED** entry, the actor, time and reason; previous sealed entries remain intact.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the app requires a reason, enter it before submitting.
- If **Suspend Instrument** reports that there is no active scope, confirm the instrument is currently active for the selected company.
- If the activation is already **REVOKED**, do not revoke it again.
- A suspended instrument can be reinstated without a new evidence chain. A revoked instrument requires a completely new evidence chain; the direct **Reinstate Instrument** action refuses a revoked record. Contact your platform administrator to arrange the approved new eligibility path.

## What should I do next?
