# Register a model

Create a catalogue record for a model the platform has adopted, starting its registration in Development status.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can create and update registrations. The app declares no narrower role restriction; follow your model-governance process when recording a model or changing its stage.

## What do I need before I start?

- A unique **Model Code** that will remain unchanged after creation.
- The **Model Name**, **Version** and initial **Status**.
- The registration date and registrant, if your procedure requires specific values.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Models Catalogue** and select **Create**.
2. Enter **Model Code**, **Name** and **Version**. A new record defaults to **Development** status and today's **Registered At** date.
3. Confirm the **Status** describes the model's current stage. The app records the status; it does not perform validation or approval.
4. Save and activate the draft using the app's normal save flow.
5. Reopen the registration and verify its code, name, version, status and registration date.

## How do I know it worked?

The model appears in the list with its code, name, version, status and registration date. **Governance** shows who created and last changed the record.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the model code already exists, open that registration instead of creating a duplicate.
- If a required field is missing, complete **Model Code**, **Name** and **Status**.
- Keep the code within 50 characters, the name within 255 and the version within 20. **Registered At** cannot be a future date.
- The app does not approve or validate a model; complete those activities through the model-governance process before setting a later status.

## What should I do next?
