# Manage temporary company gate bypasses

Temporarily bypass selected gates for a non-production company, then clear the bypasses when their purpose or expiry period ends.

## Who can do this?

You need the **Treasury** role to update bypass flags or run either deactivation action.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company’s existing enablement record and a documented reason for each exception under your operating procedure.
- A company in **UAT** or **Development**. Bypass flags cannot be activated for a **Production** company.
- An expiry date within 30 days for every flag you turn on.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Company Feature Enablements** and open the record for the company. Confirm its environment before changing a flag.
2. In **Bypass Flags**, switch on only the required flags: **Bypass Readiness Gates**, **Bypass Policy Gates**, **Bypass Know Your Customer Gate**, **Bypass Business Window** or **Bypass Market Data**.
3. Set an expiry date within 30 days for each enabled flag, then save.
4. When the exception is no longer needed, either switch off the relevant flags on this record or select **Deactivate All Bypasses** to clear every bypass on this record.
5. To clean up expired bypasses across enablement records, select **Deactivate Expired Bypasses**. This action checks all records, not only the one currently open.
6. Refresh the affected records and confirm the bypass switches are off and their expiry dates are cleared.

## How do I know it worked?

Enabled flags show an expiry date and are honored only in UAT or Development. After you turn a flag off or use a deactivation action, its expiry date is cleared. **Deactivate Expired Bypasses** clears only flags whose expiry date has passed.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the company is in **Production**, bypass activation is refused by design. Production ignores bypass flags.
- If a flag cannot be saved, set its expiry date to today or within the next 30 days.
- If you turn a flag off but an expiry remains, save and refresh the record; disabling a flag clears its expiry date.
- If you cannot update the record or run the actions, check that you have the Treasury role and that the gate is in a state that permits the action.

## What should I do next?
