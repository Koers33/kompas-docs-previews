# Change a company’s gate enforcement mode

Set whether a company’s readiness gates block work or provide warnings, and record the reason when changing enforcement to informational.

## Who can do this?

You need the **Treasury** role to update Company Feature Enablements records.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company’s existing enablement record. There is one record per company.
- The approved enforcement decision and, when switching to **Informational**, the reason to record in the audit trail.
- The company’s environment. The environment is fixed to the company and cannot be changed on the enablement record.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Company Feature Enablements** and find the record for the company.
2. Open the record and change **Gate Enforcement** to **Blocking** or **Informational**.
3. If you switch to **Informational**, enter the required change reason.
4. Save the record.
5. Reopen the **Audit Trail** section and verify the enforcement change, timestamp, user and reason.

## How do I know it worked?

The record shows the selected **Gate Enforcement** mode. Under **Blocking**, actions are held until gates pass. Under **Informational**, gates issue warnings instead of blocking. The **Audit Trail** records who changed the mode, when and why.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the app refuses a switch to **Informational**, provide the required reason and save again.
- If you cannot edit the record, check that you have the Treasury role.
- If the company or environment is wrong, correct the company record first; those fields are fixed on an enablement record.
