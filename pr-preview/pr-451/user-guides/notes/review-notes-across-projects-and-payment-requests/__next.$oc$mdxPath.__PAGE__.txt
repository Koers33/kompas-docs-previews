# Review notes across projects and payment requests

Find notes written across supported work areas and read their classification, detail, level and author without opening each source record.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users can read all notes. Client users can read only notes linked to their own client business partner; available records therefore differ by role and relationship.

## What do I need before I start?

- The note's approximate **Note Date**, project or payment request, or the area where it was written.
- Access to the source record if you need to follow up or change the note.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Notes** and filter by **Note Date** to narrow the period.
2. Choose **All Notes**, **Projects**, **Project Items** or **Payment Requests** based on the note's source area.
3. Open the matching note and read **Classification**, **Note Detail**, **Level**, **Level Description**, **Note Date** and who recorded it.
4. If you need to add or change a note, open the source record in the app that owns it.

## How do I know it worked?

The note appears in the relevant view and its record details identify its source area, level, text, date and author.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the note is not in the selected view, switch to **All Notes** or the other relevant area view.
- If a client note is missing, confirm the active relationship linking your user to that client business partner.
- No create or edit action is available here. Add or change the note in Projects, Payment Requests or the other app that owns its source record.

## What should I do next?
