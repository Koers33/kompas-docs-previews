# Take a company financial node out of use

Reject an active company financial node to switch it off while retaining its configuration and approval history.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles your administrator assigned. Follow your company’s approval procedure before rejecting an active node.

## What do I need before I start?

- The active company node that should no longer be used.
- The required rejection reason and confirmation that dependent mappings or treasury accounts have been reviewed.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Company Standard Financial Nodes** and find the node by company and **Company Node Code**.
2. Open the active record and review its **Global Financial Node**, **Permitted Paths** and **Effective From**.
3. Select **Reject** and enter the required rejection reason.
4. Refresh the record and confirm **Active** is off and the approval details have been cleared.

## How do I know it worked?

The node is inactive and remains in the app’s records. It is retained rather than deleted.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Reject** is unavailable, confirm the record is active; rejection is not offered for an inactive node.
- If the app requests a rejection reason, provide it before submitting the decision.
- If the record is already inactive, no further rejection is needed.

## What should I do next?
