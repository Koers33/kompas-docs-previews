# Add or update a glossary term

Add a platform term or correct its definition and context so the published glossary uses the agreed wording.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Coordinate changes to shared terminology with the relevant domain owner.

## What do I need before I start?

- The approved term and any abbreviation.
- A clear **Short Definition** and, where useful, an **Extended Definition**, formula/example or regulatory reference.
- The **Category**, **Scope** and **Audience Level** that describe the entry.
- The apps or pages where the term is used, related terms and an effective date if applicable.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Glossary**. Select **Create** for a new term, or find and open the existing term to correct it.
2. Enter **Term** and **Abbreviation**. Choose **Category**, **Scope** and **Audience Level** from their lists.
3. Write the **Short Definition** first, then complete the extended definition and supporting fields as needed.
4. Keep **Where Used** and **Related Terms** current. If this term replaces another, enter **Superseded By** and **Effective From**.
5. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal save flow. To retire a term while retaining its history, switch **Active** off.

## How do I know it worked?

The term appears in the list with its classification and short definition. Open it to review the complete entry. The published [Glossary](/glossary) uses the short definition from this same term set.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If a category, scope or audience level is not available, contact the owner of the corresponding platform list; these values are not created in this app.
- If the term already exists, update that record rather than creating a duplicate.
- **Where Used** and **Related Terms** are notes, not links; enter the names as text and keep them accurate.
- If a term has been replaced, create or update the replacement first, then reference it in **Superseded By**.
