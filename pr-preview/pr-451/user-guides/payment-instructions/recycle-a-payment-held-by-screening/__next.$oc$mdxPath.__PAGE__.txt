# Recycle a payment held by screening

After the screening case is resolved, return a held payment to authorisation and let it pass through screening again.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to work with payment instructions. Follow the applicable sanctions and AML case procedure; do not recycle a payment while its blocking case remains unresolved.

## What do I need before I start?

- A payment instruction with **Payment Status** **Held**.
- Confirmation that the **Blocking Reason** has been investigated and the related case has been resolved.
- The payment reference and relevant case record for your review.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Payment Instructions** and filter **Payment Status** to **Held**.
2. Open the payment and review its **Screening Status**, **Blocking Reason**, deal and payment details.
3. Confirm through your AML/sanctions process that the hold is resolved.
4. Select **Recycle Held Payment**.
5. Refresh the record. Confirm **Payment Status** returns to **Awaiting Authorisation**, **Screening Status** is **Pending Screening**, and the blocking reason is cleared.
6. Continue with **Authorise Payment** only after the screening result no longer reports a **Hit**.

## How do I know it worked?

The payment returns to the authorisation queue and screening is pending again. The platform records the recycling decision in its payment-recycling audit ledger.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Recycle Held Payment** is unavailable, confirm the payment is still **Held**; the action is only offered in that state.
- If the hold is not resolved, stop and follow the AML/sanctions escalation process. Recycling is not a way to override a screening hit.
- If the payment is no longer held, review its current status before taking another action.
- Payment details are read-only in this app. Do not edit the amount, beneficiary or screening fields to clear a hold.

## What should I do next?
