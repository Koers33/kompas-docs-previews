# Approve or reject a treasury account

Review a submitted treasury-account mapping and approve it for use or reject it, which deactivates the account.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to use **Approve** or **Reject**. Follow the company's approval procedure and record the rationale in the approval narrative or supporting governance record.

## What do I need before I start?

- A treasury account with an approval record linked in its **Approval** section.
- The company, company financial node, GL account, accounting path, purpose and leg type reviewed.
- The approval decision and any narrative required by your procedure.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Treasury Account Master** and select the submitted account.
2. Review its **General** section and linked **Approval** record.
3. Select **Approve** and enter an **Approval Narrative** if prompted, or select **Reject** if the mapping should not proceed.
4. Refresh the record and review the approval record and **Is Active** state.

## How do I know it worked?

After approval, **Approved By** is populated and the linked approval record shows the approver and time. After rejection, **Is Active** is switched off; the account remains in the register rather than being deleted.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the approval record is missing, submit the account first using [Submit a treasury account for approval](./submit-a-treasury-account-for-approval).
- If the action is refused, confirm the user has the Treasury role and check the current account and approval record.
- Rejection deactivates the account. If it should be used later, follow your governance process before activating or submitting a corrected account.
