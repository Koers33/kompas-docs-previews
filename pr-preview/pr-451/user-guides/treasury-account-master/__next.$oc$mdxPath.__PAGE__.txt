# Treasury Account Master app

Treasury Account Master is where you register the treasury accounts a company posts through. Each record gives the account its own code and name, attaches it to a company, a **Company Financial Node** and a **GL Account**, and records the **Accounting Path**, **Account Purpose** and **Leg Type** that describe how it is used. These records sit between a company's financial nodes and its general ledger, so an account mapping can name the treasury account that leads to a given ledger account.

## What is this app for?

Each record answers one setup question: which treasury account applies here? The **Accounting Path** says whether the account covers **Own Book**, **Client**, **All** or **Hybrid** business, the **Account Purpose** says what the account captures — **Position**, **Income**, **Settlement**, **Fund Transfer Pricing** or **Expected Credit Loss** — and the **Leg Type** describes the account's job in a deal leg, for example a position account or a settlement account. An **Effective From** date and the **Is Active** flag say when the account applies.

## When should I use this app?

Use it while you set up a company's accounting — after the [General Ledger Accounts app](/user-guides/general-ledger-accounts) and the [Company Standard Financial Nodes app](/user-guides/company-standard-financial-nodes) hold the accounts and nodes, and before the [Financial Node Account Mappings app](/user-guides/financial-node-account-mappings) rows that reference a treasury account. Come back when a company needs a new treasury account, when an account's purpose or path changes, or when you need to check which node or ledger an existing account points to during a review.

Client-specific account overlays are not done here — they belong in the [Client GL Overlays app](/user-guides/client-gl-overlays).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The register itself carries no audience restriction beyond portal access, so reading treasury accounts is open to anyone who reaches the app. Creating or changing a treasury account, and using **Submit For Approval**, **Approve** or **Reject**, additionally require the **Treasury** role; treat approval as a governed step and follow your approval procedure before using it.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Create the account as a draft: choose the **Company**, **Company Financial Node**, **GL Account** and **Accounting Path**, give the record a **Treasury Account Code** and **Treasury Account Name**, add its **Account Purpose**, **Leg Type** and **Effective From** where they apply, then save.
2. Saving runs two checks: the selected financial node must belong to the selected company, and the node's category must suit the type of the selected general ledger account.
3. Select **Submit For Approval** on the saved record. This raises an approval record against the account, which starts in a pending state.
4. A Treasury user selects **Approve**, optionally adding an approval narrative. The approver and the time of approval are written to the approval record, and **Approved By** is filled in on the account.
5. Alternatively, **Reject** switches the account off — the record stays in the register but is marked inactive rather than deleted.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Role:** without the **Treasury** role you can read records but cannot save changes or use any of the three approval actions.
- **Locked identity:** the **Treasury Account Code** cannot be changed once the record is saved.
- **Compatibility rules:** a save is refused when the financial node belongs to another company, or when the node's category and the general ledger account's type do not suit each other.
- **Mandatory inputs:** the company financial node, general ledger account and accounting path must be filled before the record can be saved.
- **Active state:** a rejected account becomes inactive, and the list can be filtered on **Is Active** to separate live accounts from ones that were switched off.
- **No deletion:** records cannot be removed from this app, so **Reject** is the way to retire an account.
- **Picks from fixed lists:** company, financial node, general ledger account, accounting path, purpose and leg type are all chosen from the values offered in the field, not typed in.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows each account's code, name, company, company financial node, general ledger account and active state at a glance. On the record, the **General** section holds the full definition with its path, purpose, leg type and effective date, while the **Approval** section shows who approved it and the approval record it is linked to. Once an account exists, you can also see it selected on the mapping rows in the [Financial Node Account Mappings app](/user-guides/financial-node-account-mappings).

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- Saving is refused with a compatibility message: check that the financial node belongs to the same company, and pick a general ledger account whose type suits that node's category.
- An approval action is refused with a permission message: **Submit For Approval**, **Approve** and **Reject** need the **Treasury** role — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
- A field will not accept a value: company, financial node, general ledger account and accounting path are picked from the offered list, and the account code is fixed after saving.
- The app itself does not open: check your access first — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
