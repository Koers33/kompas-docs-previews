# Review a financial node mapping flagged for review

Reconfirm or unlink a mapping after a change to its company financial node makes the existing mapping require review.

## Who can do this?

Users with access to the Financial Node Account Mappings app can review a mapping. Follow the company’s accounting approval process before confirming a mapping that is used for postings.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved mapping in **Review Required** status.
- The current company node definition and the mapping’s accounting path, leg role, treasury account and general ledger account.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Financial Node Account Mappings** and filter **Mapping Status** to **Review Required**.
2. Open the mapping and review the **Mapping Definition** and **Chain Preview** against the current node definition.
3. Select **Review GL Mapping** and confirm whether the mapping is still valid.
4. If valid, confirm the review; the status returns to **Linked**.
5. If it is not valid, do not confirm it. The mapping becomes **Unlinked** and its general ledger account association is cleared.

## How do I know it worked?

The status reflects the decision: **Linked** for a confirmed mapping or **Unlinked** when it was not confirmed. The mapping is no longer left in **Review Required**.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Review GL Mapping** is not available, check that the saved mapping is in **Review Required** status.
- If the mapping does not match the current node or account configuration, leave it unconfirmed and have the correct mapping established before relying on postings.

## What should I do next?
