# Request approval for a balance-sheet mapping

Raise an approval request for a balance-sheet general ledger mapping, then have a Treasury user approve the pending request.

## Who can do this?

Users with access to the app can request approval for eligible mappings. **Approve GL Mapping** requires the Treasury role.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved mapping whose general ledger account type is a balance-sheet type.
- A mapping in **Linked** or **Unlinked** status.
- The account and node details reviewed under your company’s approval process.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the eligible mapping and select **Request CFO Approval**.
2. Confirm that an approval request is created and the mapping moves to **Pending Approval**.
3. A Treasury user opens the pending mapping and reviews its company, node, path, leg role and accounts.
4. The Treasury user selects **Approve GL Mapping**.
5. Reopen or refresh the mapping and confirm the status returns to **Linked** and the approval request is recorded as approved.

## How do I know it worked?

The mapping returns to **Linked**, and the linked approval request shows an approved decision. The mapping retains its account pairing and approval reference.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Request CFO Approval** is refused, confirm the mapping’s account type is balance sheet and the status is **Linked** or **Unlinked**.
- If **Approve GL Mapping** is refused, the acting user must have the Treasury role and the approval request must still be pending.
- If the mapping is in another status, resolve its review or linkage state before requesting approval.
