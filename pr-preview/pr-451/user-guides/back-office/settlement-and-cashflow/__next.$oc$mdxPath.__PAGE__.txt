# Settlement and Cashflow Guide

> **Goal**: Process the settlement of a confirmed deal through payment instructions, bank confirmations, and cashflow matching.
>
> **Prerequisites**: Phase 3 complete (Deal confirmed, cashflow items and journal entries generated).

---

## Step 4.1 — View the Payment Instruction

**App**: Payment Instructions
**Action**: View the auto-generated payment instruction

After deal confirmation, the system generates payment instructions for settlement.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Payment Reference | Unique payment identifier |
| Deal | Link to the confirmed deal |
| Amount | Payment amount |
| Currency | Payment currency |
| Value Date | When payment is due |
| Payment Status | `Awaiting Authorisation` |
| Screening Status | `Pending Screening` |
| Payment Channel | `SAMOS`, `SWIFT`, etc. |

---

## Step 4.2 — Authorise the Payment

**App**: Payment Instructions
**Action**: Click "Authorise Payment"

Before payment can be processed, it must be authorised.

1. Open the Payment Instruction record
2. Review the payment details
3. Click "Authorise Payment"
4. Sanctions screening runs automatically
5. Status changes to `Submitted` after authorisation. If a SAMOS payment is authorised outside its configured operating window, it becomes `Queued SAMOS`; the app reports the next window time.

**What happens**:
- Screening status and any blocking reason are recorded on the instruction.
- **Authorise Payment** is available only while the payment is `Awaiting Authorisation` and screening has not reported a `Hit`.
- If screening reports a hit, resolve the case before using **Recycle Held Payment**. Recycling returns the payment to `Awaiting Authorisation` with screening pending again; it does not override an unresolved hold.

---

## Step 4.3 — Recycle a Held Payment After Case Resolution

**App**: Payment Instructions
**Action**: Click "Recycle Held Payment" after the screening case is resolved

If screening placed the instruction on hold, investigate and resolve the related case through the applicable sanctions/AML process first. Then:

1. Open the held Payment Instruction and review its screening status and blocking reason
2. After the case is resolved, click **Recycle Held Payment**
3. Confirm the instruction returns to `Awaiting Authorisation` with `Pending Screening`
4. Continue authorisation only after screening no longer reports a hit

Do not recycle a payment to bypass a screening block. The recycling decision is recorded in the payment audit ledger.

---

## Step 4.4 — Process Bank Confirmation

**App**: Payment Instructions
**Action**: Click "Process Bank Confirmation"

After the bank processes the payment, confirm receipt.

1. Open the Payment Instruction record after it reaches `Submitted`
2. Attach at least one bank-confirmation document under **Documents**
3. Confirm a projected cashflow exists for the same deal and currency
4. Click **Process Bank Confirmation**
5. Status changes to `Confirmed`; the platform creates settlement, actual-cashflow and cashflow-match records

The action does not ask you to enter a separate bank confirmation reference; the payment reference is used for the resulting records.

---

## Step 4.5 — View the Settlement

**App**: Settlements
**Action**: View the settlement record

The settlement tracks the final exchange of funds.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Settlement Reference | Unique settlement identifier |
| Deal | Link to the confirmed deal |
| Payment Instruction | Link to the payment |
| Settlement Amount | Amount settled |
| Settlement Currency | Currency settled |
| Nostro Account | Which bank account was used |
| Settlement Date | When settlement occurred |
| Status | `Settled` |

---

## Step 4.6 — View Cashflow Matching

**App**: Settlements (Confirmation Matches section)
**Action**: View the matching between projected and actual cashflows

The system matches projected cashflows against actual bank confirmations.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Projected Cashflow | Expected cashflow from the deal |
| Actual Cashflow | What the bank confirmed |
| Projected Amount | Expected amount |
| Actual Amount | Actual amount received/paid |
| Difference | Variance between projected and actual |
| Match Status | `Matched`, `Unmatched`, `Tolerance` |
| Match Method | `Exact`, `Tolerance`, `Manual` |

---

## Step 4.7 — View Cash Account Balances

**App**: Cash Account Balances
**Action**: View the nostro account balance after settlement

Track the running balance on each nostro account.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Nostro Account | Bank account |
| Currency | Account currency |
| Period | Current period (YYYY-MM) |
| Opening Balance | Balance at start of period |
| Closing Balance | Current balance |
| Total Inflows | Money received |
| Total Outflows | Money paid |
| Reconciliation Status | `Reconciled`, `Unreconciled` |

---

## Step 4.8 — Settle the Deal

**App**: Deals
**Action**: Click "Settle Deal"

Once settlement is confirmed, mark the deal as settled.

1. Open the Deal record
2. Click "Settle Deal"
3. Status changes from `CONFIRMED` to `SETTLED`
4. Position closes automatically

**After Phase 4**: The deal is fully settled. Cashflows are matched, journals are posted, and the position is closed.
