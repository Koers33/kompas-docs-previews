# Give a company the current rounding policies

Derive the active global rounding policies into a company's rounding-policy set after the global rules change.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to manage company rounding policies. Confirm the target company before running the derivation.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company that should receive the policies.
- The global policies already reviewed and saved, with the intended policies marked **Active**.
- Confirmation that inactive global policies should not be copied; the derivation includes active global policies only.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Rounding** and review the active global policies and their scopes.
2. Select **deriveCompanyPolicies** and choose the target **Company**.
3. Confirm the action and read the result counts for policies **Created** and **Updated**.
4. Re-run the derivation after future changes to global policies when the company needs the latest copies.

## How do I know it worked?

The action returns created and updated counts. Existing current company rows derived from a superseded global row are marked superseded, and a new current copy is created for the updated active policy.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the action is refused, check that a company was selected and that you have Treasury access.
- If the created count is zero, confirm the global policies are active and that the company already has current copies.
- Changing a global policy does not automatically update company copies; rerun the derivation for each company that should receive the change.

## What should I do next?

Review the counts and continue using the company-specific rounding configuration.
