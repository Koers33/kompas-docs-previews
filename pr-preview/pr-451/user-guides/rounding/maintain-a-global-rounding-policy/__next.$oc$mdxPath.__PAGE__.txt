# Maintain a global rounding policy

Add or update the global rule that controls how the platform rounds a configured calculation.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read policies. The **Treasury** role is required to create or change them.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Policy Set** and **Scope** the rule governs.
- Any applicable **Instrument Type** or **Currency**.
- **Decimal Places**, **Rounding Mode**, optional **Increment Value**, **Effective From** and, if applicable, **Effective To**.
- Approval for the policy change; rounding rules affect financial calculations.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Rounding** and filter by policy set, scope, instrument type or active state.
2. Select **Create** for a new policy or open the existing policy to update it.
3. Set the policy set, scope, decimal places, rounding mode, optional increment and effective dates. Set **Active** to show whether the rule is in force.
4. Save and activate the draft. If you are replacing a policy, confirm the new effective dates and active state before saving.
5. Reopen the policy and verify its scope, rounding values and date range.

## How do I know it worked?

The policy appears with the intended policy set, scope, decimal places, rounding mode, effective date and active state. The platform uses active policies whose date range applies to the calculation.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- **Effective From** and **Active** are required.
- **Decimal Places** must be within the supported 0–14 range.
- **Increment Value** cannot be negative; enter zero or a positive value.
- If no policy is available for a calculation, confirm the policy set, company copy and effective dates before retrying the calculation.
- If a value help is missing the policy set, scope or rounding mode, contact the owner of that platform list rather than entering an unlisted value.
