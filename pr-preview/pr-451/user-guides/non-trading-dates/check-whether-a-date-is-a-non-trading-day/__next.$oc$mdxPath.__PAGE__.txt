# Check whether a date is a non-trading day

Look up the trading-calendar entries that apply to a date, country and currency before relying on a business-day calculation.

## Who can do this?

Users with portal access can read the calendar entries, subject to their assigned roles. The app displays configured holidays; it does not calculate or move transaction dates itself.

## What do I need before I start?

- The date you want to check.
- The relevant **Country** and **Currency**, if the holiday is market-specific.
- Whether you are checking for a recurring or one-off closure.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Non Trading Dates**.
2. Search or filter by **Holiday Name**, **Country** or **Currency**.
3. For recurring holidays, open **Fixed Dates** and compare the target date's month and day with the entry.
4. For dated closures, open **Non Fixed Dates** and compare the full **Holiday Date**.
5. Confirm the entry's country and currency scope before treating it as applicable to the target market.

## How do I know it worked?

You have identified whether a matching configured holiday applies to the target date and market. Business-day calculations skip weekends and recorded holidays, but this list is not a transaction date calculator.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the entry is in the other view, switch between **Fixed Dates** and **Non Fixed Dates**.
- If no matching record appears, confirm the filters and market scope. A missing holiday must be recorded before downstream calculations can account for it.
- If a date appears wrong, correct its existing record in this app; entries are not deleted.

## What should I do next?

Use the date in the app that owns your transaction or schedule.
