# Register or update a company record

Create the legal-entity record that other Kompas apps use, or correct its permitted details without replacing its linked business partner.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles your administrator has assigned. The Companies service has no additional role grant on the company entity; ask your administrator if the app or an edit control is unavailable.

## What do I need before I start?

- The Organization business partner the company represents. It must not already be linked to another company, and the company’s **Business Partner** cannot be changed after the company is saved.
- The company code, tenant code, legal name, registration number, jurisdiction, base currency, fiscal year and operating model information needed to identify the legal entity.
- The correct environment and lifecycle status. New company records start in **Onboarding**.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Companies**. Select **Create** for a new legal entity, or open an existing company to update fields that are editable.
2. For a new company, choose the **Business Partner** before saving. The value help offers Organization partners that are not already linked to a company.
3. Review the draft defaults and replace placeholder identity values. Complete the required company identity and setup fields, including **Company Code**, **Tenant Code**, **Legal Name**, **Registration Number**, **Home Jurisdiction**, **Base Currency**, fiscal-year dates, **Operating Model**, **Environment** and **Status**.
4. Complete the regulatory, financial identity, operating model and settlement details that apply to the company.
5. Save the company record. Add the related regulatory, risk and governance records on the saved company as needed.

## How do I know it worked?

The company appears in the list with its code, legal name, environment and **Onboarding** status (for a new record). Open it to verify the linked business partner and the saved identity details.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the **Business Partner** value help is empty, create the Organization partner first or check whether it is already linked to another company.
- If saving fails, read the validation message for the required field, complete it and save again.
- Company records and their supporting rows cannot be deleted. Correct permitted details or update the lifecycle state through the approved process instead.
