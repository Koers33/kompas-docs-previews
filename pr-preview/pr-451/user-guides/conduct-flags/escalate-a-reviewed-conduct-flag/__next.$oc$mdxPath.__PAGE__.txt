# Escalate a reviewed conduct flag

Escalate a conduct concern that has already been reviewed and record the reason it needs further attention.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to use **Escalate**. Follow your team’s conduct-escalation procedure before submitting the note.

## What do I need before I start?

- A conduct flag with **REVIEWED** status. An **OPEN** flag must first be reviewed.
- The linked deal or exit request and a concise **Escalation Note** explaining why further attention is needed.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Conduct Flags** and filter **Status** to **REVIEWED**.
2. Open the flag and confirm its reason, description and linked deal or exit request.
3. Select **Escalate** and enter the required **Escalation Note**.
4. Submit the action, refresh the record and confirm **Status** is **ESCALATED** and the note is present.

## How do I know it worked?

The flag changes from **REVIEWED** to **ESCALATED**. The record retains its review details and the escalation note. The status cannot be moved back in this app.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Escalate** is unavailable, confirm the flag is **REVIEWED**; escalation is not offered for an **OPEN** flag.
- If the action asks for an escalation note, enter it before submitting.
- If the action is refused, check your Treasury access and the current flag status.

## What should I do next?

Follow your team’s conduct process to address the underlying concern in the linked deal or exit request.
