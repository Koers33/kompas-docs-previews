# Decide on an accounting reversal request

Review a pending reversal request and either approve it, which creates a linked reversal journal entry, or reject it, which leaves the original journal unchanged.

## Who can do this?

You need access to the Accounting Reversal Requests app and the Treasury role. You must be a different user from the requester; the same person cannot approve or reject their own request.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved request with **Pending** status.
- The **Reversal Type**, **Original Journal** and **Reason** to review.
- The accounting approval needed to decide whether the correction should proceed.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Accounting Reversal Requests** and filter **Status** to **Pending**.
2. Open the request. Review its reversal type, reason and **Original Journal** before deciding.
3. Follow your accounting approval procedure. Select **Approve** to authorize the correction, or **Reject** to decline the request.
4. Refresh or reopen the request and check the result:
   - After approval, the request reaches **Completed** and the **Approval** section records the approver and time.
   - After rejection, the request reaches **Rejected** and the **Approval** section records the decision-maker and time.

## How do I know it worked?

For an approved request, open Journal Entries and verify that a new journal entry is linked to the original and reverses its debit/credit directions. The request shows **Completed**. For a rejected request, the request shows **Rejected** and no reversal journal entry is created; the original entry remains unchanged.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Approve** and **Reject** are unavailable, confirm that the request is saved and still **Pending**.
- If the app says a different user must decide, you are the requester. Ask another Treasury user to review it; do not try to work around the two-person rule.
- If approval fails because the original journal cannot be reversed, confirm that the selected journal exists and has postings. Contact accounting support before retrying, and check the request status first.

## What should I do next?

After approval, compare the reversal entry with the original in [Journal Entries](/user-guides/journal-entries) and follow your accounting review procedure.
