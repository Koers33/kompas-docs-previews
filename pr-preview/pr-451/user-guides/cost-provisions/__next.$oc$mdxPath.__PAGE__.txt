# Cost Provisions app

Cost Provisions is the read-only record of the cost provisions the platform raises as it carries out configured actions. Each row is one provision as it was recorded: the classification and value it took from that action's rate, when it happened, and who was signed in.

## What is this app for?

An entry holds the **Cost Provision Classification** and **Cost Provision Value** that applied at that moment, the **Provision Date/Time**, the **Username** of the person who was signed in, and the **Button Code** of the action behind it. The classification and value are copied from the action's row in the rate register, so an entry is a snapshot of what was configured at the time the provision was raised.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to check what was provisioned for an action and when, or to compare a provision against the rate you expected. To change a rate itself, use the [Cost Provision Rates app](/user-guides/cost-provision-rates) — this app only records what happened.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The register applies no role restriction of its own, so everyone who can open it sees the same rows. No role can turn it into an editing screen: the app offers no create, edit or delete for anyone.

## How does work progress in this app?

There is nothing to progress. An entry appears when the platform raises it, you read it, and any follow-up — correcting a rate, investigating the action behind a provision — happens in the app that owns that work.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Read-only everywhere:** an empty action toolbar is normal here, not a permissions problem.
- **Entries are snapshots:** the classification and value stay as they were when the provision was raised, so they can differ from the rate register's current values.
- **An empty list:** entries are raised by the platform, so no rows means none has been recorded yet — they cannot be added by hand in this app.

## Where can I review the results?

Open an entry and read the **General** section: classification, value, provision date/time, username and button code. **Button Code** is the link back to the action — match it against the row in the [Cost Provision Rates app](/user-guides/cost-provision-rates) to see the rate that produced the entry.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- You want to change a classification or a value: entries cannot be edited here. Change the rate in the [Cost Provision Rates app](/user-guides/cost-provision-rates), then review the entries that follow.
- An entry shows a different value from today's rate: check when the entry was raised — it keeps the values copied at that time.
- The app itself does not open: check your access first — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

- [Review a cost provision entry and its source rate](./review-a-cost-provision-entry-and-its-source-rate)
