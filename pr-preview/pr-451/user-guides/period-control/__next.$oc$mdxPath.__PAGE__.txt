# Period Control app

Period Control is where an accounting period for a company and a ledger is closed and locked. Each period shows its **Period Name**, **Company**, **Ledger Code**, **Status** (**Open**, **Closed** or **Locked**), the period's **Total Debits** and **Total Credits**, a **Can Close** indicator, and — once it has been acted on — who closed and locked it and when.

## What is this app for?

A period is the window that decides whether accounting can still be posted for a company and ledger. While a period is **Open**, postings for it are accepted; **Closed** stops new postings but can be undone; **Locked** is final. The app exists so that the close is deliberate: a checklist is evaluated before the period is closed, closing records who did it and when, and locking is a separate, two-person step afterwards.

The app also carries the **Period Close Checklist** for each period, which records the conditions the platform checks before a close is allowed.

## When should I use this app?

Use it during the period-end routine: evaluate the checklist, close the period once everything passes, then have a second person lock it. Use it before assuming a period is safe to post to — a closed or locked period is exactly why a posting or a correction is refused elsewhere.

Periods are not created or deleted here. If you need to correct an entry that is already posted, raise it in the [Accounting Reversal Requests app](/user-guides/accounting-reversal-requests).

## Who can access this app?

Period and checklist records are granted to the Treasury role, and the app itself is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The close and lock steps carry a two-person rule: the person who closed a period cannot lock it.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Start with a period whose **Status** is **Open** and select the checklist evaluation. It is offered on any period that is not yet **Locked**, and it refreshes the period's **Period Close Checklist** with the result of each condition and when it was evaluated.
2. When every condition passes — **All Gates Green** — close the period. Closing is only offered while the period is **Open**, and it is refused unless the checklist passes and the period's debits equal its credits. On success the status becomes **Closed**, and **Closed At**, **Closed By**, **Total Debits** and **Total Credits** are recorded.
3. Reopen if the close was premature or something still has to be posted. Reopening is offered only from **Closed** and returns the period to **Open**.
4. Lock once the period is final. Locking is offered only from **Closed**, and a different user must do it: the platform refuses the same person locking their own close.
5. After that the period is **Locked** and stays that way.

Treat this as orientation; follow your accounting close procedure.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **The status decides the buttons:** close appears only on an **Open** period, reopen and lock only on a **Closed** one, the checklist evaluation on anything but **Locked**, and a **Locked** period offers nothing.
- **The buttons carry their technical names:** **evaluateChecklist**, **closePeriod**, **reopenPeriod** and **lockPeriod** do exactly what their names say.
- **Every checklist condition must pass:** the checklist lists **TUJ Entries Balanced**, **Hash Chain Intact**, **GL Export Exists**, **No Nostro Breaks**, **All Deals Confirmed** and **MTM Valuations Current**, with **All Gates Green** as the summary. A failing condition is named in the message that blocks the close.
- **Locked is permanent:** a locked period cannot be reopened, and the status itself only moves along the transitions the platform allows.
- **Read-only fields:** **Total Debits**, **Total Credits**, **Can Close** and the closing and locking timestamps are maintained by the platform, not typed in.

## Where can I review the results?

The list answers "is this period closed?" at a glance: status, the period totals and the close and lock timestamps are all visible without opening a record. Open a period for **Accounting Period** (the dates, ledger and status), **Period Close Checklist** (each condition and when it was last evaluated) and **Closing / Locking** (who closed and locked it, and when).

The postings a period governs are reviewed in the [Journal Entries app](/user-guides/journal-entries), where each entry names the ledger it posted to and when it was processed.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- **Closing is refused because a checklist condition failed:** the message names the condition; re-run the evaluation after the underlying issue is resolved.
- **Closing is refused because the period is unbalanced:** total debits and total credits differ — compare them in the [Journal Entries app](/user-guides/journal-entries) before retrying.
- **Locking is refused as a segregation-of-duties violation:** you closed this period; a different user has to lock it.
- **Reopening is refused:** the period is already **Locked**, which is final, or it is not **Closed**.
- **A close or lock is refused:** read the message to identify which checklist item or approval condition is incomplete.
