# Add or update a general ledger account

Create or correct a company's chart-of-accounts record before it is used by accounting mappings and postings.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. It has no additional service-level role restriction and does not provide an approve/reject action.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Company**, **GL Code** and **Account Name**.
- The account's **Account Type**, **Account Group**, **Normal Balance** and **Currency**.
- The required regulatory classifications, including **Cash Classification**, **HQLA Level**, **BA700 Line**, book type, tax treatment and OCI recycling rule, as applicable.
- Existing approval and business partner records if your procedure requires **Approved By** and **Approval Reference**.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **General Ledger Accounts**. Select **Create** for a new account, or open the existing account to correct it.
2. In **Account Definition**, set the company, GL code, account name and account behavior fields, including whether it is **Management Ledger Only**.
3. In **Regulatory Classification**, choose the applicable reporting and regulatory values from the value helps.
4. Complete **Approval** details if your procedure requires them. These fields select existing records; they do not run an approval workflow.
5. Save the draft and activate it using the app's normal save flow. Reopen the account and verify the saved classifications.

## How do I know it worked?

The account appears in the list with its company, GL code, name, account type, normal balance, IAS 7 class, HQLA level, BA700 line, management-only flag and active state. The object page shows the full account and regulatory details.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If a company or classification is missing from a value help, confirm the corresponding company or platform list value has been configured.
- If the expected approval record or business partner is not available, create or correct that record in its owning app first.
- If the save fails, check required account details and the user-readable message before retrying.
- Accounts cannot be deleted. To stop using an account, follow [Retire a general ledger account](./retire-a-general-ledger-account).
