# Suspend or reactivate a nostro account

Temporarily stop a nostro account from being selected for new deals, or restore a suspended account after the issue is resolved.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow your company's bank-account and settlement change-control procedure.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company, **Nostro Code** and **Currency** of the account.
- For suspension, confirmation that the account should no longer be used for new deals.
- For reactivation, a **Suspended** account and confirmation that it is suitable for settlement again.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Nostro Accounts** and find the account by company, code or currency.
2. Review its **Status**, settlement system and correspondent details.
3. Select **Suspend** to temporarily stop new use, or **Reactivate** to return a suspended account to **Active**.
4. Reopen or refresh the record and confirm the **Status** indicator reflects the result.

## How do I know it worked?

The account shows **Suspended** and is not used for new deals, or shows **Active** and is available to settlement processing. The banking details remain unchanged by either action.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the account is already **Suspended**, there is no need to suspend it again.
- If a **Closed** account appears, treat it as permanently closed and confirm the approved next step with the account owner before using **Reactivate**.
- If the action is missing, check the current status and your assigned roles.

## What should I do next?

If the account remains suspended or closed, update the relevant account setup and settlement procedures.
