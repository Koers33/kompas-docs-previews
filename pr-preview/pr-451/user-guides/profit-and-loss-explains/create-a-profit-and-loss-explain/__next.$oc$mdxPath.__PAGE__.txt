# Create a profit and loss explain

Generate a dated explanation record for a deal and review its system-calculated profit-and-loss components and unexplained amount.

## Who can do this?

The **Treasury** role is required to read and create explain records.

## What do I need before I start?

- The existing **Deal** to explain.
- The **Explain Date** for the explanation record.
- The deal owner or accounting contact available if the generated values require investigation.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Profit & Loss Explains** and select **Create**.
2. Choose the **Deal** and enter the **Explain Date**.
3. Save and activate the draft using the app's normal save flow. The platform populates the component values and summary when the record is created.
4. Review **P&L Components** and **Summary**, including **Total Profit & Loss**, **Unexplained Profit & Loss** and **Threshold Breached**.

## How do I know it worked?

The explain appears in the list for the selected deal and date. Its component and summary fields are populated by the platform and read-only; the threshold flag reflects the result recorded at creation.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the deal is missing, confirm that it exists in [Deals](/user-guides/deals) and that you have Treasury access.
- If the deal or component fields are read-only, that is expected; only the deal and explain date are selected for a new explain.
- If the generated breakdown appears incomplete or unexpected, do not edit the component values. Contact the deal or accounting owner and review the source deal and related journal entries.
- A saved explain cannot be deleted, so confirm the deal and date before creating it.

## What should I do next?
