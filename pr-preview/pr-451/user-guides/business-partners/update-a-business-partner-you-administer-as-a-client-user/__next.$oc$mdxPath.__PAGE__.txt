# Update a business partner you administer as a client user

Update the editable profile of a linked business partner when you hold an active **Client Admin** relationship to it.

## Who can do this?

You must be signed in with the **Client** role and have an active **Client Admin** relationship to the partner you want to update. A **Partner Manager** relationship allows read access, not updates. You cannot edit your own profile; Treasury manages your identity fields.

## What do I need before I start?

- The Business Partners app and your client account.
- An active **Client Admin** relationship to the organization you need to update. The partner must appear as linked to your account.
- The corrected information and any supporting details needed for the fields you are allowed to maintain.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Business Partners** and find the linked organization you need to maintain.
2. Open its record and select **Edit**.
3. Update the profile fields that are editable for your account. Treasury-managed client and trading configuration fields are not available to client users.
4. Review your changes and select **Save**.
5. Reopen the record and verify that the changes appear in the relevant section.

## How do I know it worked?

The partner record displays the values you saved. If you changed a field in a related section, confirm the updated row appears under that section on the partner record.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the partner is not visible, ask your administrator to verify that your client account is linked to it by an active **Client Admin** relationship.
- If the record is visible but **Edit** is unavailable, check that you have **Client Admin** rather than **Partner Manager** access. Your own profile is managed by Treasury and cannot be edited by you.
- If a client or trading configuration field is hidden, that field is Treasury-managed. Contact your Treasury administrator rather than trying to change your role.

## What should I do next?
