# Review a cash account balance and reconciliation status

Find the balance for a company’s nostro account and value period, then see whether reconciliation has matched the book balance to an external statement.

## Who can do this?

Users who can open the Cash Account Balances app can review its records. The app is read-only for everyone; it has no create, edit or delete actions.

## What do I need before I start?

- The company and **Nostro Account** you want to review.
- The **Currency** and **Value Period** to inspect. You can also filter by **Reconciliation Status**.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Cash Account Balances**.
2. Filter by **Company**, **Nostro Account**, **Currency** and **Value Period**. Add **Reconciliation Status** if you are looking for a particular outcome.
3. Open the matching row.
4. In **General Information**, check the reconciliation result and the **Last Settled At**, **Last Movement At** and **Last Reconciled At** values.
5. In **Balances**, review the **Opening Balance**, **Closing Balance**, **Confirmed Settled Balance**, **Total Inflows** and **Total Outflows** for the period.

## How do I know it worked?

You have the balance record for the selected company, account, currency and period. **Matched** means the book balance agrees with the external statement. **Pending** means reconciliation has not run yet; a **Break** or variance indicates a difference that needs follow-up.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the list is empty, clear the filters and check whether a balance record exists for that period.
- If the status is **Pending**, **Break**, **Book SAMOS Variance**, **Book CBS Variance** or **Full Variance**, investigate through the cash and reconciliation process. This app does not correct balances.
- If the app does not open, ask your administrator to check your portal access.
