# Add, correct or retire an engine record

Register or update the catalogue description of a processing engine, or mark an engine as retired while keeping its record.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and has no additional role restriction. Follow your platform's configuration and release-governance process when changing the shared engine register.

## What do I need before I start?

- The **Engine Code**, **Engine Name** and **Purpose Summary**.
- The **Engine Group**, **Sprint Introduced**, **Pipeline**, **Effect Domain** and **Implementation Status** from their configured lists.
- The apps, buttons and scheduled jobs that trigger the engine, plus its dependencies, blockers, parameters and specification/source references.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **System Engine Catalogue** and search by engine code, group or implementation status.
2. Select **Create** for a new record, or open the existing engine to update its description, purpose, triggers or dependencies.
3. Set the implementation status to **Planned**, **In Progress**, **Implemented**, **Deprecated** or **Deactivated**, as appropriate.
4. Save and activate the draft. To retire an engine, set its status to **Deprecated** or **Deactivated** rather than deleting it.
5. Reopen the engine and verify its general information and dependencies.

## How do I know it worked?

The engine appears with its code, name, group, sprint and implementation status. Its purpose, triggers and dependencies describe the approved implementation.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If save reports a missing required field, complete **Engine Code**, **Engine Name**, **Purpose Summary** and **Implementation Status**.
- If a group, sprint, effect domain or status is not offered, that platform list is read-only here; contact its owner.
- If an engine should no longer run, change its implementation status rather than deleting its history.
