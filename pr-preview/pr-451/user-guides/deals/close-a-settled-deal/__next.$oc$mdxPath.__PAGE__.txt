# Close a settled deal

Close a deal after settlement is complete, recording the final lifecycle transition and deactivating its cash state.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users with access to the settled deal can use **Close Deal**, subject to record-level permissions and the company’s close procedure.

## What do I need before I start?

- A deal in **Settled** status.
- Confirmation that the settlement and cashflow work is complete.
- A transition reason if required by your accounting procedure.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the settled deal and review its settlement and cashflow results.
2. Select **Close Deal**.
3. Enter a reason if prompted and submit the action.
4. Refresh the deal and confirm its lifecycle history records the closure.

## How do I know it worked?

The deal’s lifecycle status changes from **Settled** to **Closed**. The platform records the close transition and deactivates the deal’s cash state.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Close Deal** is unavailable, confirm the deal is **Settled**; closing is not offered for other statuses.
- If the action is refused, resolve the message shown by the app and verify settlement is complete before retrying.

## What should I do next?

Review the deal’s lifecycle history and accounting records.
