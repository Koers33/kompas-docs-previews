# Mature a confirmed deal

Record that a confirmed deal has reached contractual maturity and trigger the configured maturity processing.

## Who can do this?

Treasury users with access to the confirmed deal can use **Mature Deal**, subject to record-level permissions and the company’s procedures.

## What do I need before I start?

- A deal in **Confirmed** status whose contractual maturity has been reached.
- The reason to record for the lifecycle transition, if your procedure requires one.
- Confirmation that any expected settlement processing has been completed through the back-office process.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open the confirmed deal and verify its maturity date and processing history.
2. Select **Mature Deal**.
3. Enter a transition reason if prompted, then submit.
4. Refresh the deal and review its lifecycle history and evidence.

## How do I know it worked?

The lifecycle status changes from **Confirmed** to **Matured**. The platform records the transition and triggers the maturity effects configured for that company and instrument.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If **Mature Deal** is unavailable, confirm the deal is still **Confirmed** and that your access permits the action.
- If the action is refused, resolve the message shown by the app and confirm the deal’s current lifecycle status before retrying.
