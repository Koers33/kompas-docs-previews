# Link or unlink a currency pair on a market

Choose which registered currency pairs a market trades, or switch off a pair that should no longer be linked.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the **Treasury** role to maintain markets and their symbol links.

## What do I need before I start?

- A saved market record.
- A currency pair already registered in [Forex Symbols](/user-guides/forex-symbols).
- Confirmation that the market should trade the pair or that the link should be retired.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Forex Markets** and select the market.
2. In **Traded Symbols**, select **Create** to link a pair, or open the existing pair link to change it.
3. For a new link, choose the registered symbol. The base and quote currencies are shown from the symbol record.
4. Save the link with it active. To stop trading the pair on this market, switch its active flag off and save; links are retained rather than deleted.

## How do I know it worked?

The market’s **Traded Symbols** table shows the pair and its active state. Only one link for a given pair is allowed on a market.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the pair is not offered, register it first in Forex Symbols.
- If a duplicate-link message appears, use the existing row and correct its active state rather than adding another.
- If the trading partner is missing from the market, verify its active **Bank** relationship.
