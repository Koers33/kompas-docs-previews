# Create or update a forex market

Register a forex market or correct the market name, type or trading partner used by its symbol links.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the **Treasury** role to work with market records.

## What do I need before I start?

- The market name and **Market Type**.
- A trading partner that has an active **Bank** relationship; only those partners appear in the value help.
- The market’s base/quote currency pairs if you will also link symbols after saving.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Forex Markets**. Select **Create** for a new market, or open an existing market to correct it.
2. Enter or update **Market Name**, select **Market Type**, and choose the **Trading Partner**.
3. Save the market record.
4. If you changed an existing market, review its **Traded Symbols** table and confirm its linked pairs are still appropriate.

## How do I know it worked?

The market shows the saved name, type and trading partner. Its **Traded Symbols** section lists the currency pairs currently linked to it.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If the expected trading partner is missing, check that it has an active **Bank** relationship in Business Partners.
- If you receive a duplicate-pair message, open the existing link for that symbol rather than creating another.
- If the app or editing controls are unavailable, check your Treasury role and portal access.
