# Activate or deactivate a readiness gate

Switch a readiness gate on or off while preserving its record and audit history.

## Who can do this?

The app requires portal access and the roles assigned by your administrator. Follow the platform's approval process before changing a shared gate's active state.

## What do I need before I start?

- The gate and the reason for changing its **Is Active** state.
- A change reason of at least 10 characters.
- To activate a gate, at least one check must already exist. Checks are maintained through platform setup, not in this app.
- To deactivate a gate, identify any active gates that hard-depend on it; those must be addressed first.

## How do I complete the task?

1. Open **Gate Catalogue**, find the gate and review its **Checks** and **Depends On** sections.
2. Select **Edit** and switch **Is Active** on or off as approved.
3. Enter the **Change Reason** with at least 10 characters.
4. Save and activate the draft through the app's normal draft-save flow.
5. Reopen the gate and check **Is Active** and the corresponding **Mapping History** entry.

## How do I know it worked?

The gate shows the intended active state, and **Mapping History** records the old and new active values with the reason. Deactivation retains the gate; it is not deleted.

## What if I can’t complete it?

- If activation is refused because no check exists, ask the platform setup owner to supply at least one check before retrying.
- If deactivation is refused because active gates depend on this gate, review the named dependents and resolve their dependency before trying again.
- If the reason is missing or too short, enter at least 10 characters.
- If you tried to delete the gate, use **Is Active** to retire it instead; gate deletion is not allowed.
