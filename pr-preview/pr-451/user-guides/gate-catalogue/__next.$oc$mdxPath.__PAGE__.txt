# Gate Catalogue app

Gate Catalogue is the register of the platform's readiness gates — the requirements a company must meet before it may trade. One row per gate, identified by its **Readiness Gate Code** and **Name**, and each row carries its **Readiness Gate Series**, **Readiness Gate Type**, **Business Domain** and **Accountable Owner Role**.

## What is this app for?

Every gate states a requirement, the rule behind it, how it is enforced (by the system, by a person, or by both), its basis, who owns it, and — in the **In Plain Words** section — what it checks, why it matters, what to do and what ready and not-ready look like. The register is shared by every company: which gates apply to a particular company follows that company's institution type, resolved by the platform rather than chosen here.

Beneath each gate sit its **Checks** (the individual tests that make up the gate), **Evaluated For** (what the gate is judged on), **Depends On** (which other gates must be green first), **Applies To** (the institution types it covers) and **Mapping History** (the record of past changes).

## When should I use this app?

Use it to read and review gate definitions, to confirm or correct a gate's **Business Domain**, **Readiness Gate Type** or **Accountable Owner Role**, to switch a gate active or inactive, to improve its plain-words wording, and to audit how a gate changed over time. Assessing a company or recording whether a check passed is onboarding work, not register maintenance — this app keeps the definitions those flows read.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The register carries no additional role restriction in the app's own metadata, which also records no narrower launchpad audience.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Find the gate — filter the list by series, type, domain, owner or active flag, then open the record.
2. Read it in the section order the page gives you: **Readiness Gate Identity**, **Readiness Gate Rule**, **Readiness Gate Behaviour**, **Readiness Gate Basis**, **Readiness Gate Ownership**, **In Plain Words** and **Readiness Gate Audit**.
3. To change one of the four mapping values — **Readiness Gate Type**, **Business Domain**, **Accountable Owner Role** or **Is Active** — enter a reason for the change alongside them. The reason must be at least ten characters and is required whenever one of those values changes; wording edits do not need one.
4. Save. Each mapping value that actually changed is written to **Mapping History** with its old value, new value and your reason in the same save, and the reason then clears from the gate. If the history row cannot be written, the whole change is rolled back and nothing is saved.
5. Retire a gate by switching **Is Active** off rather than deleting it — a delete attempt is refused with a message telling you to deactivate instead.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Active state:** a new gate starts inactive, and a gate can only be activated once it has at least one check. A gate cannot be switched off while another active gate hard-depends on it — the refusal names the gates that still need it.
- **Proposed values:** **Business Domain** and **Accountable Owner Role** can carry a proposed value until you save the field to confirm it, which is what the proposed flags in the identity and ownership sections report.
- **Owner must be live:** the **Accountable Owner Role** has to be an active role; an inactive one is refused with a message naming it.
- **Fixed content:** gate code, name, series, sequence, rule text and the behaviour and basis columns are fixed after creation — they are shown read-only and refused if sent back. Gate codes are two letters plus two digits, and the letters must match the series (for example SG01 for a setup gate); the sequence number is assigned by the platform.
- **Checks:** existing check rows are read-only except for their plain wording; the register does not create or delete checks from this screen.
- **Development-only gates:** a gate flagged development-only can only change while the platform runs in development mode, and no new gate can be created in a production system — register rows arrive with the platform's setup.
- **Wording review:** **Wording Confirmed** records whether a gate's plain wording has been reviewed, and the list can be filtered on it.

## Where can I review the results?

**Mapping History** is the audit trail of your maintenance: newest first, showing when and by whom the gate changed, which field changed, the old and new values and the reason given. Its rows store the changed field and the values as short codes (for example DOMAIN or OWNER) rather than display names. **Readiness Gate Audit** carries the creation and change stamps of the record itself, **Checks** shows each test with its plain wording, and **In Plain Words** shows how the gate is explained to users. Downstream, the applicability a company sees comes from its institution type — see the [Companies app](/user-guides/companies) for where that is set.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- A message that a reason is required or too short: mapping changes need a reason of at least ten characters; wording edits do not.
- A message that a field cannot be changed here: that field is fixed after creation — adjust one of the editable mapping values instead.
- A message about the gate code or an existing code: codes follow the two-letter, two-digit pattern of their series and must be unique.
- A message about an inactive owner role or an active gate that depends on this one: fix the role, or deactivate the dependent gates first.
