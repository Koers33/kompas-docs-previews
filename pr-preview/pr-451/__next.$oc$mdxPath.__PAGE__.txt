# Kompas Platform

Kompas is a treasury and trade management platform for banks and corporate treasuries, delivered as a set of SAP Fiori apps on SAP Business Technology Platform. It manages the full life of a treasury transaction: setting up business partners, counterparties, and clients; capturing deals across instrument types such as FX spot, FX forwards, money markets, term loans, and deposits; confirming, amending, and rolling them over; producing tamper-evident accounting journals; and giving risk, compliance, finance, and audit teams the views and controls they need — all behind a single sign-on with role-based access and built-in segregation-of-duties checks.

## What are the two demo companies?

The platform is seeded with two demonstration companies that show its two operating modes. Use them to explore what a bank treasury can do versus a standalone treasury.

| Feature | MutualBank ZA (MBZ) | Treasury ABC (TABC) |
|---|---|---|
| Entity type | BANKING_AND_TREASURY | TREASURY_ONLY |
| Trading mode | HYBRID (own + client book) | OWN (own book only) |
| Acting capacity | OWN, ON_BEHALF, HYBRID | OWN only |
| Deposit direction | PLACED and RECEIVED | PLACED only |
| Loan direction | LOAN_OUT and LOAN_IN | LOAN_OUT only |
| Client FX | Yes (ON_BEHALF deals) | No |
| Nostro accounts | 4 (ZAR, USD, EUR, GBP) | 3 (ZAR, USD, EUR) |

MBZ acts as a bank that deals for itself and on behalf of clients; TABC acts as a pure corporate treasury that only deals for its own book.
