Glossary

Glossary

TermDefinition
AccrualRecording income or expense in the period it is earned or incurred, regardless of when cash actually moves.
Acting CapacityThe role in which a bank executes a deal: on its own book, on behalf of a client, or in a hybrid combination of both.
All-In RateThe complete exchange rate a client or book actually receives, combining the spot rate, forward points, and any spread or margin applied.
AML (Anti-Money Laundering)Laws and controls designed to prevent criminals from disguising illegally obtained funds as legitimate income.
Approval PoolThe centralised worklist where authorised users approve or reject actions waiting on a governance gate.
Audit LogA secure record of who did what in the system and when, designed to be tamper-evident and available to auditors and examiners.
BA700The South African regulatory return on banks assets, liabilities, and income, filed monthly with the regulator.
Basel IIIThe international framework strengthening bank capital, liquidity, and leverage requirements after the 2008 financial crisis.
Basis PointOne hundredth of one percent, the standard unit for quoting small changes in interest rates, spreads, and fee percentages.
BLACKLISTED StatusThe highest counterparty restriction status, permanently blocking all deal activity with that business partner.
Cashflow ItemA projected or actual dated money movement generated from a deal, such as interest, principal, or exchange legs.
Cashflow MatchThe process of linking an actual bank statement movement to the expected cashflow item it settles.
Company Onboarding GateThe controlled checklist a company must pass before it can transact live on the platform.
Conduct FlagA marker raised on a partner or dealer when behaviour breaches conduct standards, prompting review before normal activity continues.
Counterparty RiskThe danger that the other side in a transaction fails to meet its obligations, causing a loss of the amount owed.
Credit Valuation AdjustmentThe fair-value adjustment for the risk that a derivative counterparty defaults before the contract matures.
Deal ConfirmationThe controlled second-person approval that turns a captured draft deal into a live, binding position on the platform.
Domain groupThe subject area a list belongs to, for example ONBOARDING
Draft DocumentA work-in-progress record that is saved continuously as the user fills a form, visible only to its creator until activation.
DrawdownThe act of taking funds from an approved credit or deposit facility, converting a committed limit into an actual outstanding balance.
DSAR (Data Subject Access Request)A request by an individual to access, correct, or delete the personal data an organisation holds about them.
DV01The change in a position value for a one-basis-point move in interest rates, a linear measure of rate sensitivity.
ECP (Earning Credit Potential)A regulatory add-on to counterparty credit exposure that reflects the potential future cost of replacing an FX contract if the counterparty defaults.
Effect ChainThe ordered sequence of downstream consequences a single deal event produces across the platform engines.
Effective Interest RateThe rate that exactly discounts a financial instrument expected cashflows to its carrying amount, spreading interest income or cost evenly over the economic life.
EMIRThe European Market Infrastructure Regulation requiring OTC derivative reporting, clearing, and risk-mitigation controls.
ENG_ACC (Accounting Engine)The pipeline step that resolves financial nodes, applies GL mapping, and posts journal entries for a deal event.
ENG_CASH (Cash Engine)The pipeline step that projects cashflow items and updates cash positions and nostro balances for a deal event.
ENG_EVD (Evidence Engine)The pipeline step that assembles and seals the evidence pack for a deal event.
ENG_LMT (Limit Engine)The pipeline step that checks deal exposure against dealer authority and counterparty credit lines.
ENG_RISK (Risk Engine)The pipeline step that computes exposure, market-risk metrics, and limit reservation for a deal event.
ENG_SAN (Sanitisation Engine)The first pipeline step that normalises and validates incoming deal data before any business processing runs.
Exchange ControlGovernment restrictions on the movement of currency across borders, still partially applicable in South Africa for residents.
Expected Credit LossThe probability-weighted estimate of credit losses a lender expects over the life of a financial instrument, recognised before any default actually occurs.
ExposureThe amount of credit or market risk currently at stake with a counterparty or on a position.
FATFThe Financial Action Task Force, the global body setting anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing standards.
FICAThe Financial Intelligence Centre Act, South Africa law requiring accountable institutions to verify clients, keep records, and report suspicious activity.
Financial NodeA named standard point in the accounting model where economic events attach, such as deal confirmation, accrual, or settlement, each with a defined debit and credit account pair.
FinSurvThe Financial Surveillance department of the South African Reserve Bank that administers exchange-control regulations and approvals.
Forward Exchange ContractAn agreement to exchange two currencies at a fixed rate on a future date, locking in the rate today to remove uncertainty about the future exchange rate.
Forward PointsThe difference between the forward exchange rate and the spot rate for a currency pair, expressed in pips and driven by the interest-rate differential between the two currencies.
FSCAThe Financial Sector Conduct Authority, the South African regulator overseeing market conduct and the treatment of customers by financial institutions.
FX ForwardA bilateral agreement to buy or sell a currency at a predetermined rate on a set future date, used to hedge or speculate on currency movements.
FX SpotA foreign exchange trade exchanging two currencies for settlement typically two business days after the trade date at the current market rate.
GateA requirement a company must meet before it may trade
Gate seriesOne of the five phases a gate belongs to: setup, instrument, operational, readiness or conditional.
General LedgerThe master set of accounts through which all financial transactions of an entity are recorded and from which financial statements are produced.
General Ledger MappingThe configuration that decides which general ledger account receives each type of accounting event from each instrument.
Hash ChainA sequence of records where each entry stores a cryptographic hash of the previous one, so altering any past record breaks every link after it.
Hedge AccountingAn optional IFRS treatment that aligns the accounting for a hedging instrument with the item it protects, so gains and losses offset in the same period.
Herstatt RiskThe risk that one leg of an FX trade settles while the counterparty fails before the second leg settles, named after a 1974 German bank collapse.
HQLAHigh-quality liquid assets: cash and securities that can be converted to cash quickly with minimal loss in a stress scenario.
IFRS 9The international standard governing how financial instruments are classified, measured, provisioned for expected losses, and hedged.
Immutable LedgerAn append-only accounting record that can never be edited or deleted once written, with corrections made only by new offsetting entries.
Instrument EligibilityThe configuration that decides which instruments a company, desk, or dealer is permitted to trade.
ISO 20022The global standard for electronic data interchange between financial institutions, defining the XML and structured-data messages payments use.
JIBARThe Johannesburg Interbank Amortised Rate, the benchmark interest rate at which South African banks lend to each other, published daily for standard tenors.
Journal EntryA dated accounting record of a financial event, showing at least one debit and one matching credit to general ledger accounts.
KYC (Know Your Customer)The process of verifying a client identity and assessing its risk before doing business, refreshed periodically.
LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio)The ratio of high-quality liquid assets to net cash outflows under a 30-day stress scenario, a Basel III minimum standard.
Legal HoldA directive that freezes records related to actual or anticipated litigation or investigation, prohibiting their alteration or deletion.
Lifecycle StatusThe controlled state of a deal from draft through confirmed, settled, and matured, governing which actions are legal at each point.
Limit BreachA transaction or market move that takes exposure above an approved limit, requiring either prevention or documented override.
Limit UtilisationThe percentage of an approved credit or dealing limit currently consumed by live exposure.
Liquidity LadderA time-bucketed view of expected cash inflows and outflows showing how liquid the book is over days, weeks, and months.
Loan-to-ValueThe ratio of a loan amount to the value of the asset securing it, used to judge credit risk and set conditions.
Mark-to-MarketValuing an open position at the price it could be settled at today, replacing its original booked value with current market value.
Market RiskThe risk of loss from movements in exchange rates, interest rates, or other market prices while a position is open.
Maturity DateThe date on which a financial contract ends and principal plus any final interest must be repaid or settled.
Money MarketThe market for short-term borrowing and lending, typically overnight to one year, where banks and corporates manage daily funding and liquidity.
Mutual Banks ActThe South African statute governing mutual banks - deposit-taking institutions owned by and lending to their members.
NCAThe National Credit Act, South African law governing consumer credit agreements, affordability assessments, and debt-restructuring conduct.
Nostro AccountA bank account a institution holds with a foreign bank, denominated in that foreign currency, used to settle cross-border payments.
Nostro ReconciliationThe daily comparison of the expected nostro position of a bank with the actual balance reported by the correspondent bank, resolving differences.
Notional AmountThe face value of a derivative or funding contract on which payments are calculated, which is usually never exchanged between the parties.
P&L AttributionThe allocation of profit and loss to the desk, book, instrument, or client activity that generated it.
Period CloseThe controlled process of finalising all accounting for a period, after which no further entries may post to it.
Pipeline EngineThe ordered set of processing engines that evaluate, account for, settle, and evidence every deal event on the platform.
POPIAThe Protection of Personal Information Act, South Africa data-protection law governing how personal information may be collected, processed, and retained.
PostingThe act of recording a journal entry into the general ledger so it affects account balances and financial reports.
Repo RateThe rate at which the South African Reserve Bank lends to commercial banks against collateral, the primary policy anchor for all rand interest rates.
Revolving CreditA pre-approved credit facility a borrower can draw, repay, and redraw repeatedly during its life without new approval each time.
RolloverExtending the settlement date of an existing FX or money-market position by closing the near leg and opening a new forward leg at updated rates.
SAMOSThe South African Multiple Option Settlement system, the real-time gross settlement system operated by the Reserve Bank for high-value rand payments.
Sanctions ScreeningChecking clients and counterparties against government and international sanctions lists before allowing transactions.
SARBThe South African Reserve Bank, the central bank responsible for monetary policy, currency issuance, and stability of the financial system.
Segregation of DutiesA control requiring that no single person performs two conflicting steps of a process, such as capturing and confirming the same deal.
Settlement InstructionThe authorised direction to move funds for a deal, carrying amount, currency, accounts, and value date for execution.
SOFRThe Secured Overnight Financing Rate, the US benchmark for short-term dollar funding, replacing LIBOR for most USD contracts.
STR (Suspicious Transaction Report)A confidential report filed with the Financial Intelligence Centre when a transaction appears to involve proceeds of crime or unusual activity.
SWIFTThe Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication, the messaging network banks use to send payment and trade instructions securely.
TenorThe length of time until a financial contract matures or a rate reprices, quoted in days, months, or years.
Term LoanA loan for a fixed amount over a fixed period, usually with scheduled interest payments and repayment at or before maturity.
Time BucketA standardised date range such as overnight or 30-90 days into which cashflows and exposures are grouped for analysis.
Trader Limit MatrixThe grid of dealing authorities per dealer and instrument type defining the maximum notional or exposure each may transact.
Treasury DepositFunds placed by one party with another for an agreed term and rate, generating interest income and a return of principal at maturity.
TUJ EntryA trial-balance journal record produced by the accounting engine that summarises position movements for ledger proofing and downstream integration.
Value at RiskThe maximum expected loss on a portfolio over a given horizon at a stated confidence level, summarising market risk in one number.
Value DateThe date on which the cash flows of a transaction actually exchange or become interest-bearing, distinct from the trade date when the deal was agreed.
VolatilityThe degree to which a rate or price fluctuates over time, usually expressed as an annualised standard deviation in percent.
Yield CurveA line plotting interest rates against maturities for a given currency, showing the term structure of funding costs.
