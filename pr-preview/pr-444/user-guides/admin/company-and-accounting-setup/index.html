Company and Accounting Setup Guide

Goal: Register 4ward4u as a trading entity with a complete accounting infrastructure so deals can be captured, confirmed, and accounted for.

Starting state: 4ward4u Organization Business Partner already created. No Company, no GL Accounts, no Nostro Accounts exist yet.

Total records to create: ~28 across 8 apps.

Estimated time: 45–60 minutes (UI entry).

InstrumentName

  1. Open the instrument in the Instrument Catalogue app
  2. Click “Activate for Company”
  3. Select 4ward4u Company
  4. Confirm

Step 1.9 — Activate the Company

App: Companies Action: Click “Activate Company”

Once all the above is configured, activate the company to allow deal capture.

  1. Open the 4ward4u Company record
  2. Click “Activate Company”
  3. Status changes from Onboarding to Live
