Settlement and Cashflow Guide

Goal: Process the settlement of a confirmed deal through payment instructions, bank confirmations, and cashflow matching.

Prerequisites: Phase 3 complete (Deal confirmed, cashflow items and journal entries generated).

Step 4.1 — View the Payment Instruction

App: Payment Instructions Action: View the auto-generated payment instruction

After deal confirmation, the system generates payment instructions for settlement.

FieldDescription
Payment ReferenceUnique payment identifier
DealLink to the confirmed deal
AmountPayment amount
CurrencyPayment currency
Value DateWhen payment is due
StatusPending
Payment ChannelSAMOS, SWIFT, etc.

Step 4.2 — Authorise the Payment

App: Payment Instructions Action: Click “Authorise Payment”

Before payment can be processed, it must be authorised.

  1. Open the Payment Instruction record
  2. Review the payment details
  3. Click “Authorise Payment”
  4. Sanctions screening runs automatically
  5. Status changes from Pending to Authorised

What happens:

  • Sanctions screening checks the counterparty
  • If screening passes, payment is authorised
  • If screening fails, payment is held for review

Step 4.3 — Process Bank Confirmation

App: Payment Instructions Action: Click “Process Bank Confirmation”

After the bank processes the payment, confirm receipt.

  1. Open the Payment Instruction record
  2. Click “Process Bank Confirmation”
  3. Enter the bank confirmation reference
  4. Status changes to Confirmed

Step 4.4 — View the Settlement

App: Settlements Action: View the settlement record

The settlement tracks the final exchange of funds.

FieldDescription
Settlement ReferenceUnique settlement identifier
DealLink to the confirmed deal
Payment InstructionLink to the payment
Settlement AmountAmount settled
Settlement CurrencyCurrency settled
Nostro AccountWhich bank account was used
Settlement DateWhen settlement occurred
StatusSettled

Step 4.5 — View Cashflow Matching

App: Settlements (Confirmation Matches section) Action: View the matching between projected and actual cashflows

The system matches projected cashflows against actual bank confirmations.

FieldDescription
Projected CashflowExpected cashflow from the deal
Actual CashflowWhat the bank confirmed
Projected AmountExpected amount
Actual AmountActual amount received/paid
DifferenceVariance between projected and actual
Match StatusMatched, Unmatched, Tolerance
Match MethodExact, Tolerance, Manual

Step 4.6 — View Cash Account Balances

App: Cash Account Balances Action: View the nostro account balance after settlement

Track the running balance on each nostro account.

FieldDescription
Nostro AccountBank account
CurrencyAccount currency
PeriodCurrent period (YYYY-MM)
Opening BalanceBalance at start of period
Closing BalanceCurrent balance
Total InflowsMoney received
Total OutflowsMoney paid
Reconciliation StatusReconciled, Unreconciled

Step 4.7 — Settle the Deal

App: Deals Action: Click “Settle Deal”

Once settlement is confirmed, mark the deal as settled.

  1. Open the Deal record
  2. Click “Settle Deal”
  3. Status changes from CONFIRMED to SETTLED
  4. Position closes automatically

After Phase 4: The deal is fully settled. Cashflows are matched, journals are posted, and the position is closed.
