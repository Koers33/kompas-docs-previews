Data Model Diagrams

Entity-relationship diagrams of the Kompas data model, rendered from the Mermaid sources in docs/erds/. One page per diagram. The current diagram reflects what exists today; the planned and phase diagrams show the target model as it is being built out.

Current state

DiagramDescription
Full Entity Relationship DiagramThe complete data model as built today — business partners, deals, accounting, cash, positions, and all supporting master data in one view.

Planned domain diagrams

Per-domain target models, one page per domain:

DiagramDescription
AccountingFinancial nodes, general ledger accounts, mappings, and journal entries.
Capital AdequacyRegulatory capital measurement structures.
Cash ManagementProjected and actual cashflows, matching, balances, and nostro accounts.
CollateralCollateral arrangements and coverage.
Counterparty & ClientCounterparty and client records, KYC/AML status, mandates, settlement instructions.
Data QualityData-quality rules and monitoring records.
Deal ExecutionThe deal aggregate root with legs, instrument-specific details, lifecycle history, and amendments.
Evidence & ReplayEvidence packs and replay/forensic verification structures.
Execution EffectsEffects triggered by deal execution events.
FoundationCompanies, users, roles, authorities, and segregation-of-duties configuration.
Hedge AccountingHedge relationships and effectiveness structures.
Integration & EventsOutbound integration records and event payloads.
Knowledge GraphGraph nodes and edges linking platform records.
Legal ContractsLegal contract documentation linked to counterparties and deals.
Liquidity & ALMAsset-liability management structures for liquidity measurement.
Market DataRates, curves, symbols, and market reference data.
Model GovernanceGovernance of models and governed parameters.
Notifications & TasksNotification, task, and escalation records.
OnboardingCompany and client onboarding gates and readiness.
Payments & SettlementSettlement obligations, instructions, netting, and repair records.
Period CloseAccounting period control, gates, and close checklists.
Platform ResilienceResilience and recovery support structures.
Product & InstrumentProduct families, the global instrument catalogue, and company/client activation layers.
Profitability & CostCost provisions, fee lines, and profitability attribution.
Regulatory ComplianceConduct, sanctions, and regulatory compliance records.
Regulatory ReportingRegulatory returns and reporting records.
Risk & LimitsLimit pools, utilisation, exposures, and breach records.
Scenario SimulationScenario definitions and simulation runs.
SchedulerScheduled job configuration and run records.
TaxTax setups and tax treatment records.
ValuationValuation runs, marks, and adjustments.
Workflow & ApprovalsApproval routes, steps, and decision records.
Workspaces & UIWorkspace layouts and UI configuration entities.

Target model grouped by delivery phase:

DiagramDescription
Phase 0 — Foundation LayerFoundation entities for the first delivery phase (base configuration, partners, products).
Phase 1 — Core Transactional (Deal Execution)Core transactional entities: deals, legs, lifecycle, and rollover.
Phase 2 — Risk, Capital & LiquidityRisk limits, capital adequacy, and liquidity measurement entities.
Phase 3 — Market Data, Valuation, Scenario Simulation, Profitability & Cost, TaxMarket data pipelines, valuation, scenarios, profitability, and tax entities.
Phase 4 — Governance & ComplianceGovernance, compliance, and regulatory reporting entities.
Phase 5 — Platform IntegrationIntegration and platform-level support entities.
