# Conduct Flags app

Conduct Flags is the review list for conduct concerns raised against a deal or an exit request. Each flag records a **Flag Reason** — for example an undisclosed pre-hedging, a deal confirmed without a valid client authorization, an undisclosed exit fee, or an exit request processed too slowly — the deal or exit request it relates to, a description, and a status that moves from **OPEN** to **REVIEWED** to **ESCALATED** as someone works it.

## What is this app for?

The app exists to work through flags, not to raise them. A flag is created while deal or exit-request work is processed, always pointing at either a deal or an exit request, and it arrives here as **OPEN**. What you do in this app is review the flag, record that it has been reviewed, and escalate the ones that need further attention — leaving a trail of who looked at it, when, and why it was escalated.

## When should I use this app?

Use it when you are clearing the queue of open conduct concerns, when you need to check the review history of a flag, or when a flag on a deal or exit request needs to go further. The underlying deal itself is reviewed in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals); this app is about the flag raised against it.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). At the service level, reading flags and running both **Mark Reviewed** and **Escalate** are granted to the **Treasury** role; these are service permissions, and what you can actually do still depends on the roles assigned to you. Everyone who can open the app sees the same read-only flag records — nothing is created, edited or deleted in this app itself.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. A flag appears with **Status** **OPEN** — the list colours it red so unreviewed flags stand out.
2. Open the flag, read the **Flag Reason**, the description and the linked deal or exit request, then select **Mark Reviewed**. That moves it to **REVIEWED** (green) and stamps **Reviewed By** with your user and **Reviewed At** with the time.
3. If the flag needs to go further, select **Escalate** on a reviewed flag. Escalation asks for an **Escalation Note**, which is required, and moves the flag to **ESCALATED** (orange) with the note stored on the record.
4. Both actions are one-way steps: there is no step back from **REVIEWED** or **ESCALATED** in this app.

Treat this as an orientation to the flow and follow your team's conduct review procedure before escalating.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Status:** this is what gates the work. **Mark Reviewed** is offered only while a flag is **OPEN**, and **Escalate** only while it is **REVIEWED**; on any other status the button you do not need is hidden.
- **The reason on the flag:** the reason comes from a fixed list and stays with the flag — it is what tells you what kind of concern you are reviewing.
- **What the flag points at:** a flag relates to a deal or to an exit request, and the list shows both links, so you can open the record behind it.
- **Access:** reading and both actions remain limited to the **Treasury** role at the service level.

## Where can I review the results?

The flag record itself carries the outcome: **Status**, **Reviewed By**, **Reviewed At** and **Escalation Note** in the review part of the record, and the reason, description and creation stamp in the details part. The list can be filtered by **Status**, **Flag Reason** and **Reviewed By**, which is the quickest way to see what is still open and who has already looked at it.

Follow the link on the flag to the deal or exit request to see the record the concern was raised against — the flag does not repeat that record's content, and it does not change it.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- An action is refused: the flag is not in the state that action needs — **Mark Reviewed** applies to **OPEN** flags, **Escalate** to **REVIEWED** ones. The message names the state that is expected.
- You are told the flag cannot be found: refresh the list and open it again.
- You cannot see the action or the record at all: check your access in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
- The registered messages for these actions are listed in the [block 4xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-4xx-dls).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; reviewing, escalating and checking the outcome are covered on this page.
