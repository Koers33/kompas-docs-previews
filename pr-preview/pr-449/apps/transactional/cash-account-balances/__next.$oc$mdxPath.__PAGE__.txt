# Cash Account Balances app

Cash Account Balances is a read-only view of the balance of each bank (nostro) account for a given value period. Each row answers one question: for this company, account, currency and period, what did the book open at, what did it close at, what has actually settled, and does the balance agree with the external statement?

## What is this app for?

A record covers one company, **Nostro Account**, **Currency** and **Value Period**. It carries the **Opening Balance**, **Closing Balance** and **Confirmed Settled Balance**, the period's total inflows and outflows, and a **Reconciliation Status** alongside the last time the account was settled, moved and reconciled.

The **Reconciliation Status** values carry plain-language descriptions: **Matched** (the book balance matches the external statement), **Pending** (reconciliation has not yet run), **Break** (a discrepancy was detected), **Book SAMOS Variance** (the book balance differs from the SAMOS statement), **Book CBS Variance** (the book balance differs from the core banking system statement) and **Full Variance** (the book differs from both).

## When should I use this app?

Use it to monitor account balances, to see whether reconciliation has run for a period, and to spot accounts that still show a break or variance before a review. It is also the quickest way to check the confirmed settled balance for an account without opening the underlying cash records.

Balances are not entered or corrected here — the app offers no create, edit or delete, for any user. Fixing a difference means working in the cash and settlement processes that produced it; see the [settlement and cashflow guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). Every field on the record is read-only, so no role can turn this app into an editing screen.

## How does work progress in this app?

There is no lifecycle to work through. The list opens sorted with the newest **Value Period** first, you filter to the account you care about, open the record and read it. The header shows the **Confirmed Settled Balance** and **Reconciliation Status** for that record. Any follow-up — investigating a break, chasing a confirmation, rerunning reconciliation — happens outside this app.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Filters:** company, nostro account, currency, value period and reconciliation status decide which rows appear.
- **Reconciliation status:** a status of **Pending** means no reconciliation has run for that period yet; a variance or break means one has run and found a difference.
- **Available actions:** there are none. The app is read-only for everyone, so an empty action toolbar is expected rather than a permissions problem.

## Where can I review the results?

Open a record and check the **General Information** section for the company, account, currency, period, reconciliation status and the last settled, last movement and last reconciled timestamps, then the **Balances** section for the opening, closing and confirmed settled balances with the period's inflows and outflows. Those timestamps are the quickest way to tell whether an account has moved recently or is still waiting for reconciliation.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- The list is empty: clear the filters, or check whether a balance exists for that period at all.
- The status is **Break**, a **Variance** or **Pending**: that is a reconciliation outcome, not a fault in this app. Follow your cash and reconciliation procedure; the registered cash and payment messages are listed in the [block 9xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-9xx-csh).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; reading a balance, filtering to a period and interpreting the reconciliation status are covered on this page.
