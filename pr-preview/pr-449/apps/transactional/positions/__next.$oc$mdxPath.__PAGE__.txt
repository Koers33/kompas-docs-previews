# Positions app

Positions is where the platform's positions are tracked, and where a change to a position — a partial close, a full close, a rollover, an early termination or another agreed reason — is proposed by one person and authorised by a second. Each row shows the **Contractual Amount**, **Status**, **Currency**, **Open Date**, **Contracted Rate** and **Effective Rate**.

## What is this app for?

A position carries the economics of a contract: its contractual amount and currency, the date it opened, the contracted rate, and the accumulator values for revaluation, accrued interest and other movements. Its **Status** moves through **Open**, **Proposed**, **Authorised**, **Rejected** and **Closed**, and the record groups what you can read into **General Information**, **Accumulator Fields** and **Effective Rate Information**.

The **Effective Rate** is worked out each time the record is read rather than stored, so it always reflects the position as it stands now.

## When should I use this app?

Come here to see what a contract has left behind — the amount still open, what has accrued against it, and the rate it is earning — and when a change to that position has to be agreed by two people.

Capturing and confirming the deals themselves happens in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals); this app is about the positions those contracts produce.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, so it is treated as available to anyone with portal access.

The service behind the screen is more specific: the **Treasury** role works with positions and lifecycle records, while the **Client** role reads them. Proposing, authorising and rejecting a lifecycle action each require the **Treasury** role, so a client user can watch positions but never change them.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Open a position whose status is **Open** and run **Propose Lifecycle Action**. Choose the action type — **Partial Close**, **Full Close**, **Rollover**, **Early Termination** or **Other** — enter the closing amount and add remarks. The position moves to **Proposed**.
2. A second Treasury person opens the same position and runs **Authorise Lifecycle Action**, choosing the proposal, or **Reject Lifecycle Action** and gives a reason.
3. Authorisation closes the position. Where an amount is left over, the platform opens a continuation position holding the remainder and carries the accumulator values across with it, and the lifecycle action records who authorised it and when; the position shows **Authorised**. A rejection records the reason and the position shows **Rejected**.

The status on the record, and in the list, is the check that the decision landed.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Status gates each action.** **Propose Lifecycle Action** is offered on an **Open** position; **Authorise Lifecycle Action** and **Reject Lifecycle Action** on a **Proposed** one. A position in any other state offers nothing to run.
- **Two people, minimum.** The person who proposed a lifecycle action cannot authorise it — the platform refuses it as a segregation-of-duties violation.
- **The economics are fixed.** The contractual amount and currency cannot be edited, so a change in exposure is made by proposing a lifecycle action rather than by typing over the amount; only the accumulator fields can be updated directly.
- **A closing amount is capped.** It cannot exceed the position's contractual amount.
- **The rate label follows the instrument.** For instruments measured at amortised cost the figure is labelled **Effective Yield**; for everything else, **Effective Rate**.
- **Client users see the same records read-only**, with no actions available to them.

## Where can I review the results?

Read the status on the record and in the list: a position that was closed shows **Closed**, and where an amount continued, a new **Open** row appears carrying the remaining contractual amount with its own **Open Date**. **Accumulator Fields** shows what has accumulated against the position, and **Effective Rate Information** shows the rate worked out for it.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **The action buttons are disabled:** the status does not match the action. The registered messages — already proposed, already authorised, already closed, or not proposed — are listed in the [block 17xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-17xx-pos).
- **Authorisation is refused as a segregation-of-duties violation:** a different Treasury person has to authorise the proposal you made.
- **The effective rate is withheld with a message:** part of the position's ancestor chain is incomplete or has a zero amount, so the figure is shown as unavailable rather than guessed — see the [block 17xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-17xx-pos).
- **A permission message:** your role does not cover the action — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; proposing, authorising and rejecting a lifecycle action are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.

## What should I do next?
