# Companies app

Companies is the register of the legal entities the platform operates for. A company is the anchor record: partners, accounts, deals, limits, gates and accounting all hang off it, so what you record here is read by almost every other app.

## What is this app for?

A company record holds the entity's identity (codes, legal and trading names, registration numbers), its regulatory details (institution type, jurisdiction, standing), its financial identity (base currency, fiscal year), its operating model (risk structure, accounting basis, complexity, environment, onboarding state), settlement details (bank identifier, SAMOS member ID, timezone) and its lifecycle fields. Below the main record sit the entity's supporting records: regulatory contacts, compliance programmes, regulatory framework behaviours and data confirmations, risk appetite statements and mandatory positions, and the operations and governance rows (pipeline domains, artificial intelligence policies, external dependencies and internal model approvals).

## When should I use this app?

Use it to register a new legal entity, to correct or complete an entity's details, and to maintain the regulatory, risk and governance rows attached to one company. Day-to-day work — capturing a deal, posting an entry, maintaining a partner — belongs in the app for that task; this is where the entity those tasks refer to is defined. For a worked example, see the [company and accounting setup guide](/user-guides/admin/company-and-accounting-setup).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The company records themselves carry no additional role grant, and companies cannot be deleted; the supporting rows attached to a company can be added and corrected but not deleted.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Select **Create**. A new draft arrives pre-filled with defaults — status **Onboarding**, base currency ZAR, operating model **Treasury Only**, environment **Production**, a South African home jurisdiction and an April-to-March fiscal year — plus placeholder values for the code, name and registration fields. Review every default and replace the placeholders before you rely on the record.
2. Choose the **Business Partner** the company represents. This field is mandatory and cannot be changed once saved, and only organization partners that are not already linked to a company are offered — each organization backs at most one company.
3. Complete the identity, regulatory, financial identity, operating model and settlement details, then save. The company cannot be saved without its core identity fields; the platform names the missing field in the message.
4. Add the supporting rows — regulatory records, risk appetite statements, governance rows — on the saved record.
5. Keep **Status** and **Onboarding Status** aligned with where the entity actually is. **Status** starts at **Onboarding** for a new record and is what the rest of the platform reads when deciding whether that company may capture deals.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Status:** **Onboarding** means the company exists but is not capturing deals, **Live** means full deal capture and processing, **Suspended** means deal capture is blocked, and **Deregistered** means the entity is closed with its records retained.
- **Onboarding Status:** **Not Started**, **In Progress** and **Completed** describe where the onboarding wizard is.
- **Environment:** **Production**, **UAT** and **Development** are offered; the environment also decides how feature gates and bypasses behave for that company (see the Company Feature Enablements app).
- **Business partner:** fixed after the first save, so make sure it is right before you save.
- **New versus saved record:** the related-record tables appear once the record exists; a brand-new draft shows only the main fields.

## Where can I review the results?

Everything sits on the company record. Check the **Identity**, **Regulatory**, **Financial Identity**, **Operating Model**, **Settlement & Integration**, **Lifecycle** and **Audit** sections for the core data, then the **Regulatory**, **Risk Appetite and Positions** and **Operations and Governance** groups for the supporting rows. The **Audit** section carries the creation and change stamps. Downstream, the same record is what other apps resolve when they ask which company, base currency or environment applies.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- The record will not save: the message names the missing field — complete it and save again. The registered messages are listed in the [block 1xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-1xx-glb).
- The **Business Partner** value help comes up empty: only organization partners that are not already linked to a company are offered.
- A delete command is refused: company records and their supporting rows are retained by design.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet. The [company and accounting setup guide](/user-guides/admin/company-and-accounting-setup) walks through the company record step by step, field by field.
