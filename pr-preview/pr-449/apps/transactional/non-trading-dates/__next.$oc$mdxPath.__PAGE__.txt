# Non Trading Dates app

Non Trading Dates is the platform's trading calendar. Each row is a day that is not available for trading, identified by its **Holiday Name** and, where it applies, the **Country** and **Currency** it belongs to.

## What is this app for?

Entries come in two kinds, and the kind decides which dates the record carries:

- **Fixed** entries repeat every year. They store a **Month** and a **Day**, such as a public holiday that always falls on the same date.
- Entries that are not fixed are one-off. They store a full date, such as Good Friday in a particular year.

Business-day counting on the platform skips weekends and the holidays recorded here, so a calendar that is missing a holiday produces dates the business would not expect.

## When should I use this app?

Add an entry when a new public holiday or market closure has to be recognised, when a one-off closure for a coming year is announced, or when a holiday was recorded wrongly. Come here to check the calendar before you rely on a date that has to land on a business day.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The calendar records no additional role restriction and no narrower launchpad audience, so everyone who can open it sees the same dates.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter by **Holiday Name**, **Country** or **Currency**, and switch between the **Fixed Dates** view for recurring holidays and the **Non Fixed Dates** view for dated ones.
2. Select **Create** and enter the **Holiday Name**. Add a **Country** and **Currency** when the holiday applies to one market; some entries carry both, others are left open.
3. Set the kind: turn **Fixed** on and enter the **Month** and **Day** for a holiday that repeats, or leave it off and enter the date for a one-off holiday.
4. Save. The record then shows only the fields its kind needs — **Month** and **Day** for a fixed entry, the date for a one-off entry.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Fixed** cannot be changed once the record is created, so decide the kind when you create the entry.
- The **Country** and **Currency** value helps offer configured countries and currencies only, so an entry you expect may not be selectable until it is configured.
- Dates cannot be deleted from this app: an entry is corrected by editing it rather than by removing it.
- The list views group the entries by kind, so an entry can appear in one view and not the other.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows **Holiday Name**, **Currency**, **Country**, **Month**, **Day** and **Fixed** for every entry, and the two views split recurring from dated holidays. Opening the record shows **General Information** with exactly the fields its kind uses. The same calendar also appears as a read-only view inside the [Master Data Catalogue app](/apps/transactional/master-data-catalogue).

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- The wrong kind of entry: **Fixed** is fixed at creation, and entries cannot be deleted here, so check the record carefully before saving a new one.
- A value help offers no choice: only configured countries and currencies are available.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; adding a recurring holiday, adding a dated one and checking the calendar are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.

## What should I do next?
