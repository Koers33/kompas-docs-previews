# Nostro Accounts app

Nostro Accounts is where each company's own bank accounts are registered: the account's **Nostro Code** and **Nostro Name**, its **Currency**, the **GL Account** it posts to, and the **Status** that says whether it may be used.

## What is this app for?

A nostro account is the account a company holds at a bank, and it is the account settlements land on. The record has two sections. **Nostro Details** holds the account itself — company, code, name, currency, general ledger account, status, **HQLA Level**, **Settlement System**, **Effective From** and the **Active** flag. **Correspondent Details** holds the banking side — **Correspondent Bank**, **SWIFT BIC**, **Account Number** and whether **MT940 API Available** applies.

The status list drives what the account can be used for:

- **Active** — the account is available to the projected cash flow engine for settling new deals.
- **Suspended** — the account is temporarily suspended and is not used for new deals.
- **Closed** — the account has been permanently closed and is no longer available for settlement activity.

## When should I use this app?

Use it while a company's set-up is being built out, when a new currency account is opened, when a correspondent, SWIFT BIC or account number changes, or when an account has to be paused and later brought back. It is also the place to check which accounts a company has and which of them are available before a deal is confirmed in a currency.

The step-by-step company build, in the order the platform expects it, is in the [company and accounting setup guide](/user-guides/admin/company-and-accounting-setup).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The accounts carry no additional role restriction and no narrower launchpad audience, so everyone who can open the app works with the same accounts.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter by **Currency**, **Nostro Status** or **HQLA Level**, then open the account you need.
2. Select **Create** to add an account in draft, or open an existing one to correct it.
3. Complete **Nostro Details** first — company, code, name, currency, general ledger account, settlement system and status are required before the record can be saved — then fill in **Correspondent Details**.
4. Save. Corrections later follow the same steps.
5. Pause or resume an account with **Suspend** or **Reactivate**. The status and its indicator update as soon as the action completes.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Suspend** is offered while the account is not suspended, and **Reactivate** while the account is active, so only the action that fits the current status appears.
- The **Status** indicator is green for **Active**, orange for **Suspended** and red for any other status, and it appears both in the list and in the record header.
- Suspending stops the account being used for new deals; reactivating makes it available for them again. Neither action changes the account's banking details.
- Accounts cannot be deleted from this app. A retired account is recorded with its status rather than removed.
- Companies, general ledger accounts, correspondent banks, currencies, settlement systems and HQLA levels are chosen from configured records, so an option you expect must exist before it can be selected.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows **Company**, **Nostro Code**, **Nostro Name**, **Currency**, **GL Account**, **SWIFT BIC**, **Status** and **HQLA Level** for every account, with the status indicator as the quick check. On the record, **Nostro Details** and **Correspondent Details** hold the full picture. Downstream, the [Settlement and Cashflow Guide](/user-guides/back-office/settlement-and-cashflow) shows where the account is used: settlement records name the nostro account they paid through, and the [Cash Account Balances app](/apps/transactional/cash-account-balances) tracks each account's running balance. Not every account will have settlements or balances yet — that depends on how much activity the company has done.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- A deal confirmation reports that no nostro account is configured for a currency: create an active account for that currency here first, then retry in the [Deals app](/apps/transactional/deals).
- A delete command is refused: accounts are kept, not removed. Change the status instead.
- A value help offers no choice: only configured companies, accounts and banks are available, so the entry you expect may not exist yet.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; adding an account, pausing one and checking availability are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
