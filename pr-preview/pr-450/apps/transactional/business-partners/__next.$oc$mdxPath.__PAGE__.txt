# Business Partners app

Business Partners is the register of every organisation, person and group the platform works with. One record holds who the partner is, how to reach and pay them, how they relate to other partners, and — for clients — the trading, compliance and settlement setup that the rest of the platform depends on.

## What is this app for?

Every partner record starts with a **Business Partner Category**: **Organization**, **Natural Person** or **Group**. Around that core the record collects the details that other apps read: addresses, emails, phone numbers, bank accounts, documents and notes, plus relationships to other partners through the **Children** and **Parents** sections. Organizations additionally carry counterparty details such as legal name, identifier, jurisdiction and **Counterparty Status**; when the partner is flagged as a client, the record also holds **Client & Trading Setup**, **Compliance** (KYC and AML screening results), **Mandates**, **SSIs**, **Licences**, **Ratings**, and — for persons — **Qualifications**.

## When should I use this app?

Use Business Partners when you need to create a partner, correct a partner's details, record a relationship between two partners, or maintain the client and compliance information attached to one. Maintaining shared reference data rather than a partner — a company, an instrument, a status list — belongs in the relevant master-data app instead. For onboarding a new client, follow the [client onboarding guide](/user-guides/admin/client-onboarding); the [treasury guide](/user-guides/treasury/business-partners-for-treasury) covers day-to-day partner maintenance.

## Who can access this app?

Access is decided per role and per record. Treasury users can create, read and update any business partner and work in every section. Client users can create partner records, but cannot edit their own profile — name and identity fields are managed by Treasury — and they can maintain another partner only where they hold an active **Client Admin** relationship with it, or read it where they hold an active **Partner Manager** relationship. A client user without a partner record linked to their account is told to contact their system administrator before proceeding. These are service-level permissions; what you can reach still depends on the roles assigned to you (see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access)), and sensitive counterparty status changes remain limited to compliance roles.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Select **Create** and choose the **Business Partner Category**. The category is required and cannot be changed later, so choose it deliberately.
2. Enter the identity details for that category. The platform refuses duplicates: an organization whose name already exists, or a person with the same first name, last name and birth date, is rejected with a message pointing at the existing entry.
3. Save. Defaults are filled in for you — an address's validity starts today, and a bank account's account holder takes the partner's name — and the related-entry sections appear once the record exists: **Addresses**, **Emails**/**Company Emails**, **Phone Numbers**/**Company Phone Numbers**, **Bank Accounts**, **Children**, **Parents**, **Documents** and **Notes**, alongside the sections your category and client flag make relevant.
4. From there, maintenance continues in those sections: add contact and bank details, record relationships, attach documents, and keep client and compliance information current.

When a client user creates a partner, the platform links that user to the new record as its **Client Admin** and notifies the configured recipients that a partner has been submitted and is pending review. For partners flagged as clients, the platform also sets up default project master data the first time that record is read.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Category:** an **Organization** shows **Business Partner Information**, **Counterparty Details**, **Licences** and **Ratings**; a **Natural Person** shows **Natural Person** details and **Qualifications**; a **Group** shows the group fields including the company-link indicator.
- **Client flag:** **Compliance**, **Mandates** and **SSIs** appear only once the partner is marked as a client, and **Client & Trading Setup** appears for client organizations.
- **Who is signed in:** client users have the client-trading and margin/cost settings hidden, and cannot edit their own profile.
- **New versus saved record:** a brand-new draft shows the core fields; the related-entry sections appear after the record is saved.
- **Qualification expiry:** a qualification is flagged **Valid**, **Expiring Soon** (within 30 days) or **Expired** so renewals stand out.
- **List views:** the list can be viewed for all partners or filtered to **Organizations**, **Persons** or **Group**, which changes the columns shown.

## Where can I review the results?

Everything about a partner sits on its own record: the **General** fields, the category-specific sections, the relationship sections, and the **Documents** area for uploaded evidence. After a change, reopen the record and confirm the section you edited; relationship changes show on both partners involved. Client partners also carry their **Compliance**, **Mandates** and **SSIs** records on the same page, and their default project master data is visible in the projects master-data app.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- A duplicate message: the partner already exists — open the existing record instead of creating another. The registered messages are listed in the [business partner error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-2xx-bp).
- A client user is told no business partner record is linked to the account: contact your system administrator to have the record set up.
- An edit control is missing: check whether you are signed in as a client user, and whether the record is your own.

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating a partner, recording relationships and maintaining client setup are covered on this page.

## What should I do next?
