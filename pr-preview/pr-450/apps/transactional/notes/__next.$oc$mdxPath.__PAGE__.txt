# Notes app

Notes is the platform's annotation register: one row per note, with the **Classification**, the **Note Detail** text, the **Level**, the **Note Date** and who recorded it. It gathers notes from the areas that raise them instead of making you open each record in turn.

## What is this app for?

A note is the comment someone attaches to a record while work is in progress. Each note is filed against the area it belongs to — a project, a project phase, a project item, a payment request or a business partner — and each record groups its notes under that **Level**, with a **Level Description** next to it.

Notes are read here, not written: the service exposes them read-only in this app, so the note is created and changed in the app where the work happens. The Projects and Payment Requests apps both maintain notes of their own.

## When should I use this app?

Use it to review what has been written across areas — before a handover, when you need the history of a project or a payment request, or when you want to see every note dated in a period. Use **All Notes** for the whole register and the narrower views when you only care about one area.

Adding or amending a note belongs in the app that owns the record the note is attached to, not here.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The app's own metadata records no narrower launchpad audience, but record access is split: holders of the Treasury role see every note, while users with the Client role see only notes whose client record is linked to their own user record. Two people can therefore open this app and see different rows.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Filter by **Note Date** to narrow the period you are interested in.
2. Choose the view you need: **All Notes** for everything, or **Projects**, **Project Items** and **Payment Requests** for one area.
3. Open a note to read it. **General Information** shows the classification, the note detail, the level and its description, the date and who recorded it.
4. There is nothing to approve or release: a note is finished when the person who raised it saved it in its own app, and it appears here afterwards.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Level** is offered from the platform's note types — Project, Project Phase, Project Items, Payment Requests and Business Partner — so a note carries one of those values.
- The view you pick decides which rows appear, so a note can be visible in one view and not in another.
- Record access differs by role: Treasury sees the whole register, Client sees only the notes linked to their own record.
- Notes are read-only here, so no create, edit or delete command is offered in this app.

## Where can I review the results?

The list shows **Classification**, **Note Detail**, **Level**, **Note Date** and **Created By** in one place, with the wider views sorted newest note first. Opening a note gives the full **General Information** detail. The source record is where the note was written, so the note itself is the outcome to check here and the record it belongs to is the outcome to check in the area's own app.

## What should I check if I can't continue?

- No create or edit control appears: notes are read-only in this app. Add or change the note in the app that owns the record it belongs to.
- A note you expected is missing: check whether it belongs to another area and switch views, then check your record access in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; filtering the register, switching views and reading a note are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
