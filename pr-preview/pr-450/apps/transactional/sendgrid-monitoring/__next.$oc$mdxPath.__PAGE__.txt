# SendGrid Monitoring app

SendGrid Monitoring is where you see what the platform's outgoing emails actually did. It is a read-only copy of the email account: nothing here is typed in, everything is refreshed from the email provider.

## What is this app for?

The app answers two practical questions — did the email reach the recipient, and if not, why not. The **Sent Emails** view lists each message with its sender, recipient, subject, **Status**, open and click counts, the provider's **Last Event Time** and the row's **Synced At** stamp.

The other views carry the supporting evidence: **Sending Overview** for daily delivery and suppression statistics, **Failed Deliveries**, **Blocked Addresses**, **Spam Complaints** and **Opt-Outs** for addresses the provider stopped delivering to, **Email Templates** for the templates the platform sends from, and **Data Sync History** for the state of the synchronisation itself.

## When should I use this app?

Use it when someone reports that a platform email never arrived, when you need to confirm that a notification was opened, when you are investigating repeated delivery failures for an address, or when you simply want to check that the email integration is healthy.

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, and every view is read-only: there is nothing to maintain or approve in this app.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Choose the view for the question you are asking — most work starts in **Sent Emails**, filtered by **Status** or by sender and recipient.
2. Open a message. **General Information** carries the addresses, subject, status and timestamps; **Engagement** carries the open and click counts; **References** carries the message and template identifiers.
3. Select **Sync All** when you want the latest data now rather than waiting for the scheduled refresh. It refreshes every view in the app, not just the one you are on.
4. Read the outcome: a **Status** of delivered, opened or clicked means the message went through, while a bounce, block or spam complaint explains a message that did not.

The scheduled refresh runs at the cadence set in the [Schedule Configurations app](/apps/transactional/schedule-configurations).

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Statuses are colour-coded:** green for delivered, opened or clicked, orange for deferred, and red for bounced, blocked or spam.
- **Opens Count** is green once a message has been opened and orange while it has not.
- **The data is a copy.** **Synced At** on each row tells you when it was refreshed, and **Last Event Time** is the provider's own event time — a row can be older than you expect if a sync has not run.
- **Only the last ten runs are kept per data set.** Data Sync History shows the ten most recent runs for each data set, so an older failure scrolls out of view.
- Nothing is editable: the records arrive from the provider and are replaced wholesale at each sync.

## Where can I review the results?

**Sent Emails** gives the per-message answer, with **Engagement** showing whether it was opened or clicked. **Sending Overview** shows the day-level picture — requests, deliveries, opens, clicks, bounces, spam reports and opt-outs. **Data Sync History** is the health check: one row per data set per run, with **Records Synced**, start and finish times and any **Error Message**.

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **Nothing new after Sync All:** open **Data Sync History** and look for a row that carries an **Error Message** — it names the data set that failed. Ask your administrator to check the email integration before retrying.
- **A view is empty:** the sync has not brought anything in yet. Check **Synced At** and **Data Sync History** before concluding that nothing was sent.
- **A field or control is missing:** this app is read-only by design, and your own roles decide what you can reach — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; checking a delivery, reading engagement and diagnosing a sync are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how filtering and views work across the platform.

## What should I do next?
