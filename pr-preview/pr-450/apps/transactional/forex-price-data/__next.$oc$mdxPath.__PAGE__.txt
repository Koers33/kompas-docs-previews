# Forex Price Data app

Forex Price Data holds recorded price observations for a market's traded symbol: the **Market** and **Market Symbols** the row belongs to, **Dates**, **Time** and **Value Period**, the **Bid Value** and **Offer Value**, plus the **Difference Amount** and **Difference Percentage** the platform works out from them.

## What is this app for?

Each row is one observation of a quote for a chosen market symbol on a chosen date and period. The two difference columns are calculated rather than typed: whenever the record is read, the platform compares the bid and offer you entered and shows the gap as an amount and as a percentage of the bid. Use the list to review how those quotes sit across dates and periods.

## When should I use this app?

Come here to record a new quote observation, to correct a recorded value, or to review a symbol's bid and offer over time — filtered to a market, a symbol or a period. Defining the pair belongs in the [Forex Symbols app](/apps/transactional/forex-symbols), defining the market belongs in the [Forex Markets app](/apps/transactional/forex-markets), and refreshed reference rates are read in the [Forex Rates app](/apps/transactional/forex-service).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). The price records carry an additional service-level grant to the **Treasury** role, so working with them needs that grant as well; the app's own metadata records no narrower launchpad audience.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Select **Create** and complete the draft: pick the **Market** and the **Market Symbols** row, then set **Dates**, **Time** and **Value Period**.
2. Enter the **Bid Value** and **Offer Value** for that observation.
3. Save. The **Difference Amount** and **Difference Percentage** fill in for you — they are derived columns, so there is nothing to type there.
4. Correcting an observation later is the same flow: open it, edit the values, save. Records are kept rather than deleted, so fix a wrong row instead of removing it.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **Difference columns stay empty** until both **Bid Value** and **Offer Value** are present — the calculation needs the two values to compare.
- **Value Period** comes from a fixed list of periods (Spot, 30 Days, 60 Days, 90 Days), so a row always states which period its quote belongs to.
- **Read-only derived values:** the difference amount and percentage belong to the platform, not to you — they recalculate whenever the row is read, so they never carry a stale value forward.
- **No deletes:** observations are retained by design; correct the record instead.

## Where can I review the results?

The list itself is the quickest check: it shows the market, symbol, date and time next to the bid, offer and both difference columns for every row. Open a record and use **General** to see the full observation. The pair behind a row is maintained in the [Forex Symbols app](/apps/transactional/forex-symbols) and the market in the [Forex Markets app](/apps/transactional/forex-markets).

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- The difference columns are blank: enter both a bid and an offer value — the calculation runs once both are there.
- A value you expect is not offered in a value help: **Market**, **Market Symbols** and **Value Period** only offer existing entries, so create the missing entry in its own app first.
- An edit control is missing: check your roles in [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; recording an observation, reading the difference columns and correcting a row are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
