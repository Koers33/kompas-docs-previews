# Instrument Catalogue app

Instrument Catalogue is where the platform's shared instruments are described and where each company earns the right to use one. A catalogue row holds the instrument's code, name, product family, instrument type, IFRS 9 classification and regulatory asset class, and the object page carries the eligibility records and company activations behind it.

## What is this app for?

The list answers two questions at a glance: what an instrument is, and whether it is ready to be used. **Lifecycle Completeness** reports whether the instrument is activated for at least one company, **Accounting Completeness** reports whether accounting mappings exist for its instrument type, and **Active for Selection** records whether the instrument is offered for selection. Both completeness signals are worked out when you read the list, so they follow current configuration rather than a stored value.

The object page groups the same record into its general, classification and configuration fields, then shows the **Company Activation** and **Eligibility Record** sections plus a **Documents** section for supporting files.

## When should I use this app?

Use it before a company trades an instrument: the instrument must be activated for that company first, and the platform refuses a deal on an instrument that is not active with a message pointing back to this app.

Also use it to check an instrument's classification, to see which companies have it activated, or to work through the eligibility and evidence steps for a company. Configuration that governs how an instrument behaves for a company lives in the [Company Instrument Effects app](/apps/transactional/company-instrument-effects), and its accounts are mapped in the [Financial Node Account Mappings app](/apps/transactional/financial-node-account-mappings).

## Who can access this app?

The app is reached through portal access plus the roles your administrator has assigned you — see [Roles and Access](/getting-started/roles-and-access). No narrower launchpad audience is recorded for it, so it is treated as available to anyone with portal access.

The service behind the screen is stricter than the launchpad: reading the catalogue and creating an eligibility record go with portal access, while changing catalogue rows and running the evidence, confirmation, activation, suspension, revocation and reinstatement actions require the **Treasury** role. An action you are not entitled to is refused with a permission message rather than silently ignored.

## How does work progress in this app?

1. Open the instrument from the list and read its completeness signals.
2. Run **Create Eligibility Record** and pick the company. The platform applies the eligibility rule for the instrument's family, the company's regulatory category and jurisdiction, and lists the evidence the rule requires.
3. Run **Upload Evidence** for each pending evidence item and attach the document.
4. Run **Validate Evidence Chain** to see how many required items are complete and which are still pending.
5. Run **Confirm Eligibility** and enter a compliance statement. Confirmation is refused until every required item is complete.
6. Run **Activate Instrument** for the company. The activation becomes active, a new entry is added to the eligibility history, and a follow-up task is raised to complete the general-ledger mapping.
7. Later, **Suspend Instrument** or **Revoke Instrument** (each requires a reason) take the activation out of use, and **Reinstate Instrument** brings a suspended instrument back.

Each of these steps appends a sealed entry to the instrument's eligibility history rather than overwriting the previous one.

## What can change what I see or do?

- **The eligibility rule decides the path:** an instrument the rule prohibits for that company cannot be taken further, and a family with no matching rule cannot start — the message tells you which happened.
- **Only one live eligibility record per instrument and company:** if one already exists, the platform points you at it instead of creating a second.
- **Evidence gates confirmation:** confirmation is blocked while required items are outstanding, and the message shows how many are pending.
- **Separation of duties:** the person who uploaded the evidence cannot confirm it; the message that appears asks for a different Compliance Officer to confirm.
- **Confirmation gates activation:** activation is refused until eligibility is confirmed, and an instrument already active for the company is refused as a duplicate.
- **Revocation is the long road back:** a revoked instrument needs a completely new evidence chain before it can be reinstated, while a suspended one returns directly.
- **Suspension and revocation both need a written reason**, so the buttons stay unusable until you supply one.

## Where can I review the results?

Open the instrument and read the **Eligibility Record** section: company, instrument, sequence number, status, who uploaded, confirmed and activated it, the compliance statement, any suspension or revocation reason, and the record's integrity hashes. The **Company Activation** section shows whether the scope is active, when it was activated, and any suspension or revocation dates. Both are also visible in the list through the completeness columns.

Attached evidence sits in the **Documents** section of the same object page. Once an instrument is activated, deals for that company can use it; the general-ledger mapping work it triggers is tracked in the [Financial Node Account Mappings app](/apps/transactional/financial-node-account-mappings).

## What should I check if I can’t continue?

- **"Evidence chain is incomplete"** — finish the pending items and confirm again.
- **Same person confirming what they uploaded** — hand the confirmation to a different Compliance Officer.
- **"No eligibility rule found"** — the family and regulatory category combination is not configured; contact your administrator.
- **"Prohibited for the selected company"** — the rule blocks it; the message carries the stated basis.
- **"Already active"** — the company already has the instrument; nothing further is needed.
- The registered messages for this app are listed in the [block 8xx error reference](/reference/error-codes/block-8xx-icn).

## Which task guides are available?

No task guide is published for this app yet; creating an eligibility record, completing evidence, activating and suspending are covered on this page. Read [Using the Fiori Apps](/getting-started/using-fiori-apps) once for how drafts, value helps and save behaviour work across the platform.
