# Client Onboarding Guide

> **Last updated: 2026-08-19**
>
> **This guide is subject to change and is provided for feedback purposes.** The sections, fields, and behaviours described here are based on the current system implementation and may be updated as the platform evolves. Please share any feedback or corrections with the Development team.

> **This guide is written for users logged in with the Kompas Client role.** As a Client, you can view your own Business Partner profile but you **cannot edit it** — your name, email, and other identity fields are managed by Treasury. You can, however, maintain other Business Partners (such as your employer Organization) if you hold the relevant child-parent relationships. See [Maintaining Other Business Partners](#maintaining-other-business-partners) below.

## What Happens When Treasury Claims Your Account

When a Treasury user claims your account from the IAS User Management app, the system automatically creates a **Business Partner** record for you. This is your personal profile in Kompas — a "Natural Person" record linked to your IAS identity.

Once created, you can find and manage your profile in the **Business Partners** app.

---

## How to Find Your Profile

1. Log in to SAP Build Work Zone.
2. Open the **Business Partners** app from the launchpad.
3. In the List Report, switch to the **Persons** table view (filters to Natural Person category only).
4. Click on the arrow for your entry to open your Object Page.

---

## What You See on Your Object Page

Your profile is a Natural Person (category code `02`). The Object Page shows the following sections:

### General (always visible)

| Field                     | What It Shows                                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business Partner Category | "Natural Person" — set automatically, cannot be changed |

### Person (visible for Natural Person only)

| Field                     | What It Shows                                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| First Name                | Your first name from IAS                               |
| Middle Name               | Your middle name (if any)                              |
| Last Name                 | Your last name from IAS                                |
| Birth Date                | Your date of birth (empty by default — you can add it) |
| Business Partner Category | "Natural Person" (shown again for reference)           |

### Addresses

A table of your physical addresses. You can add, edit, or remove addresses.

### Emails

A table of your email addresses. Your IAS email is pre-populated when Treasury claims your account. You can add additional emails.

### Phone Numbers

A table of your phone numbers. Empty by default — add your contact numbers here.

### Children / Parents

Relationship tables linking you to other Business Partners. Empty by default.

A **child-parent relationship** between two Business Partners determines what the child BP can see and do for the parent BP. The parent can be an Organization, a Person, or a Group — though Organizations are the most common case. The table below lists every relationship type that grants actual platform access. All relationships must be **active** (`isActive = true`) to take effect.

| Relationship Type    | Code    | What the Child BP Can Do                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Services Affected                            |
| -------------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Client Admin**     | `19`    | **UPDATE + READ** the parent's Business Partner profile; **Create, Read, Update** on Documents; **READ** Qualifications                                                                                                                        | Business Partners, Documents, Qualifications |
| **Partner Manager**  | `27`    | **UPDATE + READ** the parent's Business Partner profile; **Create, Read, Update** on Documents (excluding FEC trades); **READ** the Org section                                                                                                | Business Partners, Organizations, Documents  |
| **Project Manager**  | `20`    | **Create, Read, Update** on Projects, Project Phases, Project Items, and Project Master Data — plus actions like `createRequest`, `duplicateProjectItem`, `createPaymentRequest`; **READ** Business Partners and Clients within those projects | Projects, Project Items, Project Masters     |
| **Project Viewer**   | `21`    | **READ** on Projects, Project Phases, Project Items, and Project Master Data; **READ** Business Partners within those projects                                                                                                                 | Projects, Project Items, Project Masters     |
| **Request Author**   | `22`    | **Create, Read, Update** on Client Requests and Payment Requests — plus actions like `duplicateRequest` and `sendRequest`; **READ** related Projects, Business Partners, and Clients                                                           | Requests, Payment Requests                   |
| **Request Viewer**   | `23`    | **READ** on Client Requests, Payment Requests, and Project Filters                                                                                                                                                                             | Requests, Payment Requests                   |
| **Response Viewer**  | `24`    | **Create, Read, Update** on Internal Responses                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Internal Responses                           |
| **Authoriser**       | `05`    | **Create, Read, Update** on Internal Contracts — plus `acceptContract` and `rejectContract` actions; **READ** related Business Partners and Projects                                                                                           | Internal Contracts                           |
| **Deals Manager**    | `29`    | **READ + CREATE + UPDATE** Deals; **READ** Deal-related Business Partners and the counterparty list                                                                                                                                            | Deals                                        |
| **Deals Viewer**     | `30`    | **READ** Deals                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Deals                                        |
| **On Behalf Of**     | `28`    | **READ** Deals where acting on behalf of the parent; **READ** Deal-related Business Partners                                                                                                                                                   | Deals                                        |
| **Any active child** | _(any)_ | **READ** Deals where the parent is the counterparty                                                                                                                                                                                            | Deals                                        |

**How it works in practice:** If your Person Business Partner has an active child-parent relationship with code `20` (Project Manager) to an Organization, you can create, read, and update all Projects belonging to that Organization. Without that relationship, you cannot see those Projects at all. Note: deletion is not available in Kompas — even though the authorization layer grants broad access, the service layer prevents delete operations.

**Multiple relationships:** You can hold more than one relationship to the same parent. For example, you could be both a **Project Manager** (`20`) and a **Request Author** (`22`) — giving you full access to both Projects and Requests for that parent.

### Documents

File attachments linked to your profile (e.g., ID documents, proof of address).

### Qualifications (visible for Natural Person only)

A table of your professional qualifications, certifications, or licences. Empty by default — add your credentials here.

### Notes

General notes attached to your profile.

---

## Sections NOT Visible on Your Profile

The following sections are only visible for Organization-type Business Partners or for users with the Client trading role enabled. They will not appear on your Natural Person profile:

- **Bank Accounts** — hidden for Natural Person category
- **Company Emails** — hidden for Natural Person category
- **Company Phone Numbers** — hidden for Natural Person category
- **Licences** — hidden for Natural Person category
- **Ratings** — hidden for Natural Person category
- **Compliance (KYC / AML)** — hidden unless you have the Client trading role
- **Mandates** — hidden unless you have the Client trading role
- **Standard Settlement Instructions** — hidden unless you have the Client trading role
- **Client Trading Setup** — hidden for Natural Person category

---

## What You Can Do on Your Own Profile

As a Client, you can only **view** your own profile. You cannot edit any fields or add new records (addresses, phone numbers, qualifications, documents, notes) — all changes to your Business Partner must be requested through Treasury.

| Action                | How                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **View your profile** | Open the Business Partners app, switch to Persons view, click your name |

| Column                        | Meaning                           |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **First Name / Last Name**    | Your name as registered in IAS    |
| **Business Partner Category** | "Natural Person" for your profile |

## What Happens Next

After Treasury onboards you as a Client (assigns the "Kompas Client" group in IAS), you gain access to the Kompas platform with client-level permissions. Your Business Partner profile remains in the system and can be referenced when creating deals, FEC trades, or other transactions on your behalf.

If your access is later revoked via the IAS User Management app, your Business Partner record is **not deleted** — it remains in the database. You simply lose platform access until Treasury re-onboards you.

## Test Cases

### Test Case 1: Creating an Organization Business Partner (4ward4u)

**Precondition:** You are logged in as a Kompas Client. You already have a Person Business Partner record (created by Treasury when they claimed your account).

**Steps:**

1. Open the **Business Partners** app.
2. Click **Create** to start a new Business Partner draft.
3. Fill in the following fields:

| Field                         | Value                      | Notes                                         |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Business Partner Category** | `Organization` (code `01`) | Select from the dropdown                      |
| **Organization Name**         | `4ward4u`                  | The legal or trading name of the organization |

4. Optionally, add supplementary details:

| Field          | Value               | Notes                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal Name** | `4ward4u (Pty) Ltd` | Full registered name, if different from organization name |
| **Short Code** | `FW4U`              | 4-character uppercase alphanumeric code                   |

5. Click **Save** (or **Activate** if draft-enabled).

**What happens automatically:**

When you save the new Organization, the system automatically creates a **child-parent relationship** between your Person Business Partner and the new 4ward4u Organization:

| Field                 | Value                                          |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Parent**            | 4ward4u Organization (the BP you just created) |
| **Child**             | Your Person Business Partner (your user ID)    |
| **Relationship Type** | `19` — Client Admin                            |
| **Area**              | `SF` (System Functions)                        |
| **Active**            | `true`                                         |

This means you are now the **Client Admin** of 4ward4u. Per the authorization rules, this gives you:

- **UPDATE + READ** on the 4ward4u Business Partner profile
- **Create, Read, Update** on Documents linked to 4ward4u
- **READ** on Qualifications of 4ward4u and its people

**Expected result:** The 4ward4u Organization appears in the Business Partners List Report. In your Person profile's **Children / Parents** section, a new row shows the relationship to 4ward4u with type "Client Admin".

**To verify:** Open your Person Business Partner, scroll to the Children / Parents section, and confirm the 4ward4u relationship row is present and active.

**What you can do next:** Now that you hold the Client Admin relationship to 4ward4u, open the 4ward4u Organization's Object Page. You can add entries to the following tables:

| Table              | What to Add                                                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Addresses**      | Physical addresses for the organization                       |
| **Emails**         | Contact email addresses                                       |
| **Phone Numbers**  | Contact phone numbers                                         |
| **Bank Accounts**  | Bank account details for settlements                          |
| **Documents**      | Attachments such as registration certificates, IDs, or proofs |
| **Notes**          | General notes about the organization                          |
| **Qualifications** | Professional qualifications or certifications                 |

You can also edit the Organization Name and Legal Name fields directly on the General section.

### Test Case 2: Creating a Counterparty Organization (GlobalFX Bank)

**Precondition:** You have completed Test Case 1 and are the Client Admin of the 4ward4u Organization.

**Purpose:** Create a counterparty Business Partner that can be selected when creating deals, FEC trades, or other transactions where a counterparty is required. The counterparty must be linked to 4ward4u via a relationship to appear in the counterparty dropdown.

**Steps:**

1. Open the **Business Partners** app.
2. Click **Create** to start a new Business Partner draft.
3. Fill in the following fields:

| Field                         | Value                      | Notes                    |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Business Partner Category** | `Organization` (code `01`) | Select from the dropdown |
| **Organization Name**         | `GlobalFX Bank`            | The counterparty's name  |

4. Optionally, add supplementary details:

| Field          | Value               | Notes                                   |
| -------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **Legal Name** | `GlobalFX Bank Ltd` | Full registered name                    |
| **Short Code** | `GFXB`              | 4-character uppercase alphanumeric code |

5. Click **Save** (or **Activate** if draft-enabled).

**What happens automatically:**

When you save, the system automatically creates a **Client Admin** relationship (same as Test Case 1):

| Field                 | Value                                       |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Parent**            | GlobalFX Bank (the BP you just created)     |
| **Child**             | Your Person Business Partner (your user ID) |
| **Relationship Type** | `19` — Client Admin                         |
| **Area**              | `SF` (System Functions)                     |
| **Active**            | `true`                                      |

**Linking the counterparty to 4ward4u:**

For GlobalFX Bank to appear as a selectable counterparty when creating deals or trades for 4ward4u, a **counterparty relationship** must exist between the two Organizations. This relationship links 4ward4u (as parent) to GlobalFX Bank (as child) with one of the following relationship types:

| Relationship Type | Code | When to Use                                                       |
| ----------------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bank**          | `06` | The counterparty is a banking institution                         |
| **FSP Trader**    | `09` | The counterparty is a financial service provider acting as trader |
| **Supplier**      | `11` | The counterparty is a goods or services supplier                  |
| **Customer**      | `26` | The counterparty is a customer of 4ward4u                         |

To create this relationship:

1. Open the **4ward4u** Organization's Object Page.
2. Scroll to the **Children** section.
3. Click **Create** to add a new relationship.
4. Fill in:

| Field                 | Value                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Child**             | `GlobalFX Bank` (select from the dropdown) |
| **Relationship Type** | `26` — Customer (or the appropriate type)  |
| **Area**              | `TD` — Trading                             |

The **Active** field defaults to `true`.

5. Click **Save**.

**Expected result:** GlobalFX Bank now appears in the **Counterparty Relationships** list when creating deals or trades for 4ward4u. The counterparty dropdown in Deals, and other transactional services will show GlobalFX Bank as a selectable option.
